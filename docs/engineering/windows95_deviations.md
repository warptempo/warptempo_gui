# Where the chrome deviates from Windows 95

The GUI's chrome is Windows 95 measured pixel by pixel (the unit, the DrawEdge families, the roster case, the fonts' cells; `CLAUDE.md`'s rounding and colour rules, `src/gui/render.h`'s palette block). This list records the FEW MAJOR DESIGN DECISIONS where it deliberately departs from Windows, one line each, with the reason and the owner (architect 2026-10-03: "start a list of things where we deviate from Windows"). The obvious (antialiased proportional faces, the dim and dark levels, Display-P3 bytes) is not a deviation worth a line; the rule stands at its owner and is not restated here.

- **The trim lane is a scroll bar whose thumb is the kept region** (2026-10-02/03): Windows' thumb shows the viewport and its arrow buttons stand at the bar's ends; here the thumb spans the kept region between the trim bounds, and the two 16-px arrow buttons ride the thumb's own ends as the bounds' handles (the left arrow is the begin, the right the end). Owner: the trim lane block at `kTrimLaneHeightPx` and `render_trim_flags` (render.h / render.cpp).
- **The flags are not Windows at all** (2026-10-03): the marker flags are the Acid flag — the program's own flat box with its own recorded label, a one-px outline in the theme's DkShadow, selection that outline in white — standing on the well's border with one Windows px of air above for the ruler's minor ticks. Owner: render.h's marker-lane paragraph, the marker-lane block at `kMarkerLaneAirPx` (render.h).
- **Cards, not message boxes** (the Windows look 2026-10-02): what Windows would say in a modal message box the app says on a notification card — the cards stacking down from the top of the window, each on Windows' tooltip face, timed and closed by a press — so a message never stops the work. Owner: `src/gui/notifications.h`'s head, `paint_notifications` (paint_handler.cpp).
- **A red frame on invalid input** (2026-10-03): Windows answers a refused entry with a message box or a balloon; here a refused Enter turns the editor's frame red (the flag editor's one black line, the dialog field's both sunken lines) and selects the whole text, the first keystroke replacing it and the frame returning — no glyph. Owner: `text_editor::refuse` (text_editor.h), `render_flag_editor_box` (render.cpp), the dialog field in `paint_modal_dialog` (paint_handler.cpp).
- **Row 8 is one status panel and a line of words, not a status bar of parts** (2026-10-03): the clock alone sits in the sunken panel at the monospace face; the state / history line is painted on the bare chrome at the normal face, the Vegas / ACID pattern (words on the chrome, a time in a sunken cell). Owner: `paint_bottom_row_buttons_and_clock` (paint_handler.cpp).
- **Chrome buttons act at the lift, hotkeys at the press** (2026-08-13): Windows' buttons act at the release too, but its menus and toolbars differ in places; here the one timing doctrine covers every roster button, with the anchors' toggle, dismissals and the keyboard at the press. Owner: the doctrine block at `GuiInputHandler::on_key` (input_handler.h), `AppState::ChromePress`.
- **No hover faces anywhere** (2026-10-02, the frozen design): Windows 95 itself had none on push buttons but its later toolbars did; the roster, the dialogs and the lists paint no hover state. Owner: render.h's palette head.
