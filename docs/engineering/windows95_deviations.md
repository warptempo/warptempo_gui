# Where the chrome deviates from Windows 95

The GUI's chrome is Windows 95 measured pixel by pixel (the unit, the DrawEdge families, the roster case, the fonts' cells; `CLAUDE.md`'s rounding and colour rules, `src/gui/render.h`'s palette block). This list records the FEW MAJOR DESIGN DECISIONS where it deliberately departs from Windows, one line each, with the reason and the owner (architect 2026-10-03: "start a list of things where we deviate from Windows"). The obvious (antialiased proportional faces, a dark palette, Display-P3 bytes) is not a deviation worth a line; the rule stands at its owner and is not restated here.

- **The trim lane is a scroll bar whose thumb is the kept region** (2026-10-02/03): Windows' thumb shows the viewport and its arrow buttons stand at the bar's ends; here the thumb spans the kept region between the trim bounds, and the two 16-px arrow buttons ride the thumb's own ends as the bounds' handles (the left arrow is the begin, the right the end). Owner: the trim lane block at `kTrimLaneHeightPx` and `render_trim_flags` (render.h / render.cpp).
- **The flags are not Windows at all** (2026-10-03): the marker flags are the Sonic Foundry programs' flags — their own black label in every state, outside the chrome's luminance text rule, standing on the well's border with one Windows px of air above for the ruler's minor ticks. Owner: `kMarkerFlagLabel`, the marker-lane block at `kMarkerLaneAirPx` (render.h).
- **Row 8 is one status panel and a line of words, not a status bar of parts** (2026-10-03): the clock alone sits in the sunken panel at the monospace face; the state / history line is painted on the bare chrome at the normal face, the Vegas / ACID pattern (words on the chrome, a time in a sunken cell). Owner: `paint_bottom_row_buttons_and_clock` (paint_handler.cpp).
- **Chrome buttons act at the lift, hotkeys at the press** (2026-08-13): Windows' buttons act at the release too, but its menus and toolbars differ in places; here the one timing doctrine covers every roster button, with the anchors' toggle, dismissals and the keyboard at the press. Owner: the doctrine block at `GuiInputHandler::on_key` (input_handler.h), `AppState::ChromePress`.
- **No hover faces anywhere** (2026-10-02, the frozen design): Windows 95 itself had none on push buttons but its later toolbars did; the roster, the dialogs and the lists paint no hover state. Owner: render.h's palette head.
