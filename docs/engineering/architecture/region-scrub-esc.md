# Region, Scrub, the Esc Bindings, x / Shift+X

Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md in the 2026-07-25 slim-down (project feature-complete). This file is now the authoritative home for this detail; CLAUDE.md carries only a digest and a pointer here. Keep it current with landed rulings.
Continues from marker-ui.md.

THE REGION SIMPLIFICATION (architect-chartered 2026-07-30, the kdenlive redesign's opening arc; landed the same day): the region highlight is TRIM-ONLY FREE SCRATCH — "a temporary selection of what could potentially become a trim region" — and nothing else. The selection no longer paints a span, the cursor playhead is never suppressed, and the whole two-forms model (the region as the playhead's SPAN form, the split half-triangles, exactly-one-form-visible, the cursor/stem suppressions, the never-span-less guarantee and its producer analysis) is RETIRED. RegionProvenance is deleted whole — `RegionState` is `{active, a_frame, b_frame}` and nothing branches on where a span came from. The trim-window highlight coupling is retired with it: no route publishes the trim window as a region any more (`sync_region_to_trim_window` and both wrappers are deleted), the trim window's permanent display being its chips, strip stems and bridge bar, which are ALWAYS painted now that trim is always-set (normalization-and-boundaries.md owns that model).

TWO GESTURES HAVE BEEN CALLED "THE SCRUB"; ONLY ONE KEEPS THE NAME (architect 2026-07-28, stated here because this file is the docs map's designated scrub owner). (1) THE AUDITION SCRUB — the waveform LOWER-HALF one-shot press, `scrub_press_at` / `scrub_act_at` / `scrub_launch_at`: stop a live session, else launch one at the clicked column. It owns the word, it is the only thing `src/` names "scrub", and since the Space region launch's retirement (2026-07-30) the lower-half press is `scrub_launch_at`'s ONLY caller class — and it is the REGION'S PREVIEW GESTURE by ruling ("user can click scrub region to preview"): it touches no selection, no region, no cursor and no follow state, so a scratch span rests through it and the user auditions the span by clicking inside it. (2) THE PLAYHEAD STEP — the horizontal ARROW keys, one painted column per press, TWO-LANE by the selection (see selection-model.md's lane model): with an EMPTY selection it is the waveform lane — stop playback, clear the focus, dissolve a resting region, move the playhead one column; with a NON-EMPTY selection it is the MARKER lane — the same press carries the FOCUSED marker (a position nudge in either column's home view; W+target is a consumed refusal), and the playhead rides along VISIBLY because every such route re-lands it on the committed focus. LANE EXIT IS ANY DESELECTING ROUTE (Home/End, a waveform click, a trim setter's deselect, an undo restore that empties the selection); a marker-lane press that refuses is a consumed no-op, never a waveform-lane step.

THE PLAIN WAVEFORM PRESS splits by HALF (the scrub area, architect-designed 2026-07-23). The UPPER half is the placement press: it CLEARS THE SELECTION, drops the playhead at the clicked column (`playhead_frame_at_click_column` through the live-domain clamp; marker-BLIND — no snap magnet), reseeks a live scanner, suppresses follow for the session, and arms the region drag (`arm_region_drag_at`), which dissolves any resting highlight AT MOUSE-DOWN — so the old span vanishes on press whether the gesture stays a click or becomes a fresh drag. Past the shared 8px press-becomes-drag gate the drag extends a REGION: a span of ACTIVE-DOMAIN playable frames (`RegionState`, session-only, never serialized, outside selection AND undo — a SPAN, never markers), painted as an OPAQUE GROUND RECOLOR (`kRegionCanvas`, before the plate blit, so the waveform ink composites over it untouched — the Ableton read) with the CURSOR PLAYHEAD PAINTING STRAIGHT ACROSS IT like any other ink (2026-07-30 — the region is not a playhead form and suppresses nothing). Endpoints are unordered (drag order); the painter and `x` normalize. Sub-threshold rested spans dissolve at release (the sliver rule). A SHIFT-exact waveform press FORMS a region in one act: it CLEARS THE SELECTION and spans from the playhead (nothing selected) or from the selected marker FURTHEST from the click (argmax |pos − click|, the longest side) to the clicked column, arms the drag at the far endpoint (`arm_region_drag_preserving` — a motionless release keeps the formed span), moves no playhead, stops no playback, seeds no double-click, read-only allowed, slivers refused. BOTH FORMERS DESELECT AT PRESS, AND THEY ARE THE ONLY FORMERS LEFT (the delete demotions and the trim-window sync died 2026-07-30), so A REGION RESTS ONLY BESIDE AN EMPTY SELECTION — structurally, verified with no counterexample: every selecting route clears a resting region before a selection can rest. That derivation is what deleted the phase-reset overlay's mirrored region gates as dead code (the band and `Selection::phase_overlay_subject` — a region and a focused overlay subject cannot coexist; recorded at the band, paint_handler.cpp). Esc mid-drag does nothing at all (pointer gestures have no cancel, 2026-07-29: release/button-lost/Ctrl+Q/resize/WM-close end a gesture through the release bodies and what stands stands; undo is the mitigation). Gutter presses arm nothing; region endpoints hold PLAYABLE live-domain frames only (every former clamps through clamp_playhead_to_live_domain). The coupling is DOWNWARD-ONLY (architect 2026-07-23; unchanged): SELECTION → REGION → TRIM, nothing upward — the plain-drag region SELECTS NOTHING, and trim never selects.

THE REGION'S WHOLE LIFECYCLE: formed by the two waveform formers above; PREVIEWED by the lower-half scrub press (which leaves it resting); CONSUMED by `x` (below — which clears it after the write, its job done); CLEARED BY EVERYTHING ELSE that moves the playhead or changes the selection — the route inventory lives at `clear_region_highlight`'s declaration (input_handler.h) and is unchanged in membership from the two-forms era: the navigation jumps (Tab family + `c` through the one jump tail, the waveform-lane playhead step, Home/End, the settings playhead key), every marker click (unconditional — the result-size split died with the extent), `enter_text_edit`, both position nudges' commit tails, both marker drops, the three membership-wholesale routes (undo/redo's visual tail, the `p` swap, the propagate paste), the settings engine-commit, and the in-place structural clears (file load/adopt, Ctrl+Tab, the S/T switch — active-domain endpoints die with the domain — and the kick validator's live-domain reclamp). THE DELIBERATE KEEPERS: the lower-half scrub press, Space (which no longer reads the region at all and touches nothing), and pure viewport moves (paging, zoom steps, pans, bare `0`). SPACE ALWAYS PLAYS FROM THE PLAYHEAD (architect 2026-07-30: "play issues from playhead OR scrub" — the region left-bound launch is deleted); auditions do not move the cursor, and there is NO LOOPING ANYWHERE (same ruling, normalization-and-boundaries.md).

WHAT PAINTS (2026-07-30, the one-form model): the cursor playhead (kPlayheadCursor 1px line + tip-down triangle) paints UNCONDITIONALLY at the resting cursor column — whatever the selection, whatever the region; a cursor on a marker simply passes under that marker's flag (the z-order flip). The scanner is unchanged. A singleton selection additionally paints its always-on stem (kSelectedStem), NEVER suppressed; a group's cue is its members' kWaveform ink triangles plus the visible cursor landed on the focus. The region paints its ground recolor and nothing else — `render_split_playhead` is deleted, and the degenerate one-column span simply paints no ground (zero width) while the cursor stands wherever it stands.

`x` IS SET-ONLY AND CONSUMES THE SPAN: a live region → set trim to it (active-domain lo/hi through `active_domain_to_source_frame` — one snap, monotone — then the shared walls and the `auto_clear_crossed_trim` commit tail, which under always-set trim resets a crossed pair to the full window), then DESELECT (x is a trim setter; every setter deselects, 2026-07-29) and CLEAR THE REGION (2026-07-30 — the span's whole job was aiming this gesture; the always-visible chips/bar/stems show the result); a DEGENERATE result — the inverse-mapped, wall-clamped pair coming out `end <= begin`, reachable for a narrow span over ~16x bracket-legal compression — REFUSES OUTRIGHT, writing nothing (ARCHITECT-CONFIRMED 2026-07-29; the refusal tests the pair x would write, so it is blind to where the span came from); NO region → silent no-op. `Shift+X` IS THE MAXIMIZER (2026-07-30, trim always-set): it expands the window to the full song behind an already-full identity guard (an already-maximized press stops nothing, repaints nothing, triggers nothing — the refusal-gated stop rule), touches no region and no selection (it is a trim CLEARER-class route, not a setter). Both are read-only-refused at the keyboard gate alone. The plain chip-row click that never becomes a drag is a CONSUMED NOTHING (2026-07-30 — its entire act was publishing the now-retired window highlight); the chip/bridge DRAG takes the setter's deselect and the trim-mutation stop at its FIRST ACCEPTED bound change (input_trim.cpp), and the ctrl / ctrl+shift bound-set clicks keep their in-handler stop and deselect. `x`/`Shift+X`, the chip/bridge drags, the bound-set clicks, and Home/End are trim's whole interface.

BARE ESC IS BOUND IN FOUR PLACES AND NOWHERE ELSE (architect 2026-07-29, contortion ruling 5 — this file is the docs map's Esc owner; membership re-derived 2026-07-30 at the authoritative src enumeration, input_handler.cpp). The four: (1) THE FOUR EDITORS — flag, BPM bracket, settings, render-commit — where Esc closes / cancels the edit through `route_modal_editor_key`; (2) THE PROMPTS, where Esc activates the rightmost response; (3) THE EDITOR TEXT-DRAG ESC HATCH — a bare-exact Escape ends an in-flight text-selection drag, a sub-part of the editor class (the drag-modal gate is NOT an Esc binding — it tests only Ctrl+Q, so mid-gesture Esc is swallowed with every other key by the gate's general drag-modality); (4) THE RENDER / BATCH CANCEL (`handle_escape_cancels` → `cancel_archival_session`), its own binding class, ARCHITECT-CONFIRMED 2026-07-29. Everywhere else bare Esc is a no-op: it never deselects, never clears a highlight, never moves the playhead — a resting span clears at the next playhead-moving point command, and the accepted cost is that no route clears a span WITHOUT also moving the playhead or changing the selection. The whole selection/region Esc ladder and the demote-as-a-concept died 2026-07-29 (the Esc ladder's history and the deleted rungs are in git and the 2026-07-29 commits; none of that machinery exists to re-propose). Modified Escape remains unbound everywhere, at every Escape reader.
