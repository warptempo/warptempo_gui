TWO GESTURES HAVE BEEN CALLED "THE SCRUB"; ONLY ONE KEEPS THE NAME (architect 2026-07-28, stated here because this file is the docs map's designated scrub owner). (1) THE AUDITION SCRUB — the waveform LOWER-HALF one-shot press, `scrub_press_at` / `scrub_act_at` / `scrub_launch_at`: stop a live session, else launch one at the clicked column. It owns the word, it is the only thing `src/` names "scrub", and this arc did not touch it. (2) THE PLAYHEAD STEP — the horizontal ARROW keys, one painted column per press. It is no longer called a scrub anywhere, in docs or in source; the read-only allowlist entry that used to be `is_scrub` is now `is_playhead_step`. Note that the DAW sense of scrub — audition audio by dragging the cursor — is not a gesture this product has at all, in either family. The playhead step is the one this arc changed: it is now TWO-LANE, and the lane is decided by the selection (see selection-model.md's lane model). With an EMPTY selection it is the waveform lane and behaves exactly as before — stop playback, clear the focus, dissolve a resting region, move the playhead one column. With a NON-EMPTY selection it is the MARKER lane: the same press carries the marker under it (a position nudge in either column's home view, or the W+target tempo-image step), and the playhead rides along because every one of those routes re-lands it on the committed focused marker. Esc is the explicit collapse back to the waveform lane; there is no fallback, so a marker-lane press that refuses (off home, an ineligible tempo-image step, a read-only tab) is a consumed no-op and never degrades into a waveform-lane step. HOME/END JOINED THE WAVEFORM-LANE CLEAR 2026-07-28: they are playhead jumps to the trim bounds, so like the arrows' waveform arm they drop the selection on the way — a route that LEAVES the marker lane leaves the playhead where it lands, for the cursor to paint again (the converse half of the marker-lane-owns-the-playhead rule; the rule itself is stated at `land_playhead_on_marker`, input_pointer.cpp). THE LAND IS A PURE PLAYHEAD WRITE AND EVERY POINT COMMAND OWNS ITS OWN COLLAPSE (architect 2026-07-29 — the two-forms ruling, superseding both the 2026-07-28 every-site dissolve and its same-day movement gate, whose composition with land-on-focus failed in live use): the playhead has a POINT form and a SPAN form (the region), exactly one is ever visible, and a command that asserts the point form collapses any active region at its OWN site, unconditionally — the collapse is never a land side effect and never motion-gated. The routes whose region story is instead the MEMBERSHIP CLEAR (a replace clears a SelectionExtent span outright — never demotes it to Free) are the non-moving membership ops: the flag/BPM editor opens carry the chokepoint clear at `enter_text_edit`, while the Ctrl+N collapse relies on the membership clear alone (a TrimWindow region resting through it, the value-gesture rule) — and the `p` swap and the propagate paste joined the undo/redo restore under the MAXIMALLY GREEDY COLLAPSE (architect 2026-07-29, later the same day): all three membership-wholesale routes clear ANY resting region, any provenance. The rule statement lives at `land_playhead_on_marker` (input_pointer.cpp); the point-command clear-site list at `clear_region_highlight` (input_handler.h).
