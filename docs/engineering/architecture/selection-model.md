SELECTION AND PLAYHEAD ARE RE-COUPLED (Ableton-parity arc step 1, architect-designed 2026-07-23). GENEALOGY: coupled originally → DECOUPLED 2026-07-20 (for the lead-in workflow) → RE-COUPLED 2026-07-23, legitimate because the audition scrub supplies the upstream-audition workflow the decoupling existed for. THE INVARIANT: clicking a marker ALWAYS lands the playhead on it — the PLAIN marker click (the FLAG BOX, the marker's ONE pointer surface — the lane run died with the marker-text lane at row 5, and the upper-half stem band died with the stems-inert ruling of 2026-08-12 below; both views/columns, read-only allowed) single-selects AND seats the playhead on the marker with NO viewport move (direct cursor write through the two-step placement basis `source_frame_to_active_domain` then `clamp_playhead_to_live_domain`; the sole difference from Tab and `c`, which both recenter — and `c` alone re-zooms to the working level, Tab having carried that landing for one commit on 2026-08-05 before the same-day revert) — and the playhead FOLLOWS the focused/grabbed marker through nudges and drags UNCONDITIONALLY: every focus route lands the playhead on the focus it sets (A LAND IS ALWAYS ON THE FOCUS since 2026-07-28), so the TOWED SET IS EMPTY and the follow is a re-land as the marker moves, never a repair. Every marker click is also a POINT command that HIDES THE TRIM REGION OVERLAY unconditionally at its own site (since 2026-07-30 with no result-size split — a click says "the playhead is HERE"; it was a CLEAR of a free scratch span until 2026-08-18 and is a hide that discards nothing since, the region BEING the trim now). THE SELECTION MODEL (arc step 2): Shift+click is an INCLUSIVE RANGE from the anchor (`app.shift_range_anchor`, session scratch seeded by adopting the focus; owned by the Selection mutators alone — no release owner since 2026-07-29, the accepted post-release delta recorded at the field's declaration, app_state.h), each successive shift-click REPLACING the selection with the anchor..clicked range, clicked = range end = FOCUS, landed there; Ctrl+click is the individual membership TOGGLE, landing on the focus it leaves. Neither arms drags, seeds double-clicks, or opens editors; the shared land is `land_playhead_on_marker` (input_pointer.cpp). **ADD TO SELECTION IS A STICKY CTRL FOR GLASS (architect 2026-08-18)** — bare `k` and a bottom-row button light a MODE (`AppState::add_to_selection`, whose declaration is the whole contract) in which a PLAIN flag click takes the ctrl branch whole: toggle membership, land on the focus the toggle leaves, keep the rest. His reason is the rig: "none of the modifier-click vocabulary exists on touch, so buttons are how glass gets selection work" — a finger has no ctrl to hold, so the one gesture that ACCUMULATES a selection was unreachable there. It is folded into the toggle term at `run_marker_click_act` rather than grown into a fourth arm — `ctrl || (add_to_selection && !shift)` — so the two spellings cannot come to mean different things, and **SHIFT BEATS IT**: that `&& !shift` is load-bearing, since the fork is `if (toggle) … else if (shift)` and a bare fold onto ctrl would let a lit mode swallow a held shift and turn a range select into a toggle ("Shift+click needs no rule here, because it has its own gesture"). A real ctrl+click is unaffected either way. IT IS SCOPED TO FLAG HITS BY CONSTRUCTION, not by a second gate: that owner is the mode's ONE site and only ever runs on a resolved flag hit, so the empty lane, the ruler and the waveform keep their whole vocabulary while it is lit. IT AUTO-CLEARS AT THE BOUNDARY THAT ENDS A PLAIN CTRL+CLICK'S EFFECT — the accumulated membership, which any selection REPLACE or CLEAR ends — implemented as THE SHIFT-RANGE ANCHOR'S OWN RULE WITH THE KEEPER SWAPPED, which is what makes the two mirror images: `toggle_selection_membership` (the mode's own act) keeps the mode and clears the anchor, `select_range_from_anchor` keeps the anchor and clears the mode, and every OTHER `Selection` mutator clears both; `repair_last_selected` is deliberately NOT one of them, being a focus repair reached from inside the toggle itself. Riding the `Selection` chokepoint means a mutator added later inherits the clear by default. ITS GATES ARE ITS OWN: the `h` view greys the button through the derived partition, while the READ-ONLY LOCK LEAVES IT LIT — a selection is navigation, not authored content, the reasoning that already keeps the trim gestures legal on a locked tab, so bare `k` sits on `read_only_key_blocked`'s allowlist explicitly. ALL MARKER CLICKS ARE FLAG-LANE-ONLY, IN EVERY VIEW: the 2026-08-06 SYMMETRY RULING put the MODIFIED clicks there (generalizing the 2026-08-01 stem gate — over the waveform ctrl is the zoom (the one nav drag's ctrl phase since 2026-08-14) and shift is the trim sweep, at the very pixels a stem stands on, so A WAVEFORM MODIFIER IS GESTURE VOCABULARY AND SELECTION IS LANE VOCABULARY; the `h` view's one-day stem-based range select and ctrl toggle were DELETED by it), and the SEVENTH GLASS RULING (architect 2026-08-12, STEMS POINTER-INERT IN ALL CONTEXTS) repealed the clause that survived it — "PLAIN clicks keep the stem hit, that being the one surface where an item and the waveform overlap with no modifier to spend" — so the PLAIN click is lane vocabulary too: no waveform press resolves a marker in any view, a drag can never accidentally grab one, and the stems are a painted cue alone (marker-ui.md carries the succession — the question stayed open through the touch arc, was answered "stems stay" while the scrollbar plan lived, and closed the other way once the waveform-height clamp put the flag lane in easy reach everywhere). THE `h` HISTORY VIEW TAKES THE SAME MODEL EXACTLY (github-recheck.md's press router): its lane clicks are the live model re-expressed over the diff-flag list, its waveform press the navigation surface's own at every column (the pending click / grab-pan since pan-primary, 2026-08-12). The two views therefore answer a waveform click identically — the standing live model IS the model, and the history view has no click vocabulary of its own to learn. THE PLACEMENT IS DEFERRED TO THE RELEASE on the navigation surface since the same ruling (the eighth): a plain press on the upper waveform half, the ruler or the empty marker lane arms a PENDING CLICK — nothing pops at press — whose MOTIONLESS release runs the placement (deselect, the trim region overlay's hide, playhead to the press column, reseek, follow override) while a crossed drag is the GRAB-PAN, a pure viewport move that clears nothing; the FLAG clicks are the deliberate carve-out and STAY PRESS-TIME — "select + seat at press, the immediate pending marker drag, so marker work keeps its immediacy while the surface around the flags pans". THAT CARVE-OUT WAS SUPERSEDED FOR ONE DAY AND THEN RESTORED: the act-at-lift sweep took it on 2026-08-15 ("all actions should be on mouse-up / finger-up" — the sweep was scoped to the ICONS, grew to the overview's teleport, and the flag had never been ruled an exception because it had never been in scope), and the architect's first real drive of that sweep inverted it on 2026-08-17 under the THIRD CLAUSE — CONTENT ACTS THE MOMENT ITS IDENTITY IS CERTAIN, and a flag press can only mean one thing (`conventions.md` carries the clause and its certainty audit; the immediacy argument above is what the drive found again from the other side, the double-click open being "a tad slow compared to using enter key"). So the flag press runs the whole click (stop, the modifier fork, the land, the overlay hide, the plain arm's double-click consume-open) on its OWN LIVE HIT, and the plain arm alone then arms `PendingMarkerPress` for the two later-edge owings — the drag a crossing may begin and the seed a motionless lift writes (`marker-ui.md` is authoritative). The EMPTY MARKER LANE's double-click CREATES a marker, SELECTS it and LANDS the playhead on it — exactly the bare-`s` drop, whose own body single-selects what it creates (`marker-ui.md`). MULTI-SELECT VISUALS (row-5 form since 2026-08-01): a selection's cue is its members' BRIGHTENED FLAGS — the class ladder's brighter fill/edge pair — with the ALWAYS-VISIBLE playhead cursor standing on the landed focus; SELECTION NO LONGER PAINTS A SPAN (2026-07-30, the region simplification: `set_region_to_selection_extent`, the SelectionExtent provenance, and the membership-replace region clear are all deleted). The pre-row-5 cues — the members' kWaveform ink triangles and the kSelectedStem singleton stem — are RETIRED FORMS: row 5 gave every enabled marker an always-on class-colored stem, so the selection cue rides the flag's brightened class pair alone (kdenlive-redesign.md's row-5 record is authoritative).

DRAG MECHANICS: waveform plain/shift/ctrl presses stay marker-blind; the follow moves the RESTING cursor only (the nudge stops playback first; the drag never does), all target mapping through the live memoized cache; the drag's commit lands the playhead against the POST-commit map and runs regardless of net change; the drag has NO CANCEL (rulings 3+4, 2026-07-29 — Esc mid-drag is a consumed no-op, any end COMMITS, undo is the way back). HORIZONTAL MOVEMENT IS A FOCUS ACT — GROUPS ARE NEVER MOVED (architect 2026-07-29, ruling 9): ONE MARKER MOVES PER GESTURE. The POINTER: a plain flag click on a member of a 2+ selection single-selects and lands — AT THE PRESS (press-time again since 2026-08-17, after the one-day lift model of 2026-08-15), and the collapse is what matters here rather than the timing (the 2026-07-23 WITHHELD-CLICK deferral machinery, which held the click back so `begin_drag` could seed the intact group, stays deleted and is a different thing entirely from either timing) — and the drag it may become is an ordinary singleton drag, its subject single-selected by the arming PRESS's own click act and the crossing running no act of its own. The KEYBOARD: the bare Left/Right marker-lane step with 2+ selected COLLAPSES to the focus in the twins' shared prologue (`position_nudge_prologue`) and steps that focus alone; a press that refuses at its wall KEEPS the collapse. The singleton drag survives bit-for-bit (viewport clamp widened to contain zero, release column snap through `authored_frame_at_column`, the [0, eof_wall] belt). THE GROUP-VERB DOCTRINE lives at the head of `position_nudge.h`: GROUP-CAPABLE where the members are INDEPENDENT or the selection's span is merely READ — Ctrl+D, the Up/Down cent step, Delete, `m`, Ctrl+P, the zoom framing, the undo/paste restores (bare `x` left this enumeration on 2026-08-18: it reads no span at all now, being the trim region overlay's show/hide toggle); FOCUS-COLLAPSE where the members are COUPLED or the act is POSITIONAL — Ctrl+N (the pass→owner freeze reads the resolved walk) and all horizontal movement. THE COLLAPSE FAMILY IS TWO SITES (2026-07-30, down from four): the nudges' shared prologue and Ctrl+N — `t` and `c` CARRY a group whole now (the region simplification undid their collapses: the spanless-group state they existed to avoid is simply the normal state under the always-visible cursor). The architect's general rule stands verbatim at the doctrine: if group is relatively cheap to implement, implement it; otherwise collapse to last selected — no hybrid third form. The nudge keeps its by-repeat-identity undo coalescing with SINGLETON identity hints.

undo/redo VISUAL LANGUAGE (architect 2026-07-25, simplified 2026-07-30): the touched set is SELECTED (a removal clears instead), identified by EXPLICIT IDENTITY HINTS for the reposition drags (`UndoEntry::touched_snapshot`/`touched_live`, always singleton-scoped since ruling 9; the diff-based row matcher stays the hint-less fallback), focus defaulting to the EARLIEST touched marker. The entry's A/B tab is switched in when foreign. The restore's VISUAL TAIL (undo.cpp, after sanitize, running for EVERY entry — its unconditional overlay hide included, and the settings-only 'S' arm clearing the selection beside it, ruling 6): a SINGLETON restore LANDS on the touched marker through land_playhead_on_marker (offscreen → plain recenter); a GROUP restore lands on the EARLIEST touched member — the group's visual is the members' brightened flags with the cursor on that focus (the extent-region write died 2026-07-30; the ink-triangle cue retired at row 5) — and when any touched member is offscreen PREFERS A PLAIN SCROLL at the current zoom, deriving the span it frames from the touched members' own positions (the land's per-member formula; the region is no longer the span source), zooming only when the group cannot fit (the shared framer; it only ever zooms out). THE STAY-PUT PAIR IS ROLLED BACK, DELETED WHOLE (architect 2026-08-07): `Ctrl+Alt+Z` / `Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Z` — one day live, 2026-08-06..07 — were undo and redo with the restore's VIEWPORT WRITES suppressed. THE REASON IS A CHORD COLLISION, not a design change: LABWC OWNS BOTH CHORDS ON THE TARGET DESKTOP, the compositor grabbing them before the client sees anything, so the GUI never received the presses at all; the architect had forgotten the collision when he asked for the feature. The chords are RELEASED to labwc, and the whole mechanism went with them — the dispatch arm's alt bit (back to `ctrl && !alt && key == Z`, so an alt-carrying shape is again an unbound no-op under strict modifier validation), the `keep_viewport` parameter through `do_undo` / `do_redo` / `restore_history_entry`, and its two readers' gates (the singleton's offscreen recenter and the group's scroll-or-frame block, both unconditional again). The plain pair's behavior is byte-for-byte what it was before the feature, the repeating-Ctrl-chord set is back to THREE (`marker-ui.md`), the read-only gate and the `h` history mode consume the family's TWO surviving shapes as they always did, and the toolbar Undo / Redo buttons — never touched by the feature — dispatch the plain chords still. DO NOT RE-PROPOSE THE FEATURE ON THESE CHORDS: it needs a shape labwc does not own, and the architect has not named one. KEPT playhead-onto-position writes that are drop CONSEQUENCES: bare `s` and the empty-lane double-click seat the playhead on the marker they create (P+target lands at `playhead − N/2`, the lead-in), and both drops are point commands hiding the trim region overlay at their chokepoints.

THE LANE MODEL (architect 2026-07-28; KEPT BY EXPLICIT RULING 2026-07-30 with the cursor visible — "keep arrows-nudge-the-focused-marker with the cursor visible"). The bare horizontal arrows always move the playhead, and the selection decides which LANE the press lives in: EMPTY = the waveform lane (a one-column playhead step); NON-EMPTY = the MARKER lane (the same step carrying the FOCUSED marker — a position nudge in either column's home view, NOTHING in W+target, a consumed refusal). The honest statement since the suppression retired: the arrows move the focused marker and the playhead rides along VISIBLY — both marker-lane routes re-land the playhead on the committed focus (`finish_position_nudge`), so cursor and focus stay coincident on screen. ONE PREDICATE OWNS THE SPLIT (`GuiInputHandler::playhead_in_marker_lane`, exactly a non-empty selection) with TWO readers — the `on_key` dispatch and `read_only_key_blocked`'s `is_playhead_step` entry, the sole read-only defense for both marker-lane routes (in the lane the same press AUTHORS). LANE EXIT IS ANY DESELECTING ROUTE; a refused marker-lane press is a consumed no-op, no fallback. THE MARKER LANE OWNS THE PLAYHEAD (architect 2026-07-28): any route that changes WHICH marker is focused while the lane is active LANDS the playhead on the new focus — otherwise the cursor would visibly rest away from the marker the arrows are about to move, and Space would play from that stale spot. The routes: every flag/BPM editor open and retarget (`enter_text_edit`, the one chokepoint), the Ctrl+N collapse, the two position nudges' prologue, the propagate paste landing on the FIRST created reset, and the `t` S/T toggle's selection-gated land — which since 2026-07-30 lands WITHOUT collapsing (the group carries; the land is the EXACT re-expression of a cursor already resting on the focus, closing the T→S inverse double-round that the generic translation would leave — architect-confirmed: the land fires only when a selection is non-empty, where the cursor already rests on the focus, and the empty-selection generic translation is untouched). The CONVERSE clause: a route that LEAVES the lane (Home/End, every deselect) leaves the playhead where it lands, for the cursor to keep painting — which it now always does. Value gestures take the rule in image form: both arms of the cent step re-land on the focused marker's post-step image in target view (the singleton for the label-definition repricing, the only map-change re-land left). THE PLAYHEAD HAS ONE FORM (2026-07-30, retiring the two-forms model; the row-5 form since 2026-08-01 is the white kPlayheadStem head + stem, the aliased head in the MARKER lane (it moved OUT of the ruler at the row-5 live test — the occlusion rationale and the record live at the head painter, paint_handler.cpp); the pre-row-5 line + triangle is a retired form), painted unconditionally at the resting cursor column — no suppression under a selection, no span form, no dissolve; the TRIM REGION OVERLAY is the trim painted a second time — derived from the resting bounds every frame — and the cursor simply crosses its ground recolor. THE OVERLAY HIDES WHEN THE PLAYHEAD'S POSITION IN THE MUSIC CHANGES, WHEN A MARKER IS TOUCHED AND WHEN THE SWEEP ENDS (architect 2026-08-19, the third clause 2026-08-20), which is two owners and two extra sites rather than a list of commands: `Viewport::move_playhead_to`, the marker land, `run_marker_click_act` and `commit_region_sweep` (the rule at `clear_region_highlight`, input_handler.h — a hide, never a clear: the trim persists and one `x` re-shows an identical overlay; bare Esc was a fourth class until 2026-08-21, when the manual hide became bare `x`'s alone). THE NON-HIDING KEEPERS, none of which needs an exception written down because none reaches an owner: EVERY TRIM WRITE as one class (the endcap/bridge drag on either surface, the sweep, the bound-set clicks, the settings `:trim_*=` commits, the crossed/coincident resets and `Shift+X` — the trim family writes the cursor DIRECT), the lower half's scrub click act, Space (which reads nothing), the view switches, and pure viewport moves. The Tab family and `c` land through the ONE owner since 2026-07-30 (`jump_playhead_to_focused_marker` calls `land_playhead_on_marker` — the hand-copied recipe unified; the jump keeps its own stop and recenter, and the land owns the hide since 2026-08-19). THE SELECTION IS NEVER PARKED (architect 2026-07-29, contortion ruling 1). THE SCOPE RULE: A COLUMN OR TAB SWITCH CLEARS THE SELECTION; THE AUDIO-VIEW SWITCH (`t`) CARRIES IT — and since 2026-07-30 `t` carries a GROUP whole (no collapse; the selection-gated land above). `p` and Ctrl+Tab call `clear_selection` outright (each clear placed before its flip so the erase damage resolves on the leaving pixels). COINCIDENCE AUTO-SELECT replaces the memory, at EXACTLY FOUR ENTRY CHOKEPOINTS — the source load, the `p` column entry, the Ctrl+Tab tab entry, and the `'` load-in-place (joined 2026-07-30) — each stating its own class and pointing at the one owner, `auto_select_marker_at_playhead` (input_pointer.cpp, homed beside the land whose formula it reuses). THE TEST IS THE LAND'S OWN FORMULA, EXACTLY: scan the active column's store in order and single-select the first marker whose `clamp_playhead_to_live_domain(source_frame_to_active_domain(time_frame))` equals `playhead_cursor_sample` — an EXACT int64 compare, "is the playhead STANDING ON a marker", first-in-store on ties; the adjacent land is a provable no-op (its early-return equality test IS the selection predicate). The payoff: the entry's stored cursor already described a marker under the cursor, and selecting it arms the lane the user would expect there — with the cursor visibly standing on the selected flag (the `t` land's exactness is what keeps this compare true across domain flips). WHAT THE USER LOSES AND GAINS: a group or singleton left in the other column/tab is GONE, re-acquired with one click; the stored cursor is honored VERBATIM, no switch silently arms an authoring mode on an unexpected marker, and the product holds ZERO raw store indices across commands. THE GENERALIZATION: VALUE-SHAPED SESSION STATE PARKS SAFELY; INDEX-SHAPED SESSION STATE DOES NOT — the per-tab band (viewport, zoom, playhead, trim, `read_only`, and since 2026-08-18 bare `0`'s `zoom_recall_level`) is frame-, value- or flag-shaped; the parked selections were the one exception and their liveness machinery is deleted. ONE PLAYHEAD PER TAB (`ViewState`): `playhead_cursor_sample` is the whole of it. The rule and rationale live at `ViewState` and `AppState::selected_markers` (app_state.h).
