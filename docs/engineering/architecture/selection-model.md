THE LANE MODEL (architect 2026-07-28) — the reason the horizontal arrows split on selection emptiness. With a selection the focus model stops painting the cursor playhead and the focused flag's kWaveform-filled ink triangle IS the playhead; with a group it is the extent region's recolored ground WHEN THE GROUP HAS ONE (a spanless 2+ selection draws no playhead form at all — reachable since contortion ruling 6, see the retirement paragraph below). So the arrows ALWAYS move the playhead, and the selection decides only which LANE it currently lives in: empty = the waveform lane (a one-column playhead step), non-empty = the MARKER lane (the same step, carrying the marker under it — a position nudge in either column's home view, and NOTHING AT ALL in W+target, where the arrows are a consumed refusal since the tempo-image step's deletion, contortion ruling 8). The model is not a fiction layered over two unrelated gestures: both marker-lane routes re-land the playhead on the committed focused marker (`finish_position_nudge`, the twins' shared tail), so when the selection is dropped the cursor genuinely reappears at the nudged position rather than where it was when the selection began. LANE EXIT IS ANY DESELECTING ROUTE (architect 2026-07-29, contortion ruling 5, superseding "Esc is the explicit collapse back to the waveform lane" — Esc is unbound outside the editors, the prompts, the drag swallow and the render cancel): Home/End, a waveform click, a trim setter's publish, an undo restore that empties the selection. This project still has no gesture fallbacks, so a marker-lane press that refuses stays a consumed no-op. ONE PREDICATE OWNS THE SPLIT (`GuiInputHandler::playhead_in_marker_lane`, currently exactly a non-empty selection) with TWO readers — the on_key dispatch that picks the route, and `read_only_key_blocked`'s `is_playhead_step` entry, which admits the bare horizontal arrows only in the waveform lane because in the marker lane the same press AUTHORS and that gate is the sole read-only defense for BOTH surviving routes (three until the tempo-image step's deletion, contortion ruling 8). THE MARKER LANE OWNS THE PLAYHEAD (architect 2026-07-28, completing the model and REVERSING the flag editor's "opening is a selection act, not a playhead move" rule): any route that changes WHICH marker is focused while the lane is active LANDS the playhead on the new focus, because otherwise a flag asserts it is the playhead while `playhead_cursor_sample` rests somewhere else entirely and Space plays from that stale spot. The routes brought into line: every flag-editor open and retarget including the BPM-editor open (`m`) — the shared `enter_text_edit` chokepoint, where the land now sits beside `set_single_selection` on the pointer clicks' adjacency convention — the `t` S/T toggle landing on a surviving focus — which since contortion ruling 6 may be a GROUP's focus, the toggle no longer collapsing anything (the land exists because the generic cursor re-express double-rounds off the focus's image by a frame, and it covers a surviving singleton and a surviving group identically), the Ctrl+N inherit-toggle collapse, the two POSITION NUDGES through their shared prologue's collapse+land (contortion ruling 9), and the propagate paste landing on the FIRST created reset (its focus moved from the last created to the first, matching undo/redo's earliest-touched rule; the paste SETS its created set's extent region since 2026-07-29, reversing its earlier no-region rule — a mass-marker event reads like undo/redo's group restore: wholesale clear, select, land on first, derive the extent). The CONVERSE clause is why Home/End CLEAR the selection rather than land: a route that LEAVES the lane leaves the playhead exactly where it lands, for the cursor to paint again. THE PLAYHEAD HAS TWO FORMS, POINT AND SPAN, AND EXACTLY ONE IS EVER VISIBLE (architect 2026-07-29, superseding the one-day-old MOVEMENT GATE after its composition with land-on-focus failed in live use — a shift-range-then-Enter collapse was a no-motion land, the gate skipped the dissolve, and the demoted extent span rested frozen under a marker the arrows then moved). The POINT form is the cursor (empty selection) or the singleton stem / focused flag; the SPAN form is the REGION — which is why an active region dissolves the cursor into the split half-triangles and now ALSO suppresses the singleton stem (paint_selected_stem's early return; the selection persists beneath, the focused flag keeping its ink triangle — only the point-form PAINT yields). The land is a PURE playhead write with no region side effect, and every playhead-moving POINT command owns its own region collapse at its own site, UNCONDITIONALLY — never gated on whether the playhead actually moved: the plain click (its four deferred completions died with the group drag, every marker press committing its whole click at PRESS time now), the multi-select clicks at a result under two, `enter_text_edit` (the one chokepoint of every editor open/retarget), BOTH position nudges (always point commands since the collapse moved into their prologue), both marker DROPS at their chokepoints (2026-07-29, overruling the drops' earlier keep), and the navigation jumps that always owned theirs (the site list lives at `clear_region_highlight`, input_handler.h). Composing with it, a membership REPLACE clears a SelectionExtent region OUTRIGHT — never demotes it to Free (`clear_region_on_membership_replace`, app_state.h; a demoted-but-visible span is a lie) — which is the whole region story of the non-moving membership ops (Ctrl+N, sanitize, clear_selection — and there is no exception left: Esc's two-step demote was the one route that put an extent's pixels back after a deselect, and the whole ladder is deleted, contortion ruling 5, 2026-07-29 — see region-scrub-esc.md's Esc paragraph), while the THREE MEMBERSHIP-WHOLESALE routes go further and clear ANY resting region, any provenance (the maximally greedy collapse, architect 2026-07-29): the undo/redo restore's visual tail (before the group arm derives its own extent), the `p` swap, and the propagate paste — each replaces the whole membership at once (the swap by EMPTYING it since contortion ruling 1, the other two by writing a set they took in wholesale), so a span none of them built has nothing left to describe. The SPAN-PRESERVING exceptions each earn it: the GROUP CENT STEP re-derives the highlight it moves — the last member of that class, the position movers having left it with contortion ruling 9 and the tempo drag / tempo-image step with ruling 8 (its re-derive became UNCONDITIONAL and its TrimWindow re-sync arm was deleted 2026-07-29, contortion ruling 2 — a TrimWindow region cannot rest beside the selection a tempo gesture requires, every trim setter having deselected; a TrimWindow highlight does still survive Ctrl+N and the other non-moving value gestures, by the membership-clear rule), Space plays the span, `x` consumes it into trim, pure viewport moves leave it alone. A 2+ SELECTION MAY REST WITHOUT A SPAN — THE NEVER-SPAN-LESS ENFORCEMENT IS RETIRED (architect 2026-07-29, contortion ruling 6, the set's last source ruling; NOTHING was added in its place, the settle pass having been withdrawn). What died: the distributed collapse protocol — nine `size() >= 2 → collapse_to_focused()` sites at its peak, SIX after the parked selections went (contortion ruling 1 took three), all deleted — the never-rest-2+-without-a-span paragraph that enumerated them at `clear_region_highlight`, and the injected `Selection&` dependencies it needed (`sync_region_to_trim_window`'s parameter; the propagate's member had already gone with ruling 1, and the settings editor's STAYS because the new clear below uses it). THE TWO-FORMS MODEL ITSELF STANDS UNCHANGED: while a span is ACTIVE it outranks and suppresses both point cues. What the enforcement bought on top of that was one extra guarantee, "a group always has a DRAWN playhead form", and the architect withdrew it as not worth a protocol spread across the tree. THE LAST TWO PRODUCERS CLOSED SYMMETRICALLY at the two sites where a map rebuild invalidates every index and image at once: the ENGINE-KEY SETTINGS COMMIT and the SETTINGS-ONLY ('S') UNDO/REDO RESTORE each now clear the SELECTION beside the region (GUI-kind keys are history-less, so 'S' is the only settings entry kind and the pair is complete), which also killed BOTH #19 map-changer re-lands — nothing selected means no focus to re-land, so the 'S' gate is exceptionless again: no select, no region write, no land. THE PREMISE WAS VERIFIED AGAINST THE TREE, NOT INHERITED, and it did not hold: THREE COMMANDS DO produce a resting spanless 2+ selection, and each records the state at its own site — the S/T DOMAIN FLIP (the span's endpoints are active-domain frames and cannot survive the flip, while the selection is carried across; reachable through the bare `t` key AND the settings editor's `active_audio_view=` GUI key, which the engine-commit clear does not cover), bare `0`, and `c` (both pure viewport/playhead commands that clear the span and touch no membership). THE SELECTION IS NEVER PARKED (architect 2026-07-29, contortion ruling 1 — the whole per-tab/per-column parked-selection feature DELETED WHOLESALE and replaced by COINCIDENCE AUTO-SELECT; both pending-architect flags it carried, the Ctrl+Tab stored-pair override and the parked-slot liveness rule, retire BY DELETION rather than by confirmation). THE SCOPE RULE: A COLUMN OR TAB SWITCH CLEARS THE SELECTION; THE AUDIO-VIEW SWITCH (`t`) CARRIES IT. `t` is deliberately outside the rule and untouched by it — it translates the same markers into another domain rather than changing which column is addressed, and folding it in would drop selections zoom-dependently through the T→S inverse double-round; it keeps the selection by identity and lands through the focus. So `p` and `Ctrl+Tab` now call `clear_selection` outright (the `p` swap's clear is `switch_active_markers_view_to`'s own, before the mode flip so the erase damage resolves on the LEAVING column's pixels; Ctrl+Tab's sits before the band flip for the same reason, and it subsumes the hand-spelled shift-range-anchor clear that site used to carry). COINCIDENCE AUTO-SELECT is what replaces the memory, at EXACTLY THREE ENTRY CHOKEPOINTS — the source load (past the target-view validity gate and its forced-S re-clamps), the `p` column entry (after its wholesale region clear), and the Ctrl+Tab tab entry (after playhead, trim, viewport and zoom are all live) — each stating only its own class and pointing at the one owner, `auto_select_marker_at_playhead` (input_pointer.cpp, homed beside the land whose formula it reuses). THE TEST IS THE LAND'S OWN FORMULA, EXACTLY: scan the ACTIVE column's store IN ORDER and single-select the first marker whose `clamp_playhead_to_live_domain(source_frame_to_active_domain(time_frame))` equals `playhead_cursor_sample` — the land's two helpers reused verbatim, never a second spelling of the same conversion, and an EXACT int64 compare with NO tolerance, so this reads "is the playhead STANDING ON a marker" and not "is it near one" (a cursor one frame away selects nothing, which is right: selecting it would move no pixel while silently arming the marker lane). FIRST-IN-STORE wins on a tie — markers may coincide exactly in both stores, and in target view distinct source frames can share one target image under a compressing segment — an arbitrary but total rule, and the stores being time-ordered makes "first" the earliest-authored of the coincident group. The ordinary single-select + land adjacency is kept, and the land is a PROVABLE NO-OP here (its early-return equality test IS the selection predicate): it stays so the marker-lane rule keeps no exception. The payoff is the lane model itself — with that marker selected the flag's ink triangle IS the playhead, exactly the state the entry's stored cursor already described. WHAT THE USER LOSES AND GAINS: a group or singleton left standing in the other column or tab is GONE, re-acquired with one click (the accepted loss; the payoff of parking anti-correlated with the reason to switch — the liveness rule dropped the slot precisely whenever the trip had done productive work). What is gained: the tabs' stored cursor is honored VERBATIM again, so the A/B contract carries no exception; every view switch leaves the bare arrows in the WAVEFORM lane, so no switch can silently arm an authoring mode; and the product holds ZERO raw store indices across commands, retiring the held-index lesson as a live concern instead of managing it. THE GENERALIZATION, the rule this ruling would write (auditor 1): VALUE-SHAPED SESSION STATE PARKS SAFELY; INDEX-SHAPED SESSION STATE DOES NOT. The per-tab band that survives — viewport, zoom, playhead, trim, read_only — is frame-, value- or flag-shaped, so global-store edits made in the other tab cannot invalidate it (a FRAME still names the same position after an insert, a delete or a wholesale replace; a raw INDEX does not), and its one domain hazard, a target total changed between switches, is handled by a single `clamp_playhead_to_live_domain` call at the restore. The parked selections were the product's one exception, and the machinery they needed to stay honest — a SECOND generation counter on each marker store with its whole bump-site taxonomy, `marker_rows_identical`, the reorder remap into the slots (and the remapper's `column` parameter, which had no other justification), the collapse-at-restore at three sites, `park_selection_stamp` / `drop_parked_selection_if_stale` and their post-wholesale-replace stamping loops — is gone with them. ONE PLAYHEAD PER TAB, recorded at `ViewState`: `playhead_cursor_sample` is the whole of it, the columns share it, and no per-column sibling ever existed as a value (what acted as one was the parked FOCUS, under the lane model, and it died with the slots). The rule and its rationale live at `ViewState` and at `AppState::selected_markers` (app_state.h).
