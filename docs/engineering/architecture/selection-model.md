THE LANE MODEL (architect 2026-07-28) — the reason the horizontal arrows split on selection emptiness. With a selection the focus model stops painting the cursor playhead and the focused flag's kWaveform-filled ink triangle IS the playhead; with a group it is the extent region's recolored ground. So the arrows ALWAYS move the playhead, and the selection decides only which LANE it currently lives in: empty = the waveform lane (a one-column playhead step), non-empty = the MARKER lane (the same step, carrying the marker under it — a position nudge in either column's home view, the tempo-image step in W+target). The model is not a fiction layered over two unrelated gestures: every marker-lane route re-lands the playhead on the committed focused marker (`finish_group_position_nudge` for both position nudges, the post-re-warp re-land in `step_tempo_image`), so on Esc the cursor genuinely reappears at the nudged position rather than where it was when the selection began. Esc is the EXPLICIT collapse back to the waveform lane — the ladder's own rung, not a fallback; this project has no gesture fallbacks, so a marker-lane press that refuses stays a consumed no-op. ONE PREDICATE OWNS THE SPLIT (`GuiInputHandler::playhead_in_marker_lane`, currently exactly a non-empty selection) with TWO readers — the on_key dispatch that picks the route, and `read_only_key_blocked`'s `is_playhead_step` entry, which admits the bare horizontal arrows only in the waveform lane because in the marker lane the same press AUTHORS and that gate is the sole read-only defense for all three routes. THE PLAYHEAD HAS TWO FORMS, POINT AND SPAN, AND EXACTLY ONE IS EVER VISIBLE (architect 2026-07-29, superseding the one-day-old MOVEMENT GATE after its composition with land-on-focus failed in live use — a shift-range-then-Enter collapse was a no-motion land, the gate skipped the dissolve, and the demoted extent span rested frozen under a marker the arrows then moved). The POINT form is the cursor (empty selection) or the singleton stem / focused flag; the SPAN form is the REGION — which is why an active region dissolves the cursor into the split half-triangles and now ALSO suppresses the singleton stem (paint_selected_stem's early return; the selection persists beneath, the focused flag keeping its ink triangle — only the point-form PAINT yields). The land is a PURE playhead write with no region side effect, and every playhead-moving POINT command owns its own region collapse at its own site, UNCONDITIONALLY — never gated on whether the playhead actually moved: the plain click and its four deferred completions, the multi-select clicks at a result under two, `enter_text_edit` (the one chokepoint of every editor open/retarget), the singleton position nudge, both marker DROPS at their chokepoints (2026-07-29, overruling the drops' earlier keep), and the navigation jumps that always owned theirs (the site list lives at `clear_region_highlight`, input_handler.h). Composing with it, a membership REPLACE clears a SelectionExtent region OUTRIGHT — never demotes it to Free (`clear_region_on_membership_replace`, app_state.h; a demoted-but-visible span is a lie) — which is the whole region story of the non-moving membership ops (Ctrl+N, sanitize/prune, clear_selection — Esc's ladder instead walks the explicit two-step demote — deselect with the span resting, then collapse — see region-scrub-esc.md), while the THREE MEMBERSHIP-WHOLESALE routes go further and clear ANY resting region, any provenance (the maximally greedy collapse, architect 2026-07-29): the undo/redo restore's visual tail (before the group arm derives its own extent), the `p` swap, and the propagate paste — each hands the lane a selection it took in wholesale, so a span it did not build has nothing left to describe. The SPAN-PRESERVING exceptions each earn it: the group gestures and the whole TEMPO family re-derive/re-sync the highlight they move (the built arms, bit-for-bit — a TrimWindow highlight deliberately survives the value gestures, Ctrl+N included), Space plays the span, `x` consumes it into trim, pure viewport moves leave it alone. The one open that RE-DERIVES a span is `m` (the span's focus jumps to its owner): its handler re-derives the extent highlight right after restoring the membership — the clear-then-extent order, the only direction that works — so the span cue survives the open. Value gestures take the same rule in image form — both tempo steps re-land on the focused marker's post-step image in target view, the singleton for the LABEL-DEFINITION repricing that can move even its own image.
