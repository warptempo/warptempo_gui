SELECTION AND PLAYHEAD ARE RE-COUPLED (Ableton-parity arc step 1, architect-designed 2026-07-23). GENEALOGY: coupled originally → DECOUPLED 2026-07-20 (for the lead-in workflow) → RE-COUPLED 2026-07-23, legitimate because the audition scrub supplies the upstream-audition workflow the decoupling existed for. THE INVARIANT: clicking a marker ALWAYS lands the playhead on it — the PLAIN marker click (the FLAG BOX, the marker's ONE pointer surface — the lane run died with the marker-text lane at row 5, and the upper-half stem band died with the stems-inert ruling of 2026-08-12 below; both views/columns, read-only allowed) single-selects AND seats the playhead on the marker with NO viewport move (direct cursor write through the two-step placement basis `source_frame_to_active_domain` then `clamp_playhead_to_live_domain`; the sole difference from Tab and `c`, which both recenter — and `c` alone re-zooms to the working level, Tab having carried that landing for one commit on 2026-08-05 before the same-day revert) — and the playhead FOLLOWS the focused/grabbed marker through nudges and drags UNCONDITIONALLY: every focus route lands the playhead on the focus it sets (A LAND IS ALWAYS ON THE FOCUS since 2026-07-28), so the TOWED SET IS EMPTY and the follow is a re-land as the marker moves, never a repair. Every marker click is also a POINT command that HIDES THE TRIM REGION OVERLAY unconditionally at its own site (since 2026-07-30 with no result-size split — a click says "the playhead is HERE"; it was a CLEAR of a free scratch span until 2026-08-18 and is a hide that discards nothing since, the region BEING the trim now). THE SELECTION MODEL (arc step 2): Shift+click is an INCLUSIVE RANGE from the anchor (`app.shift_range_anchor`, session scratch seeded by adopting the focus; owned by the Selection mutators alone — no release owner since 2026-07-29, the accepted post-release delta recorded at the field's declaration, app_state.h), each successive shift-click REPLACING the selection with the anchor..clicked range, clicked = range end = FOCUS, landed there; Ctrl+click is the individual membership TOGGLE, landing on the focus it leaves. Neither arms drags, seeds double-clicks, or opens editors; the shared land is `land_playhead_on_marker` (input_pointer.cpp). **ADD TO SELECTION IS A STICKY CTRL FOR GLASS (architect 2026-08-18)** — bare `k` and a bottom-row button light a MODE (`AppState::add_to_selection`, whose declaration is the whole contract) in which a PLAIN flag click takes the ctrl branch whole: toggle membership, land on the focus the toggle leaves, keep the rest. His reason is the rig: "none of the modifier-click vocabulary exists on touch, so buttons are how glass gets selection work" — a finger has no ctrl to hold, so the one gesture that ACCUMULATES a selection was unreachable there. It is folded into the toggle term at `run_marker_click_act` rather than grown into a fourth arm — `ctrl || (add_to_selection && !shift)` — so the two spellings cannot come to mean different things, and **SHIFT BEATS IT**: that `&& !shift` is load-bearing, since the fork is `if (toggle) … else if (shift)` and a bare fold onto ctrl would let a lit mode swallow a held shift and turn a range select into a toggle ("Shift+click needs no rule here, because it has its own gesture"). A real ctrl+click is unaffected either way. IT IS SCOPED TO FLAG HITS BY CONSTRUCTION, not by a second gate: that owner is the mode's ONE site and only ever runs on a resolved flag hit, so the empty lane, the ruler and the waveform keep their whole vocabulary while it is lit. IT AUTO-CLEARS AT THE BOUNDARY THAT ENDS A PLAIN CTRL+CLICK'S EFFECT — the accumulated membership, which any selection REPLACE or CLEAR ends — implemented as THE SHIFT-RANGE ANCHOR'S OWN RULE WITH THE KEEPER SWAPPED, which is what makes the two mirror images: `toggle_selection_membership` (the mode's own act) keeps the mode and clears the anchor, `select_range_from_anchor` keeps the anchor and clears the mode, and every OTHER `Selection` mutator clears both; `repair_last_selected` is deliberately NOT one of them, being a focus repair reached from inside the toggle itself. Riding the `Selection` chokepoint means a mutator added later inherits the clear by default. ITS GATES ARE ITS OWN: the `h` view greys the button through the derived partition, while the READ-ONLY LOCK LEAVES IT LIT — a selection is navigation, not authored content, the reasoning that already keeps the trim gestures legal on a locked tab, so bare `k` sits on `read_only_key_blocked`'s allowlist explicitly. ALL MARKER CLICKS ARE FLAG-LANE-ONLY, IN EVERY VIEW: the 2026-08-06 SYMMETRY RULING put the MODIFIED clicks there (generalizing the 2026-08-01 stem gate — over the waveform ctrl is the zoom (the one nav drag's ctrl phase since 2026-08-14) and shift is the trim sweep, at the very pixels a stem stands on, so A WAVEFORM MODIFIER IS GESTURE VOCABULARY AND SELECTION IS LANE VOCABULARY; the `h` view's one-day stem-based range select and ctrl toggle were DELETED by it), and the SEVENTH GLASS RULING (architect 2026-08-12, STEMS POINTER-INERT IN ALL CONTEXTS) repealed the clause that survived it — "PLAIN clicks keep the stem hit, that being the one surface where an item and the waveform overlap with no modifier to spend" — so the PLAIN click is lane vocabulary too: no waveform press resolves a marker in any view, a drag can never accidentally grab one, and the stems are a painted cue alone (marker-ui.md carries the succession — the question stayed open through the touch arc, was answered "stems stay" while the scrollbar plan lived, and closed the other way once the waveform-height clamp put the flag lane in easy reach everywhere). THE SELECTION IS NEVER PARKED (architect 2026-07-29, contortion ruling 1). THE SCOPE RULE: A COLUMN OR TAB SWITCH CLEARS THE SELECTION; THE AUDIO-VIEW SWITCH (`t`) CARRIES IT — and since 2026-07-30 `t` carries a GROUP whole (no collapse; the selection-gated land above). `p` and Ctrl+Tab call `clear_selection` outright (each clear placed before its flip so the erase damage resolves on the leaving pixels). COINCIDENCE AUTO-SELECT replaces the memory, at EXACTLY FOUR ENTRY CHOKEPOINTS — the source load, the `p` column entry, the Ctrl+Tab tab entry, and the `'` load-in-place (joined 2026-07-30) — each stating its own class and pointing at the one owner, `auto_select_marker_at_playhead` (input_pointer.cpp, homed beside the land whose formula it reuses). THE TEST IS THE LAND'S OWN FORMULA, EXACTLY: scan the active column's store in order and single-select the first marker whose `clamp_playhead_to_live_domain(source_frame_to_active_domain(time_frame))` equals `playhead_cursor_sample` — an EXACT int64 compare, "is the playhead STANDING ON a marker", first-in-store on ties; the adjacent land is a provable no-op (its early-return equality test IS the selection predicate). The payoff: the entry's stored cursor already described a marker under the cursor, and selecting it arms the lane the user would expect there — with the cursor visibly standing on the selected flag (the `t` land's exactness is what keeps this compare true across domain flips). WHAT THE USER LOSES AND GAINS: a group or singleton left in the other column/tab is GONE, re-acquired with one click; the stored cursor is honored VERBATIM, no switch silently arms an authoring mode on an unexpected marker, and the product holds ZERO raw store indices across commands. THE GENERALIZATION: VALUE-SHAPED SESSION STATE PARKS SAFELY; INDEX-SHAPED SESSION STATE DOES NOT — the per-tab band (viewport, zoom, playhead, trim, `read_only`, and since 2026-08-18 bare `0`'s `zoom_recall_level`) is frame-, value- or flag-shaped; the parked selections were the one exception and their liveness machinery is deleted. ONE PLAYHEAD PER TAB (`ViewState`): `playhead_cursor_sample` is the whole of it. The rule and rationale live at `ViewState` and `AppState::selected_markers` (app_state.h).
