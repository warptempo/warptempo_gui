SELECTION AND PLAYHEAD ARE RE-COUPLED (Ableton-parity arc step 1, architect-designed 2026-07-23). GENEALOGY: coupled originally → DECOUPLED 2026-07-20 (for the lead-in workflow) → RE-COUPLED 2026-07-23, legitimate because the scrub area supplies the upstream-audition workflow the decoupling existed for. THE INVARIANT: clicking a marker ALWAYS lands the playhead on it — the PLAIN marker click (the flag box or the upper-half stem band — the lane run died with the marker-text lane at row 5; both views/columns, read-only allowed) single-selects AND seats the playhead on the marker with NO viewport move (direct cursor write through the two-step placement basis `source_frame_to_active_domain` then `clamp_playhead_to_live_domain`; the sole difference from Tab and `c`, which both recenter — and `c` alone re-zooms to the working level, Tab having carried that landing for one commit on 2026-08-05 before the same-day revert) — and the playhead FOLLOWS the focused/grabbed marker through nudges and drags UNCONDITIONALLY: every focus route lands the playhead on the focus it sets (A LAND IS ALWAYS ON THE FOCUS since 2026-07-28), so the TOWED SET IS EMPTY and the follow is a re-land as the marker moves, never a repair. Every marker click is also a POINT command that clears a resting region UNCONDITIONALLY at its own site (since 2026-07-30 with no result-size split — the region is trim scratch and a click says "the playhead is HERE"). THE SELECTION MODEL (arc step 2): Shift+click is an INCLUSIVE RANGE from the anchor (`app.shift_range_anchor`, session scratch seeded by adopting the focus; owned by the Selection mutators alone — no release owner since 2026-07-29, the accepted post-release delta recorded at the field's declaration, app_state.h), each successive shift-click REPLACING the selection with the anchor..clicked range, clicked = range end = FOCUS, landed there; Ctrl+click is the individual membership TOGGLE, landing on the focus it leaves. MULTI-SELECT VISUALS (row-5 form since 2026-08-01): a selection's cue is its members' BRIGHTENED FLAGS — the class ladder's brighter fill/edge pair — with the ALWAYS-VISIBLE playhead cursor standing on the landed focus; SELECTION NO LONGER PAINTS A SPAN (2026-07-30, the region simplification: `set_region_to_selection_extent`, the SelectionExtent provenance, and the membership-replace region clear are all deleted). DRAG MECHANICS: waveform plain/Shift presses stay marker-blind; the follow moves the RESTING cursor only (the nudge stops playback first; the drag never does), all target mapping through the live memoized cache; the drag's commit lands the playhead against the POST-commit map and runs regardless of net change; the drag has NO CANCEL (rulings 3+4, 2026-07-29 — Esc mid-drag is a consumed no-op, any end COMMITS, undo is the way back). HORIZONTAL MOVEMENT IS A FOCUS ACT — GROUPS ARE NEVER MOVED (architect 2026-07-29, ruling 9): ONE MARKER MOVES PER GESTURE. The POINTER: a plain flag press on a member of a 2+ selection single-selects and lands IMMEDIATELY at press time (the deferral machinery is deleted), and the drag it arms is an ordinary singleton drag. The KEYBOARD: the bare Left/Right marker-lane step with 2+ selected COLLAPSES to the focus in the twins' shared prologue (`position_nudge_prologue`) and steps that focus alone; a press that refuses at its wall KEEPS the collapse. The singleton drag survives bit-for-bit (viewport clamp widened to contain zero, release column snap through `authored_frame_at_column`, the [0, eof_wall] belt). THE GROUP-VERB DOCTRINE lives at the head of `position_nudge.h`: GROUP-CAPABLE where the members are INDEPENDENT or the selection's span is merely READ — Ctrl+D, the Up/Down cent step, Delete, `m`, Ctrl+P, `x`, the zoom framing, the undo/paste restores; FOCUS-COLLAPSE where the members are COUPLED or the act is POSITIONAL — Ctrl+N (the pass→owner freeze reads the resolved walk) and all horizontal movement. THE COLLAPSE FAMILY IS TWO SITES (2026-07-30, down from four): the nudges' shared prologue and Ctrl+N — `t` and `c` CARRY a group whole now (the region simplification undid their collapses: the spanless-group state they existed to avoid is simply the normal state under the always-visible cursor). The architect's general rule stands verbatim at the doctrine: if group is relatively cheap to implement, implement it; otherwise collapse to last selected — no hybrid third form. THE LANE MODEL (architect 2026-07-28; KEPT BY EXPLICIT RULING 2026-07-30 with the cursor visible — "keep arrows-nudge-the-focused-marker with the cursor visible"). The bare horizontal arrows always move the playhead, and the selection decides which LANE the press lives in: EMPTY = the waveform lane (a one-column playhead step); NON-EMPTY = the MARKER lane (the same step carrying the FOCUSED marker — a position nudge in either column's home view, NOTHING in W+target, a consumed refusal). The honest statement since the suppression retired: the arrows move the focused marker and the playhead rides along VISIBLY — both marker-lane routes re-land the playhead on the committed focus (`finish_position_nudge`), so cursor and focus stay coincident on screen. ONE PREDICATE OWNS THE SPLIT (`GuiInputHandler::playhead_in_marker_lane`, exactly a non-empty selection) with TWO readers — the on_key dispatch and `read_only_key_blocked`'s `is_playhead_step` entry, the sole read-only defense for both marker-lane routes (in the lane the same press AUTHORS). LANE EXIT IS ANY DESELECTING ROUTE; a refused marker-lane press is a consumed no-op, no fallback. THE MARKER LANE OWNS THE PLAYHEAD (architect 2026-07-28): any route that changes WHICH marker is focused while the lane is active LANDS the playhead on the new focus — otherwise the cursor would visibly rest away from the marker the arrows are about to move, and Space would play from that stale spot. The routes: every flag/BPM editor open and retarget (`enter_text_edit`, the one chokepoint), the Ctrl+N collapse, the two position nudges' prologue, the propagate paste landing on the FIRST created reset, and the `t` S/T toggle's selection-gated land — which since 2026-07-30 lands WITHOUT collapsing (the group carries; the land is the EXACT re-expression of a cursor already resting on the focus, closing the T→S inverse double-round that the generic translation would leave — architect-confirmed: the land fires only when a selection is non-empty, where the cursor already rests on the focus, and the empty-selection generic translation is untouched). The CONVERSE clause: a route that LEAVES the lane (Home/End, every deselect) leaves the playhead where it lands, for the cursor to keep painting — which it now always does. Value gestures take the rule in image form: both arms of the cent step re-land on the focused marker's post-step image in target view (the singleton for the label-definition repricing, the only map-change re-land left). THE SELECTION IS NEVER PARKED (architect 2026-07-29, contortion ruling 1). THE SCOPE RULE: A COLUMN OR TAB SWITCH CLEARS THE SELECTION; THE AUDIO-VIEW SWITCH (`t`) CARRIES IT — and since 2026-07-30 `t` carries a GROUP whole (no collapse; the selection-gated land above). `p` and Ctrl+Tab call `clear_selection` outright (each clear placed before its flip so the erase damage resolves on the leaving pixels). COINCIDENCE AUTO-SELECT replaces the memory, at EXACTLY FOUR ENTRY CHOKEPOINTS — the source load, the `p` column entry, the Ctrl+Tab tab entry, and the `'` load-in-place (joined 2026-07-30) — each stating its own class and pointing at the one owner, `auto_select_marker_at_playhead` (input_pointer.cpp, homed beside the land whose formula it reuses). THE TEST IS THE LAND'S OWN FORMULA, EXACTLY: scan the active column's store in order and single-select the first marker whose `clamp_playhead_to_live_domain(source_frame_to_active_domain(time_frame))` equals `playhead_cursor_sample` — an EXACT int64 compare, "is the playhead STANDING ON a marker", first-in-store on ties; the adjacent land is a provable no-op (its early-return equality test IS the selection predicate). The payoff: the entry's stored cursor already described a marker under the cursor, and selecting it arms the lane the user would expect there — with the cursor visibly standing on the selected flag (the `t` land's exactness is what keeps this compare true across domain flips). WHAT THE USER LOSES AND GAINS: a group or singleton left in the other column/tab is GONE, re-acquired with one click; the stored cursor is honored VERBATIM, no switch silently arms an authoring mode on an unexpected marker, and the product holds ZERO raw store indices across commands. THE GENERALIZATION: VALUE-SHAPED SESSION STATE PARKS SAFELY; INDEX-SHAPED SESSION STATE DOES NOT — the per-tab band (viewport, zoom, playhead, trim, read_only) is frame-, value- or flag-shaped; the parked selections were the one exception and their liveness machinery is deleted. ONE PLAYHEAD PER TAB (`ViewState`): `playhead_cursor_sample` is the whole of it. The rule and rationale live at `ViewState` and `AppState::selected_markers` (app_state.h).
