# The Home-View Binding and the Tempo Cent Step

Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md in the 2026-07-25 slim-down (project feature-complete). This file is now the authoritative home for this detail; CLAUDE.md carries only a digest and a pointer here. Keep it current with landed rulings.

### The Home-View Binding (architect 2026-07-22)
Each marker column AUTHORS in its HOME audio view only — warp markers in SOURCE view, phase resets in TARGET view — via the ONE predicate `active_column_authoring_allowed` (app_state.h) consulted beside the read-only-tab checks at the dispatch chokepoints (the flag drag's threshold crossing, the bare Left/Right nudges, the bare `s` drop and its empty-lane double-click twin, the `m` bpm open, and the PHASE-RESET arms of Delete and Ctrl+D — whose home is target. The `i` toggle LEFT that list on 2026-08-07, see the third exception below; **Delete, Ctrl+D, Ctrl+N and the flag-editor open routes left it on 2026-08-24 in the WARP column**, see the fifth). Off home a column is display/navigation-only: selection, hover, Tab cycling, `c`, the marker-click land, the value pair (bare `j` and Shift+`j`, which author nothing and take no home-view term of their own), and red flags all live; every store mutation refuses SILENTLY, navigation-class, exactly the read-only convention (read-only refusals compose and outrank). Rationale: warp placement edits in target view mutate the map the view is displayed in (imprecise by construction — this killed the viewport-wall clamp bug class undiagnosed), and phase resets are inaudible in source view. THAT RATIONALE IS POSITIONAL, AND SINCE 2026-08-24 THE PREDICATE'S REACH IS TOO: what it gates is PLACEMENT — an edit that moves a marker inside the very map the view is drawn from — and status, existence and value edits had been swept into the same blanket refusal without the reason applying to them (the fifth exception below is that correction). FIVE ruled exceptions since 2026-08-24, two of them from 2026-07-22 (the same-day 2026-07-24 "third exception" — a both-views warp POSITION nudge — was RE-RULED AWAY in the second pass as a misreading: W+target collapses to ONE MOTION, stretch/squish the waveform, and no warp position authoring exists there; its scaffolding was harvested at 79b73d3, the ac0ae2c precedent).

(1) THE BARE UP/DOWN TEMPO CENT STEP in W+target — SINGLETON AND GROUP, and since 2026-07-29 the WHOLE TEMPO SURFACE. It was one of THREE flavors of the one motion until contortion ruling 8 deleted the other two wholesale: the pointer TEMPO DRAG (the Ableton-style horizontal flag drag that rewrote the group predecessor's `tempo_cents` live per cent step) and its keyboard twin the bare Left/Right TEMPO-IMAGE STEP (`step_tempo_image`). The architect's ruling: "the tempo drag was an Ableton-parity nicety; the keyboard step is just as good", and then the image step went with it — so tempo is authored by Up/Down alone, in both ruled contexts, W+target included. GONE WITH THEM, recorded so none of it is re-proposed: `TempoDragState` and its whole pointer plumbing, `PendingTempoDrag` (and the press DEFERRAL that surface carried), `tempo_drag_predecessor`'s eligibility walk with all six refusal legs, `TempoGroupSeed` / `seed_tempo_group_participants` with the deduped participant set and its `walled` pin, the GROUP LABEL WALL that guaranteed the objective's monotonicity, `solve_tempo_group_delta`'s two-path solve (closed-form singleton / exact monotone bisection over hypothetical map builds), the image step's MINIMUM-STEP rule on the painter-quantized spp, and `GestureKind::TempoImageStep`. Two consequences worth naming: bare Left/Right in W+target WITH a selection is now a CONSUMED REFUSAL (no fallback, by the standing rule that a refused marker-lane press is a consumed no-op; with NO selection it stays the waveform-lane playhead step, unchanged), and W+TARGET HAS NO POINTER AUTHORING GESTURE AT ALL — a plain flag click there selects and lands the playhead but no drag can begin from it, exactly like P+source — and that click runs AT THE PRESS (press-time again since 2026-08-17, after the one-day lift model of 2026-08-15), with the two authoring gates at the threshold CROSSING rather than at the arm, so the arm itself is unconditional and it is the DRAG alone that off-home refuses (`marker-ui.md` is authoritative). Two residues reached into the FROZEN parser and are now DELETED THERE TOO, under an explicit surgical freeze approval from the architect (2026-07-29): the `quiet` resolve parameter — which suppressed normalization stderr lines for the bisection's HYPOTHETICAL never-live candidate maps, and went caller-less with them — together with the GUI shim parameter that forwarded it, so every resolve and every target-view map build in the product is now LOUD unconditionally; and `MarkerEffective::owner_idx`, the raw-store index of an inheritance walk's terminal owner, whose sole consumer was the drag's forward-label coupling guard. `resolve_inherited_tempo` keeps its defaulted `owner_index` out-parameter, which no caller passes today — a capability rather than a residue, and outside the approval's scope. `from_ref` beside the deleted field stays: the GUI's normalization-red set reads it.

THE STEP ITSELF. It stays reachable in target view (that is the exception) but is OWNER-ONLY there — a pass or label ref refuses silently, no freeze conversion (source view keeps the pass/ref-to-owner freeze unchanged) — and a COINCIDENT-COLLAPSED owner refuses too (render-inert: the resolver replaces every exact-frame run of 2+ effectively-enabled markers with one synthetic plain 1.00 owner, so the write would move nothing; tested via `warp_red_flag_set_cached`, and for a payload-owner red ⇔ collapsed, the bdf4336 argument — the stack is fixed at the source in warp view). THE GROUP ARM (architect 2026-07-23, post-labwc — "bump the speed of a group at once"; `adjust_tempo_cents_group`, warpmarkers_ops.cpp) is MAXIMALLY STRICT and all-or-nothing — an ineligible member is "the wall being hit before it starts to move", never skipped: every selected marker's OWN tempo steps one cent, and the VIEW-INDEPENDENT wall set — pass, ref, DISABLED, coincident-collapsed (`warp_red_flag_set_cached`), or already at the bracket edge in the step direction — refuses the WHOLE press silently (no per-member skip; the source-view pass/ref→owner freeze conversion stays SINGLETON-ONLY, and the singleton keeps its per-view behavior bit-for-bit incl. the target-only collapsed refusal and constructive clamp). One coalescing-eligible undo entry per press with touched hints (touched_snapshot == touched_live, no reorder — positions never move), and the target-view tail re-lands the playhead on the FOCUSED marker's post-step image (an upstream member's change moves downstream images). THE SINGLETON RE-LANDS TOO SINCE 2026-07-28, closing what had been a recorded accepted gap: its own image is fixed by construction in the ordinary case — a marker's tempo shapes only the segment AFTER it — but a LABEL DEFINITION reprices every reference to it, including references EARLIER in the timeline, whose spans then change duration and shift everything downstream, the stepped marker's own image included. Under the marker-lane-owns-the-playhead rule that is no longer cosmetic: the focused flag IS the playhead, so an image moving out from under it makes the flag lie. The re-land costs nothing when the image did not move (the playhead is written the value it already holds), and both arms gate on target view — source is the identity domain, where no image moves at all. The GROUP's reach is what differs from the singleton's, not the rule. These two re-lands were the ONLY map-change re-lands in the product until 2026-08-24, when the fifth exception below added FOUR MORE on exactly this pattern (Ctrl+D, Ctrl+N, Delete and the flag-editor commit); the caller inventory is `Viewport::reseat_playhead_to`'s definition, viewport.cpp. The settings engine-commit's and the settings-only undo arm's twins died with contortion ruling 6, which has both CLEAR the selection instead, leaving no focus to re-land.

THE COLUMN GATE IS A NAMED PREDICATE, AND THE TWO BOTTOM-ROW ARROW BUTTONS WEAR IT (architect 2026-08-13). `adjust_tempo_cents` refused on an unnamed first line — `app.active_markers_view != 'W'`, ahead of the selection and focus checks and shared by both arms — and that line is now `tempo_cent_step_column_allowed` (app_state.h). It is deliberately NOT `active_column_authoring_allowed`: the cent step is that predicate's exception (1) above, live in W+target as well as W+source, so what it asks is the COLUMN and not the home view. The bottom row's UP and DOWN buttons — bare Up / Down like every chrome button is its chord — read the same predicate through `redesign_button_enabled`, so they wore the grey disabled face in the phase-reset marker view for a few hours on 2026-08-15 and DO NOT ANY MORE: the roster's face policy settled that same day on mirroring only what the SCREEN DOES NOT ALREADY ANSWER, and this pair is the architect's own worked example of why — in the WARP view they need a selection and in the PHASE RESET view they cannot act at all, so the mirror blinked them on and off at every marker selection while the W/P radios already say which column is live. The step's VALUE-SHAPED refusals stay live-faced consumed no-ops (a label ref, a pass in target, a bracket wall), while its STRUCTURAL ones now grey too since 2026-08-15, when the architect ruled the whole bottom row must be accurate to the live state: `tempo_cent_step_actionable` is `adjust_tempo_cents`' own first three returns made literal and called by the act itself, so face and act are one decision. LEFT AND RIGHT ARE NO LONGER UNTOUCHED by that ruling either — still the marker nudge in the focused marker's home view with gates of their own, but their face read `horizontal_arrow_step_actionable` for those same few hours and are ALWAYS-ON again, the predicate DELETED as producer-less — the act never called it, so the face-and-act-are-one-decision claim was never true of that pair; Left/Right were always-on for a REASON rather than by omission, the playhead being nearly always able to move, so mirroring a rarely-true refusal bought a nearly-always-live pair that blinked per selection.

THE GROUP STEP'S REGION TAIL. An ACTIVE SelectionExtent region FOLLOWS the moved images, and THE TEMPO SURFACE NO LONGER BRANCHES ON PROVENANCE AT ALL (architect 2026-07-29, contortion ruling 2): the step re-derived the extent unconditionally in its target-view tail — UNTIL 2026-07-30, when the extent itself died with the region simplification: selection no longer paints a span, `set_region_to_selection_extent` is deleted, and the group cent step's tail keeps only the playhead re-land (the kick's live-domain reclamp still wholesale-clears an out-of-domain scratch region; a region cannot rest beside the selection a tempo gesture requires — a highlight only ever rests with nothing selected). The TrimWindow re-sync arms were DELETED at every tempo site, the singleton step's #16 re-sync included (its reach chain — click a marker, click the trim bar, Up/Down — is foreclosed at the trim-bar click's deselect), and with them the COINCIDENT-arm collapse story: `TempoDragState::grab_trim_highlight`, the ERROR-state / no-mid-gesture-carve-out ruling, and the b44ad2e verbatim `pre_drag_region` restore are all gone (the coincident arm itself is deleted — a coincident window now rests ACTIVE at one column; see region-scrub-esc.md). The extent-maintainer class is EMPTY since 2026-07-30 (the region simplification deleted the extent itself): the group cent step was its last member, the position movers having left when groups stopped being movable (ruling 9) and the tempo drag and image step with ruling 8.

(2) The phase-reset PROPAGATE is a warp-view gesture (one or more selected warp markers under the section rule, like the bpm command — see below) available in BOTH audio views — it authors phase resets, never warp markers — and it is the one action that STARTS in source view and ENDS in target view: every paste tail lands in target view through the `t` toggle chokepoint (`land_paste_in_target_view`, wired via a GuiInputHandler friend + back-pointer like the settings editor), switches to P view, and SELECTS exactly the pasted resets — the FIRST one focused and landed (e5a37e5, matching the programmatic earliest-focus rule) (the created set's extent-region write died with the span form, 2026-07-30 — the members' ink triangles and the landed cursor are the cue) — replacing the old undo/redo inspection round-trip; a paste that materializes nothing instead does NOTHING AT ALL to the selection or the playhead (the no-created-set arm, general to both paste kinds, reduced to an empty arm by contortion ruling 1, 2026-07-29: the tail's column swap now CLEARS the selection and nothing restores one, so there is no group to collapse and no focus to land on — the cursor is the playhead, resting where the S→T re-express put the paste anchor's image, and a paste that did nothing changes nothing). Its route through the `t` chokepoint is also why the toggle's own spanless-group outcome (see selection-model.md's retirement paragraph) never surfaces here: the column swap immediately after clears the selection, and the tail then writes its own.

(3) THE ITERATION-BRACKET WIPE IN W+TARGET (granted 2026-08-07, with the architect's ruling that ITERATION MODE IS TARGET-LEGAL). TWO SITES, one argument, stated once at `run_iteration_sweep_render`'s success tail (input_key_dispatch.cpp): the SWEEP'S SUCCESS TAIL — which turns the mode off and clears every bracket through `wipe_iter_state`, and which is what gated the whole sweep command to source view (bdf4336, codex P1, 2026-07-22; that gate is now gone, so `Ctrl+Alt+R` dispatches the sweep from either audio view) — and the `i` TOGGLE'S OFF BRANCH, which runs the same clear and is now reachable in target because the mode rests there. It is the NARROWEST of the three exceptions and takes THE CENT STEP'S OWN CLASS of argument: iter brackets are SESSION-ONLY warp-store fields — never serialized (no sidecar key), excluded from the render recipe, pushed with `affects_persistence=false` — so the write reaches neither disk nor a render, and in the sweep's case it clears the very state the command just consumed. What is NOT an exception: the `i` TOGGLE ITSELF, which relaxed from `active_column_authoring_allowed` to the WARP COLUMN ALONE and toggles in either audio view. That is MODE STATE, not authored content — the bit selects what `Ctrl+Alt+R` means and what the flags show — so it leaves the predicate's consumer list rather than joining the exception list. BRACKET AUTHORING WAS THE LAST THING THIS EXCEPTION LEFT BEHIND, AND IT FOLLOWED ON 2026-08-24: the sentence that stood here — "the flag editor's iter grammar keeps its home gate through both open routes" — is SUPERSEDED by the fifth exception below. The grammar never had a gate of its own; it rode the editor's, and the editor is legal in both audio views now, so a bracket is typed wherever the editor opens (`enter_top_flag_edit`'s `iter_on` asks the warp COLUMN and the marker's eligibility, never the audio view, so nothing had to be relaxed for it). The S↔T toggle still closes an open editor without committing, symmetric in both directions now and for a new reason.

THE SUPERSEDED RULING BEHIND IT (recorded so it is not re-proposed as new). Until 2026-08-07, entering target view EXITED iteration mode and wiped every bracket (architect 2026-07-23, commit 434ac13: "brackets are the step-away batch tool, target view the live-by-hand tool"), which made mode-off-in-target an INVARIANT four other sites leaned on. Both that ruling and the sweep's source-view gate predate the W+target bare cent step (contortion ruling 8, 2026-07-29), which already admitted live tempo authoring in target and broke the step-away/live-by-hand partition; and nothing technical ever bound a bracket to source view, brackets being DELTA-RELATIVE (the sweep enumerates base + delta per cell), so one composes with a base-tempo change made from either view. The mode bit and the brackets now persist across S↔T in BOTH directions, everywhere — the settings-editor `active_audio_view=T` commit routing through the same edge inherits the persistence as it inherited the wipe.

(4) THE MARKER MEASURE (architect 2026-08-19; the field was the marker COMMENT for its first day and was rebranded 2026-08-20 — the exception is unchanged by the rename) — the WIDEST of the four and the only one that is BOTH COLUMNS AND BOTH VIEWS. A measure names where a marker sits IN THE SCORE rather than authoring a musical value: it reaches no engine surface at all (`MarkerForRender` never carries one, and the render fingerprint serializes resolved fields only, so a measure cannot move a render key), it changes no frame map, and nothing about it is view-dependent — so it is editable WHEREVER THE FLAG PAINTS. Its three entry routes (bare `/`, the bottom-row Measure button, the double-click on the blue measure box) consult `active_column_authoring_allowed` NOWHERE, and neither does THE MEASURE PROPAGATE that joined this exception on 2026-08-20 (Ctrl+/ copy, Ctrl+Alt+/ paste — it writes the same field through the same store path, so it inherits the same both-views legality; the phase reset propagate is its own precedent for consulting the predicate nowhere). The propagate is WARP-COLUMN ONLY, which is NOT a home-view fact: it matches destinations by LABEL and only the warp column has labels (measure_clipboard.h carries that ruling); their one gate is READ-ONLY, which still refuses, because a measure IS serialized content — it round-trips through the sidecar as the ` //<measure>` suffix (data-model.md). It is unlike the first three in the other direction too: the cent step and the bracket wipe are narrow carve-outs inside the WARP column, while this one crosses the column boundary as well, which makes the phase column's measure double-click THAT COLUMN'S FIRST POINTER AUTHORING GESTURE — measure-scoped and nothing wider, recorded at the router arm (`run_marker_click_act`, input_pointer.cpp) so no future gesture inherits the precedent by analogy. The PAYLOAD editor's own gating was untouched by this exception: it stayed warp + source through both of its open routes until the fifth exception admitted it in target view too, and the measure editor is a separate `text_editor::Kind` on the same machinery (marker-ui.md is authoritative for the surface).

(5) THE WARP STATUS/VALUE FAMILY IN W+TARGET (architect 2026-08-24; landed 2026-08-25). He asked why `Ctrl+D`, `Ctrl+N`, `Delete` and the flag editor are blocked in W+target, was told the answer is the RIPPLE — the target map reshapes under the pointer — and ruled: "add the ones we can, and omit the ones we must omit". What the ruling turns on is the binding's own rationale, quoted at the head of this file: it is about PLACEMENT, and it says nothing about a status, an existence or a value edit. Those had been swept into the blanket refusal because they sat beside placement in the same dispatch, not because the argument reached them. FOUR MEMBERS, all in the WARP column: **`Ctrl+D`** (`GuiWarpMarkersOps::toggle_disabled` — the warp arm alone; the phase-reset arm's home IS target and keeps the gate), **`Ctrl+N`** (`toggle_inherits`), **`Delete`** (`delete_selected_marker`, again the warp arm alone) and **THE FLAG EDITOR'S PAYLOAD SURFACE** through both of its open routes, bare `Return`/`KpEnter` and the flag double-click — tempo, `label_def`/`label_ref`, per-marker scale, the disabled `#` and the iter-bracket grammar, which rides the editor and therefore rides this exception (see the third exception's superseded sentence above). This is the WIDEST of the five inside the warp column and the first one that admits a whole EDITOR off home.

THE RIPPLE IS ANSWERED WITH THE CENT STEP'S OWN CONTRACT, one shape for all four, in each op's tail when `active_audio_view == 'T'` (the flag-editor commit adds one term its siblings do not need — `canonical_changed`, since an ITER-BRACKET-ONLY commit writes session-only fields that are no map input at all): (a) `viewport.kick_waveform_sync()`, the synchronous re-warp, so displayed == live at the command boundary and no divergence window is left for the displayed-basis gestures to ride out — the four JOIN the target-view re-warp inventory owned by `Viewport::kick_waveform_sync`'s declaration; (b) the playhead RE-LAND through `Viewport::reseat_playhead_to` and never through a movement owner, because a marker's IMAGE moving under a resting cursor is a TRANSLATION and not a movement, so the trim region overlay stands (the rule at `clear_region_highlight`); (c) `target_render.trigger()` as before. THE THREE THAT KEEP A FOCUS re-land on its post-change image — the flag-editor commit being the worked case, a renamed `label_def` repricing references EARLIER in the timeline and shifting the edited marker's own image with them, which is exactly the singleton cent step's own 2026-07-28 argument. THE DELETE KEEPS NO FOCUS (its selection clears), so its subject is THE PLAYHEAD'S OWN MUSICAL INSTANT: the cursor is inverted to a source frame BEFORE the write through `active_domain_to_source_frame` — the product's one inverse for a bare frame, the identity in source view — and re-landed forward after it. SOURCE VIEW NEEDS NOTHING, being the identity domain where no image moves; the contract is stated once at the head of `warpmarkers_ops.cpp` and the four tails point at it.

WHAT IS OMITTED, and it is the POSITIONAL FAMILY entire: the marker DROP (bare `s` and the empty-lane double-click), the flag DRAG, the bare Left/Right position nudge, and the `m` BPM OPEN — the last for a reason of its own rather than for placement, since it rewrites tempo through a derivation over a SPAN rather than editing one marker's value. Those four still consult the predicate and still refuse silently off home, so W+target still has NO POINTER AUTHORING GESTURE THAT MOVES ANYTHING: its pointer vocabulary there is the measure double-click (exception 4) and now the payload double-click, both of which open editors and neither of which moves a marker. Nothing changes in P+source. SHIFT+S IS NOT A SIXTH EXCEPTION (architect 2026-08-28) and the distinction is exact: the chord drops a phase reset FROM ANY VIEW, but it does not AUTHOR off home — it runs the two view chokepoints first (`switch_active_audio_view_to('T')` then `switch_active_markers_view_to('P')`) and only then calls the ONE lead-in drop body bare `s` calls, which by that point is executing in T+P under the predicate's ordinary answer. So the drop stays positional and stays home-view-only; what Shift+S adds is a trip, not a permission (`GuiInputHandler::drop_phase_reset_in_target_view`, input_handler.cpp, and the note at the predicate itself). NO BUTTON FACE MOVED with this ruling either: the bottom row's four marker verbs then greyed on READ-ONLY alone and never on the home view (the roster's no-blink policy), so a face that was lit for a refused act became lit for a live one. SINCE 2026-08-30 THE FACES FOLLOW THE SPLIT (the truthful-buttons ruling, "Any time a button would be a no-op, grey it"): Delete and Disable grey on an empty selection in both columns and OFF HOME IN THE P COLUMN — the arms' own refusal, `marker_selection_verb_actionable`, which composes this predicate — while the DROP stays lit off home by the planner's call, its shift twin (the long press, glass's only road) dropping from any view; and the bare Left/Right nudge's off-home refusal greys the Left/Right buttons through `horizontal_arrow_step_actionable`, which composes this predicate too. Both compositions are counted in the predicate's own call-site inventory.

THE MEASURE PROPAGATE (architect 2026-08-20), the second member of the propagate family and the phase reset propagate's shape for the MEASURE field. **Ctrl+/** copies: from the SAME gate Ctrl+P takes — W-mode, a non-empty CONTIGUOUS run of selected warp markers — it replaces a session-only single-slot clipboard (`measure_clipboard.h`) with one entry per selected marker that PROPAGATES, membership being the family's one shared predicate `warp_marker_propagates` (labeled AND effectively enabled, phase_reset_clipboard.h). AN UNLABELED MARKER NEVER PROPAGATES A MEASURE and that is the ruling rather than an oversight — the label is what the paste matches on. A marker with NO measure is CAPTURED as a hole (`has_measure = false`), not skipped. **Ctrl+Alt+/** pastes onto a run anchored at exactly one selected warp marker, walking the destination's own members from the anchor in lockstep with the clipboard, matched BY LABEL, stopping WHOLE at the first divergence and reporting it in the family's one register (`format_domain_timestamp`, hoisted out of phase_reset_propagate.cpp's anonymous namespace that day so both propagates report identically). **THE OFFSET MODAL REPLACES THE CONFIRM PROMPT**: the paste opens the FIFTH dialog editor (`Kind::MeasureOffset`, the SEVENTH Kind) seeded with `0` and open-selected, and Enter applies with that many measures added — a bare Enter over the seed IS the old prompt's `y`. The musical purpose is the architect's: exposition→repeat pastes at 0, exposition→recapitulation at the flat integer offset, and a negative offset runs it the other way. THREE KINDS OF ENTRY AND ONLY THE FIRST SEES THE OFFSET — a DIRECT measure has the offset added to its WHOLE part and is re-spelled canonically through marker_measure.h's own writer (the fraction rides unchanged, so there is one spelling on disk); a `+` OFFSET measure is copied VERBATIM, being relative already; a captured HOLE CLEARS the destination's measure. A clear can ORPHAN a `+` chain on a successor outside the pasted run — that successor silently becomes unresolvable, which is the load-lenient/act-strict answer and is accepted, an unresolved `+` still committing, saving, loading and painting. **IF ANY DIRECT MEASURE + OFFSET WOULD LEAVE [1, 99999] THE COMMIT REFUSES WHOLE** — red flash, editor stays open, nothing written; the apply resolves every pair before writing any. THE REASON TRAVELS OUT WITH THE REFUSAL since 2026-08-30 (the strictness ruling): `apply_measure_paste` returns a `GuiOpRefusal` instead of a `bool`, so the sentence is composed where the fact is — `That offset would put a measure outside the allowed range` for the bracket, `Measure rejected: <reason>` for a clipboard entry that will not parse — and the commit raises it on a normal card beside the flash. The offset field's own grammar refusal (a spelling that is not one canonical signed integer) cards `Enter a whole number with no leading zeros`, and that card is its whole message, there being no stderr line here. Undo and damage are the MEASURE FIELD's profile rather than the phase paste's: one whole-store undo entry guarded by an equality scan, `recompute_dirty`, top-strip damage and, on a divergence, a notification card carrying the stop report — no render, no map rebuild, NO VIEW SWITCH (there is nowhere to land: a measure is editable wherever the flag paints), playhead and selection untouched, no overlay-hide owner reached.

The `t` S↔T toggle closes an open flag editor without committing, IN BOTH DIRECTIONS since 2026-08-24 (Ctrl+Tab cannot change the audio view — it is not per-tab state). THE UNDO RESTORE IS A THIRD CALLER OF THAT CHOKEPOINT SINCE 2026-08-28: an undo entry records the S/T view beside the tab and the column and the restore hands it back through the set-to spelling `switch_active_audio_view_to`, so a restore that changes the audio view takes this teardown, this domain translation and this target-view entry gate exactly as the key does (selection-model.md owns the three-tag rule and the restore's order). The close was the S→T half's alone while the editor was a source-only surface, on the argument that the flip would strand a live one where nothing could commit it; the editor is legal in both views now, so the surviving reason is the symmetric one — a flip RE-EXPRESSES the marker lane in the other domain, and the editor is the flag UNROLLED from its marker's painted column with the playhead landed there, so closing it is simpler than re-deriving it. The teardown is `exit_top_flag_edit_no_commit`, the exact Esc one, and it serves the measure session on the same terms — kind-agnostically, so a live MEASURE editor now closes on T->S too, where before 2026-08-24 it survived only because nothing closed it; the coder flagged that for a ruling and the architect ruled 2026-08-25: KEEP, on the same symmetry reasoning as the fifth exception's payload editor. Consequences harvested as dead code (ac0ae2c, each removal proof-carried): the nudge refuse-vs-clamp wall-policy split (one regime per column now), and the `view=='T'` synchronous re-warp tails at all warp PLACEMENT sites — the surviving target-view re-warp sites, RE-DERIVED 2026-07-29 when the tempo-image step and the tempo drag's per-cent-step live kick both died with contortion ruling 8, are the Up/Down cent step (both arms), the settings engine-scale commit, undo/redo, the load-in-place, and since 2026-08-24 the fifth exception's four (the authoritative caller inventory: viewport.h's `kick_waveform_sync` declaration). What the harvest's own sentence covered is PLACEMENT sites, which is what it still covers: the drop, the marker drag and the position nudge. EVERY ONE OF THEM IS A DISCRETE ONE-SHOT PER COMMAND now — the tempo drag was the only site that re-warped per pointer frame, so no map edit runs at gesture cadence any more. NOT harvested by ruling: the marker/trim drag dispatch/publication freeze (trim and phase drags still consume it, both freezing the displayed paint basis for the gesture's whole duration; the third gesture on that gate, the tempo drag, sat there for the opposite reason and is gone), the displayed-basis mechanics, and `adjust_tempo_cents`' projection-aware freeze seeding.
