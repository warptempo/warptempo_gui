# The Home-View Binding and the Tempo Cent Step

Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md in the 2026-07-25 slim-down (project feature-complete). This file is now the authoritative home for this detail; CLAUDE.md carries only a digest and a pointer here. Keep it current with landed rulings.

### The Home-View Binding (architect 2026-07-22)
Each marker column AUTHORS in its HOME audio view only — warp markers in SOURCE view, phase resets in TARGET view — via the ONE predicate `active_column_authoring_allowed` (app_state.h) consulted beside the read-only-tab checks at the dispatch chokepoints (drag arm, nudges, the bare `s` drop, Delete, Ctrl+D, Ctrl+N, the flag-editor open routes, the `i` and `m` mode toggles). Off home a column is display/navigation-only: selection, hover, Tab cycling, `c`, the marker-click land, readouts, and red flags all live; every store mutation refuses SILENTLY, navigation-class, exactly the read-only convention (read-only refusals compose and outrank). Rationale: warp placement edits in target view mutate the map the view is displayed in (imprecise by construction — this killed the viewport-wall clamp bug class undiagnosed), and phase resets are inaudible in source view. Exactly TWO ruled exceptions (the same-day 2026-07-24 "third exception" — a both-views warp POSITION nudge — was RE-RULED AWAY in the second pass as a misreading: W+target collapses to ONE MOTION, stretch/squish the waveform, and no warp position authoring exists there; its scaffolding was harvested at 79b73d3, the ac0ae2c precedent).

(1) THE BARE UP/DOWN TEMPO CENT STEP in W+target — SINGLETON AND GROUP, and since 2026-07-29 the WHOLE TEMPO SURFACE. It was one of THREE flavors of the one motion until contortion ruling 8 deleted the other two wholesale: the pointer TEMPO DRAG (the Ableton-style horizontal flag drag that rewrote the group predecessor's `tempo_cents` live per cent step) and its keyboard twin the bare Left/Right TEMPO-IMAGE STEP (`step_tempo_image`). The architect's ruling: "the tempo drag was an Ableton-parity nicety; the keyboard step is just as good", and then the image step went with it — so tempo is authored by Up/Down alone, in both ruled contexts, W+target included. GONE WITH THEM, recorded so none of it is re-proposed: `TempoDragState` and its whole pointer plumbing, `PendingTempoDrag` (and the press DEFERRAL that surface carried), `tempo_drag_predecessor`'s eligibility walk with all six refusal legs, `TempoGroupSeed` / `seed_tempo_group_participants` with the deduped participant set and its `walled` pin, the GROUP LABEL WALL that guaranteed the objective's monotonicity, `solve_tempo_group_delta`'s two-path solve (closed-form singleton / exact monotone bisection over hypothetical map builds), the image step's MINIMUM-STEP rule on the painter-quantized spp, and `GestureKind::TempoImageStep`. Two consequences worth naming: bare Left/Right in W+target WITH a selection is now a CONSUMED REFUSAL (no fallback, by the standing rule that a refused marker-lane press is a consumed no-op; with NO selection it stays the waveform-lane playhead step, unchanged), and W+TARGET HAS NO POINTER AUTHORING GESTURE AT ALL — a plain flag press there selects and lands the playhead but arms nothing, exactly like P+source. One further residue rests inert rather than being deleted: the frozen parser's `quiet` resolve parameter (and the GUI shim that forwards it) went caller-less with the bisection's hypothetical builds, and `MarkerForRender::owner_idx` — written by the parser, read only by the drag's forward-label coupling guard — went the same way; both live under the PERMANENT HARD FREEZE, so removing them needs explicit architect approval and the GUI shim carries an honest caller-less head instead.

THE STEP ITSELF. It stays reachable in target view (that is the exception) but is OWNER-ONLY there — a pass or label ref refuses silently, no freeze conversion (source view keeps the pass/ref-to-owner freeze unchanged) — and a COINCIDENT-COLLAPSED owner refuses too (render-inert: the resolver replaces every exact-frame run of 2+ effectively-enabled markers with one synthetic plain 1.00 owner, so the write would move nothing; tested via `warp_red_flag_set_cached`, and for a payload-owner red ⇔ collapsed, the bdf4336 argument — the stack is fixed at the source in warp view). THE GROUP ARM (architect 2026-07-23, post-labwc — "bump the speed of a group at once"; `adjust_tempo_cents_group`, warpmarkers_ops.cpp) is MAXIMALLY STRICT and all-or-nothing — an ineligible member is "the wall being hit before it starts to move", never skipped: every selected marker's OWN tempo steps one cent, and the VIEW-INDEPENDENT wall set — pass, ref, DISABLED, coincident-collapsed (`warp_red_flag_set_cached`), or already at the bracket edge in the step direction — refuses the WHOLE press silently (no per-member skip; the source-view pass/ref→owner freeze conversion stays SINGLETON-ONLY, and the singleton keeps its per-view behavior bit-for-bit incl. the target-only collapsed refusal and constructive clamp). One coalescing-eligible undo entry per press with touched hints (touched_snapshot == touched_live, no reorder — positions never move), and the target-view tail re-lands the playhead on the FOCUSED marker's post-step image (an upstream member's change moves downstream images). THE SINGLETON RE-LANDS TOO SINCE 2026-07-28, closing what had been a recorded accepted gap: its own image is fixed by construction in the ordinary case — a marker's tempo shapes only the segment AFTER it — but a LABEL DEFINITION reprices every reference to it, including references EARLIER in the timeline, whose spans then change duration and shift everything downstream, the stepped marker's own image included. Under the marker-lane-owns-the-playhead rule that is no longer cosmetic: the focused flag IS the playhead, so an image moving out from under it makes the flag lie. The re-land costs nothing when the image did not move (the playhead is written the value it already holds), and both arms gate on target view — source is the identity domain, where no image moves at all. The GROUP's reach is what differs from the singleton's, not the rule. These two re-lands are now the ONLY map-change re-lands in the product: the settings engine-commit's and the settings-only undo arm's twins died with contortion ruling 6, which has both CLEAR the selection instead, leaving no focus to re-land.

THE GROUP STEP'S REGION TAIL. An ACTIVE SelectionExtent region FOLLOWS the moved images, and THE TEMPO SURFACE NO LONGER BRANCHES ON PROVENANCE AT ALL (architect 2026-07-29, contortion ruling 2): the step re-derives the extent via `set_region_to_selection_extent` UNCONDITIONALLY in its target-view tail, beside the playhead re-land — because a region resting through a tempo gesture is that selection's EXTENT by construction (TrimWindow-under-a-selection is unreachable now that every trim setter deselects, and Free rests only beside an empty selection), which made the captured booleans redundant (they always resolved true for a 2+ selection, and the extent owner self-returns at <=1) and took the whole capture-before-kick pattern with them. The unconditional re-derive is also why the kick validator's domain-shrink clear cannot strand this gesture's group (the re-derive runs AFTER the kick). The TrimWindow re-sync arms were DELETED at every tempo site, the singleton step's #16 re-sync included (its reach chain — click a marker, click the chip row, Up/Down — is foreclosed at the chip click's deselect), and with them the COINCIDENT-arm collapse story: `TempoDragState::grab_trim_highlight`, the ERROR-state / no-mid-gesture-carve-out ruling, and the b44ad2e verbatim `pre_drag_region` restore are all gone (the coincident arm itself is deleted — a coincident window now rests ACTIVE at one column; see region-scrub-esc.md). Since contortion ruling 9 the group cent step is the LAST member of the extent-MAINTAINER class: the position movers (the group nudge, the group marker drag) left it when groups stopped being movable, and the tempo drag and image step left it with ruling 8.

(2) The phase-reset PROPAGATE is a warp-view gesture (one or more selected warp markers under the section rule, like the bpm command — see below) available in BOTH audio views — it authors phase resets, never warp markers — and it is the one action that STARTS in source view and ENDS in target view: every paste tail lands in target view through the `t` toggle chokepoint (`land_paste_in_target_view`, wired via a GuiInputHandler friend + back-pointer like the settings editor), switches to P view, and SELECTS exactly the pasted resets — the FIRST one focused and landed (e5a37e5, matching the programmatic earliest-focus rule) with the created set's EXTENT REGION set (b44ad2e — a mass-marker event reads like undo/redo's group restore) — replacing the old undo/redo inspection round-trip; a paste that materializes nothing instead does NOTHING AT ALL to the selection or the playhead (the no-created-set arm, general to both paste kinds, reduced to an empty arm by contortion ruling 1, 2026-07-29: the tail's column swap now CLEARS the selection and nothing restores one, so there is no group to collapse and no focus to land on — the cursor is the playhead, resting where the S→T re-express put the paste anchor's image, and a paste that did nothing changes nothing). Its route through the `t` chokepoint is also why the toggle's own spanless-group outcome (see selection-model.md's retirement paragraph) never surfaces here: the column swap immediately after clears the selection, and the tail then writes its own.

The `t` S→T toggle closes an open flag editor without committing (the editor is a source-only surface; Ctrl+Tab cannot change the audio view — it is not per-tab state). Consequences harvested as dead code (ac0ae2c, each removal proof-carried): the nudge refuse-vs-clamp wall-policy split (one regime per column now), and the `view=='T'` synchronous re-warp tails at all warp PLACEMENT sites — the surviving target-view re-warp sites, RE-DERIVED 2026-07-29 when the tempo-image step and the tempo drag's per-cent-step live kick both died with contortion ruling 8, are the Up/Down cent step (both arms), the settings engine-scale commit, undo/redo, and adopt (the authoritative caller inventory: viewport.h's `kick_waveform_sync` declaration). EVERY ONE OF THEM IS A DISCRETE ONE-SHOT PER COMMAND now — the tempo drag was the only site that re-warped per pointer frame, so no map edit runs at gesture cadence any more. NOT harvested by ruling: the marker/trim drag dispatch/publication freeze (trim and phase drags still consume it, both freezing the displayed paint basis for the gesture's whole duration; the third gesture on that gate, the tempo drag, sat there for the opposite reason and is gone), the displayed-basis mechanics, and `adjust_tempo_cents`' projection-aware freeze seeding.
