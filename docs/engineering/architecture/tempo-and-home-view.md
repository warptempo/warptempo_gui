### The Home-View Binding (architect 2026-07-22)
THE MAGNIFICATION LEVEL MARKERS COLUMN (M, source view only since 2026-09-16; target view only for its first day, 2026-09-15) AUTHORS IN SOURCE VIEW, THE ONLY VIEW IT EXISTS IN: `active_column_authoring_allowed` answers per column — W in source view, P always, M always — and the M arm takes NO audio-view term because the column's own existence already is one (the T switch lands the column on W, the column writer refuses M off source, and the two hold that invariant between them). So the whole positional family is legal there: the `Ctrl+Shift+S` crossing and bare `s`, the flag's horizontal drag, the empty-lane create and the Left/Right nudge, each through that column's own body in `GuiMagnificationLevelMarkersOps`. The tempo step's column gate stays the warp column's — bare Up/Down on M steps the LEVEL DIGIT through its own step body instead (marker-ui.md carries the column's whole vocabulary).

THE COLUMN'S HOME FLIPPED FROM TARGET TO SOURCE (architect 2026-09-16, closing the day's session; a granted parser touch at the settings validator, recorded there). THE PRINCIPLE: at working zoom the waveform is a PLACEMENT INSTRUMENT, coarser it is a MAP. Magnification exists for horizontal placement of warp markers in quiet passages. The M column is a picture boundary, not a timing event; it belongs beside the warp markers' own authoring view — SOURCE view — and the workflow order is: the warp markers first, then magnification, then the phase resets roughly ballpark, then T+W for fine tuning (architect 2026-09-17, swapping the first two; the 2026-09-16 order put magnification first, bare `1` S+M). THE DIGITS ARE THE WORKFLOW ORDER: bare `1` is S+W, `2` is S+M, `3` is T+P, `4` is T+W, and the view bar reads S+W · S+M · T+P · T+W left to right (`RedesignButton::ViewSW` its first button, `ViewSM` its second; kdenlive-redesign.md). It was a MIRROR, not a redesign: every chokepoint, order argument and shape of the 2026-09-15 form stands with the letter changed — `switch_active_markers_view_to` refuses `M` outside SOURCE view, `switch_active_audio_view_to` lands S+M on W before it leaves for TARGET view (past the target entry's refusal, so a refused entry moves no column and S+M stays whole), `Ctrl+Shift+S` crosses S then M, the two M pastes land in S+M, the settings editor's typed `M` crosses to source first, the undo restore of an `M` entry (audio tag `S` by construction) takes that view ahead of its column, the loader's forced-S landing of M on W is deleted as dead, bare `i` on M keeps its refusal ahead of the crossing (grid iterations is target-view only, so the lit lamp and the M column are exclusive by two rules now), and `active_markers_view=M` beside `active_audio_view=T` is load-fatal in both products with no migration for a file saved under the one-day rule. Where the column's bodies argued that the cursor is always a mapped domain value, it is now the source frame itself and the inverse is the identity; the unconditional conversions are kept, cheap and symmetric with the siblings' shape.
