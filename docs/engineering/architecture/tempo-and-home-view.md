### The Home-View Binding (architect 2026-07-22)
Each marker column AUTHORS in its HOME audio view only — warp markers in SOURCE view, phase resets in TARGET view — via the ONE predicate `active_column_authoring_allowed` (app_state.h) consulted beside the read-only-tab checks at the dispatch chokepoints (drag arm, nudges, `s`/Alt+S drops, Delete, Ctrl+D, Ctrl+N, the flag-editor open routes, the `i` and `m` mode toggles). Off home a column is display/navigation-only: selection, hover, Tab cycling, `c`, the marker-click land, readouts, and red flags all live; every store mutation refuses SILENTLY, navigation-class, exactly the read-only convention (read-only refusals compose and outrank). Rationale: warp placement edits in target view mutate the map the view is displayed in (imprecise by construction — this killed the viewport-wall clamp bug class undiagnosed), and phase resets are inaudible in source view. Exactly TWO ruled exceptions (the same-day 2026-07-24 "third exception" — a both-views warp POSITION nudge — was RE-RULED AWAY in the second pass as a misreading: W+target collapses to ONE MOTION, stretch/squish the waveform, and no warp position authoring exists there; its scaffolding was harvested at 79b73d3, the ac0ae2c precedent). (1) THE TEMPO FAMILY in W+target — three flavors of the one motion: the Alt+Up/Down tempo step, the tempo drag, and the Alt+Left/Right TEMPO-IMAGE STEP (`step_tempo_image`, the drag's keyboard twin: the FOCUSED marker's image steps one painted column per press WHERE THE CENT GRID ALLOWS, else by THE MINIMUM-STEP RULE — the nearest representable directional cent, whose travel can span several columns (~8 px on a 1 s span at working zoom; codex second-pass rounds 1-2: allowing the quantized-to-zero no-op was the inertness bug, and the adjacent-column target lives on the PAINTER-QUANTIZED spp that produced the column, never the logical spp — off-screen columns shear otherwise) — solved to predecessor cents through the drag's factored `solve_tempo_group_delta` — closed-form singleton / monotone bisection group — with the drag's eligibility via the shared `seed_tempo_group_participants` seeding, group walled mapping to whole-press silent refusal, its own coalescing GestureKind, the group tempo step's capture-before-kick region tail, one sync re-warp per press, post-kick land on the focused image, and the stem stamp — genuinely lateral, the predecessor's tempo shapes the segment BEFORE the focused marker). The Alt+Up/Down tempo step stays reachable in target view but is OWNER-ONLY there — a pass or label ref refuses silently, no freeze conversion (source view keeps the pass/ref-to-owner freeze unchanged); the same exception's POINTER HALF is the TEMPO DRAG (af08ff5, refined 3b822a8): in W+target a plain horizontal flag drag rewrites the GROUP PREDECESSOR's tempo_cents Ableton-style — COINCIDENT GROUPS ACT AS ONE (architect 2026-07-22): the predecessor is the nearest marker at a STRICTLY earlier frame (`tempo_drag_predecessor` walks past same-frame siblings, so any stack member drags the stack identically; the zero-span rejection is structural), and it must be an enabled payload-owner NOT itself coincident-collapsed (tested via `warp_red_flag_set_cached` — for a payload-owner, red ⇔ collapsed, the bdf4336 argument; so a marker FOLLOWING a stack never arms, the collapsed 1.00 is fixed in warp view). The KEYBOARD half shares the rule: the target-view Alt+Up/Down step refuses on a coincident-collapsed owner (render-inert, same red-set test — 3b822a8) on top of its owner-only payload gate — pointer x inverts through the map slope (`delta_tgt = delta_src / (tempo·scale)`, faster-is-shorter, derived from build_warp_frame_map) to the cents value placing the dragged marker's target image nearest the pointer, quantized to the cents grid, bracket-clamped constructively (span ≤ 0 pins to max tempo), COMMITTED LIVE per cent step with a synchronous re-warp (kick_waveform_sync — the tempo-step precedent, displayed == live at every step boundary; NOT the DragState overlay model — the store is the live proposal), one undo entry per drag pushed at gesture end iff net change (mouse drags stay coalesce-ineligible), Esc-cancel restores the grab tempo + SelectionSnapshot + the grab playhead, the playhead follow re-lands the playhead on the dragged marker's post-commit image each step, the arm rides `PendingTempoDrag` past the shared `kDragMovedThresholdPx` (an ineligible W+T flag press selects and lands the playhead but arms nothing), `target_render.trigger()` fires ONCE at gesture end, and the marker/trim dispatch/publication freeze includes the gesture (async jobs must not race the per-step sync renders; placement never changes, no reorder ever runs). THE GROUP TEMPO GESTURES (architect 2026-07-23, post-labwc — "bump the speed of a group at once"): with 2+ selected BOTH tempo surfaces go GROUP, MAXIMALLY STRICT and all-or-nothing — an ineligible member is "the wall being hit before it starts to move", never skipped. The STEP (`adjust_tempo_cents_group`, warpmarkers_ops.cpp): every selected marker's OWN tempo steps one cent; the VIEW-INDEPENDENT wall set — pass, ref, DISABLED, coincident-collapsed (warp_red_flag_set_cached), or already at the bracket edge in the step direction — refuses the WHOLE press silently (no per-member skip; the source-view pass/ref→owner freeze conversion stays SINGLETON-ONLY, and the singleton keeps its per-view behavior bit-for-bit incl. the target-only collapsed refusal and constructive clamp); one coalescing-eligible undo entry per press with touched hints (touched_snapshot == touched_live, no reorder), and the target-view tail re-lands the playhead on the FOCUSED marker's post-step image (an upstream member's change moves downstream images — the singleton keeps NO re-land, its own image fixed by construction, the label-coupling edge a recorded accepted gap there). (2) The phase-reset PROPAGATE is a warp-view gesture (one or more selected warp markers under the section rule, like the bpm command — see below) available in BOTH audio views — it authors phase resets, never warp markers — and it is the one action that STARTS in source view and ENDS in target view: every paste tail lands in target view through the `t` toggle chokepoint (`land_paste_in_target_view`, wired via a GuiInputHandler friend + back-pointer like the settings editor), switches to P view, and SELECTS exactly the pasted resets (last one focused), replacing the old undo/redo inspection round-trip.
