### Why it exists (design §0, his use cases)

THE CAR IS THE REASON. Parked, at night: open the player, see the project's renders, start one — without leaving the app and without Audacious (U1). Queue a variation series parked and audition it one after another while driving, WITHIN ONE FOLDER — *"in realistic use I would contain my work to one or the other folder anyway"* (U3). Play-scrub: tap a later point and the audio jumps there; grab the marker and drag, and the seek commits at the release (U4). Load a recipe deliberately from the player, with a confirmation (U6). The same auditioning on the LAPTOP, in-app rather than spawned (U5). And the bottom-strip modal STAYS the dialog machinery (U8): the history view's typed load-in-place rode it and *"untangling that will be complicated"*, so the overlay FED the existing modal instead of replacing it — until R22/R23 the same afternoon retired both typed prompts for the field-less picker, which is a modal owner of its own on that same machinery.

DELIBERATELY UNSERVED, and the list is the design's own: a waveform for the played render; loops (*"advancing is not looping"*); auto-advance ACROSS folders; a nested tree with connector lines; folder navigation BETWEEN projects; arbitrary paths; a recent-files list; the stick as the car route (Bluetooth is); the sunset score video; a real second window (tried, dropped — Wayland trouble); a centred floating modal box (tried, dropped — *"flat, no drop shadow, small, blocked the whole screen"*).

### The model (R1, R2, R4, revised by R16 / R17, then by R36 / R38)

THE PLAYER NAVIGATES `tmp/` THE REGULAR WAY — *"if we do it by folders in Open project, we may as well do it the regular way in the player too"*. **THE PLAYER LIVES INSIDE `tmp/` AND NEVER RISES ABOVE IT** (architect 2026-09-01), so `tmp/` IS THE ROOT: it lists its batch folders in the enumeration's own order, and a batch lists its cells in that same order — the order `'` walks, reused as the play order. HIS RATIONALE, recorded at the listing itself: THE DELIVERABLE IN `render/` IS A NAMING-FOR-SHARING CONVENIENCE OUTSIDE THE GUI'S WORKFLOW — the tablet's engine differs from the laptop's by ULPs, so it is never driven from the glass, and it carries no sidecars, so it cannot be loaded in place (R15) — while `tmp/`'s batch cells are what the workflow auditions on both hosts. There is no `..` row anywhere: **GOING UP IS A BUTTON** on the modal row (below), with `Backspace` as its key. A listing is built WHEN ITS FOLDER IS ENTERED and never kept fresh (R4): no cache, no watcher — a render that completes meanwhile shows on the next entry of that folder. `GuiRenderPlayer::rebuild_rows` is the one builder and `AppState::RenderPlayer::folder` is TWO PLACES — Root, which IS `tmp/`, and the Batch named by `batch_dir` — never a free path.

**`render/` LEFT THE LISTING** (architect 2026-09-01, superseding the whole paragraph that stood here). It read: *`render` IS A ONE-FILE FOLDER, AND THE LISTING IS WHAT MAKES IT ONE* (architect 2026-08-29 — *"player should only list a file if it matches the current title, and delete the rest also"*, closing the seam the week's review found: the player listed every wav in `render/` while Synchronize mirrors only the current `title=`'s wav and deletes the rest from the stick). `GuiRenderPlayer::deliverable_wav` was the one question — an `optional<path>`, the title's wav iff it was there as a regular file — and it PRUNED FIRST. THAT QUESTION IS DELETED WHOLE with the two-branch root, the `Folder::Deliverable` listing under it and the `..` row every non-root listing carried; `Folder` is two enumerators now. **THE PRUNE ITSELF STAYS AND HAS ONE TRIGGER**, THE DELIVERABLE'S PUBLISH — the single archival render's GUI-thread completion, on Success and on the deliverable arm alone, after `do_render` has written the wav AND its fingerprint (`prune_render_folder`, renders_dir.h, whose declaration carries the whole contract and the succession). A batch cell publishes into `tmp/`, a different folder, never pruned; `warptempo_cli` writes the same deliverable and gets no prune, the recorded asymmetry at the owner. WHAT THE DEFINITION LOSES IS PROMPTNESS, NOT REACH: a stale pair survives until the next deliverable publishes rather than until whichever trigger came first, and the Synchronize mirror keeps the same definition on the stick by deleting whatever does not match it — one definition either way (external_sync.h rule 1). **NEITHER THE PUBLISH ROAD NOR THE MIRROR IS TOUCHED**: `Ctrl+Alt+R` still writes `render/<title>.wav` and prunes at its completion, and Synchronize still ships that deliverable beside every `tmp/` batch folder. Only the PLAYER stops looking at `render/`.

**A CLICK ACTIVATES** (architect 2026-08-29, R39, superseding R16's *"I'm expecting to use click on the item and then press a button to play or to load or whatever"* of the day before): a single click or tap on a wav row PLAYS it from its start, on a folder row ENTERS it (going up is the modal row's Up button since 2026-09-01, no row navigating any more) — Plasma's single-click, which he runs, and GNOME's touch agree, and the same ruling governs the Open project picker's rows one section down. THE ACT RIDES THE MOTIONLESS LIFT: the press still only ARMS, because that same press may become the band's scroll drag and past the drag gate it IS the drag; the HIGHLIGHT moves onto the clicked row first and then the act runs, so what opens is what the band marks. THE HIGHLIGHT IS ONE FIELD (`folder_overlay.highlight_row`): the band AND the list's keyboard focus, one thing, and ENTER is the keyboard's own click on it while Up/Down walk it without opening anything. **THE DOUBLE-CLICK RETIRED** with the ruling — `DoubleClickSurface::FolderRow`, its motionless-release seed, its press-time consume and the listing rebuild's clear of that candidate are all deleted, no second meaning being left for a second press. There is no ctrl-click and no multi-selection here, ruled out with it.

**SPACE IS HIGHLIGHT-DRIVEN, AND THE TRANSPORT IS WHAT IS LEFT** (architect 2026-08-31, R6, NARROWING R40 — which had answered *play when idle plays the current track*, Audacious's own answer, and taken the highlight out of the button entirely on 2026-08-29). `play_button_act` — the ONE body the `Play` button, `Space` and the car's undivided PlayPause all reach — forks in four arms, in this order (the car's DIRECTION-NAMED Play and Pause take the second table alone, through `transport_toggle_act`; the car section below owns that split):

| The band's highlight | the `Play`/`Pause` button, Space, the car's PlayPause |
|---|---|
| a FOLDER row | OPEN IT — the row's own act, whatever the transport is doing (it was *a FOLDER row or `..`* until the `..` row retired on 2026-09-01) |
| a WAV that is NOT the transport's item | PLAY THAT ROW from its start, live or paused or idle alike |
| the transport's own item, an empty band, no highlight | the TRANSPORT'S fork below |

| Transport (`transport_toggle_act`: the arms above having passed, or a direction-named car command arriving straight here) | act |
|---|---|
| LIVE | PAUSE the item |
| PAUSED | RESUME the item |
| IDLE with an item | the item from ITS START, ALWAYS — the folder's end included since R7 — a seek while idle being a SILENT consumed no-op (`seek_to`'s own head, the one owner every road shares — and the scrub's press refuses at its own gate, earlier, because it must not arm the handle drag either), so `resume_frame` is always 0 at an idle rest and every rest the transport's own acts leave it in (a natural end, a fresh bind) plays from frame 0 |
| IDLE with no item | nothing, silently — `toggle_pause`'s own guard is the whole answer (`play_button_act`'s copy of that predicate, which carded from 2026-08-30, is deleted with the card) |

WHY THE NARROWING IS SAFE, and it is the same fact R40 was fixing from the other side: R40's bug was A BAND LEFT BEHIND THE TRANSPORT (R36's report below), and THE BAND FOLLOWS THE ITEM since R38 — at every item change the transport makes on its own — so a highlight standing anywhere else is one THE USER WALKED THERE, deliberately, and going there is what he asked for. The row arms run `open_row`, the row click's and Enter's own body, so the acts have ONE owner and the fork walks no listing of its own; `render_player_highlight_act_row` (app_state.h) is the one question *"which row would Space open"* — two arms since the `..` row left it, with THREE READERS that must not drift — this fork, the button's disabled face and its PAUSE GLYPH.

ENTER AND SPACE DIFFER IN EXACTLY ONE CASE, which is the whole residue of the two vocabularies: on THE TRANSPORT'S OWN ITEM with a session standing, `Enter` (the click act) restarts it from 0 while `Space` toggles it. Everywhere else the two agree exactly.

R17 IS STILL RETIRED — the car-stereo OK/Play convention it gave the button was *"the play button plays whatever is highlighted"* in ALL FOUR states, the highlighted item's own restart included; what R6 restores is the three arms that name somewhere ELSE to go, and the transport keeps its own item.

PAUSED AT FRAME ZERO IS PAUSED, NOT IDLE, and that is why the state is a field rather than a reading of the resume point (2026-08-28, the same day's second correction): the tick's no-device arm parks a wav that never sounded a frame — start a wav with the device away and it rests at frame 0, paused — and no reading of the resume point could tell that rest from a fresh bind's. WHAT THE FIELD DECIDES IS WHICH TRANSPORT ARM RUNS, and that is all it decides: with the band on that item (or nowhere) the next Play RESUMES it, as a paused transport, instead of taking the idle arm's "the item from its start" — the two happen to agree at frame 0 and disagree at every other rest a no-device pause can leave. THE HIGHLIGHT STILL LEADS (R6, 2026-08-31, superseding this paragraph's original conclusion, which was written when Space read no band at all and said the next Play *must resume that item rather than fall through to the highlight's row*): walk the band to another wav while that one rests and the next Play plays THE WALKED ROW — the deliberate walk R6 reads, the same answer live, paused and idle alike — and walk it to a folder and the next Play enters it. The field is not a claim on the fork's ORDER, only on the arm the transport takes once the highlight has passed. `resume_frame` is the resume POINT alone; a seek moves it and moves no state. THE WRITERS ARE THREE CLASSES: LIVE at the two plays (`play_wav`, `toggle_pause`'s resume arm), PAUSED at THE ONE STOP BODY'S PLAYER FORK (`stop_playback_if_playing` — every live transport that stops passes there, the pause, the no-device pause, the natural end and the close alike, and it is that value's one writer), and IDLE at the THREE acts that mean "resting at the start" — the natural end's own rest, `open()` and `close()` — each written after that fork returns, `open()`'s reset being the one that never meets it (it runs with the mode down). IT WAS FOUR until 2026-09-01: `GuiRenderPlayer::stop()` was the fourth and the only USER act among them, and it went with the row's Stop button, so THE IDLE CLASS STAYS AND NO PRESS PRODUCES IT — every idle rest is the player's own lifecycle now.

THE BUG THAT RULED IT, in his words: *"if I double-click a track to play it, then hit Next, playback advances to the next song but the highlight stays on the one I double-clicked. If I then hit the stop button (the Play/Pause button's live face), it actually triggers Play on the one that's highlighted, and then I can click it again to actually stop."* The live button is about what is SOUNDING, and the row the band happens to sit on has nothing to say about it.

THE TRANSPORT'S ITEM IS NOT THE HIGHLIGHT. It keeps playing while the listing is navigated elsewhere, its row wears the transport glyph, and HOME'S PREVIOUS-TRACK WINDOW / the two Shift+`Home`/Shift+`End` ENDS / AUTO-ADVANCE walk `item_folder` — THE WAV ROWS OF ITS FOLDER AS THEY WERE LISTED WHEN IT WAS PLAYED — never another folder and never a wrap (R2).

THE HIGHLIGHT FOLLOWS THE TRANSPORT'S ITEM (architect 2026-08-28, R38, superseding R17's *"Previous and Next never move the highlight"*): at every item change THE TRANSPORT MAKES ON ITS OWN — Home's previous-track window, the folder's ends, the auto-advance — the band moves onto the new item's row and scrolls it into view WHEN THAT ROW IS IN THE LISTING ON SCREEN; with the listing elsewhere (the user has walked into another folder) THE BAND STAYS WHERE IT IS. A user's own highlight moves are untouched — nothing drags the band back onto the item. Its ONE owner is `play_wav`, the one place the item changes, so the rule needs no membership list and the user's own plays get it for free (the row is already the highlight there) — AND THE SEAT THERE FIRES AT A CHANGE, not at every call: `play_wav` is re-entered on the item already resting there by the REPEAT ONE replay at every natural end, so an ungated seat would drag the band off the row a user walked to under a lit lamp, again at each replay and under whatever Load in place he pressed next. The gate is the incoming path against the item as it stood at the call's head; the replay itself keeps the full rebind (2026-08-29).

NOTHING LOOPS, WITH ONE SANCTIONED EXCEPTION — **REPEAT ONE** (architect 2026-08-28, R26; the row's lamp and bare `r`). It is a TWO-STATE toggle, off or repeat the ONE item: *"the user can just press play once the playlist finishes... repeat one is much more useful"*, which is why there is no repeat-all. While it stands the natural end REPLAYS THE ITEM FROM ITS START through the player's own play road — the same road a user's Play takes, so the item stays the transport's and the head unit is published at that edge as at any play. THE ARM IS TERMINAL, SOUNDED OR REFUSED: a replay that cannot decode (the file deleted or republished in another shape while it played) puts the decode vocabulary's own words on a notification card and leaves the transport resting on that item at its start — it does not fall through to the next wav. Repeat ONE means this wav and nothing else, failure included. The state is SESSION-ONLY (`AppState::RenderPlayer::repeat_one`, false at every `open()`, serialized nowhere), no media command maps to it and `publish_media_state` publishes no repeat mode. The exception is recorded at the rule's other statements — `render_player.h`'s model, `playback_lifecycle.h`'s bounded audition, CLAUDE.md's two digests and HELP.

AT THE FOLDER'S LAST WAV, with the lamp off, the transport stops with the item resting at its start — **AND THAT IS ALL IT MEANS**: the next Play replays that last file. **THE FOLDER-END RESTART IS RETIRED** (architect 2026-08-31, R7: *"we simplify — play on last file means play last file"*). From 2026-08-28 to that day the bit `AppState::RenderPlayer::ended_at_folder_end` said the transport was resting THERE and turned the next Play into *"start the folder's FIRST wav"* (R27, the car's Play at the end of a playlist); the bit, its ONE writer (the natural end's last-wav arm), its ONE reader (`play_button_act`'s idle arm) and its seven clears — every play, resume, seek, row open, open, close and STOP — are all DELETED, and nothing distinguishes that rest from any other idle rest at an item's start. THE CAR'S PLAY AT THE END OF A PLAYLIST replays the last track now, which is also the row the band is resting on (R38). What replaced it for a listener who wants the folder again is the pair the same ruling leans on: `Shift+Home` — or the left skip's shift-click or long press — is the folder's first wav in one press, and a click on any row plays it.

**STOP IS RETIRED WHOLE** (architect 2026-09-01, reversing R8's keep of the previous evening — *"it sounds like stop is actually useful"* — once the row's symmetry with the main window became the goal): **THE MODAL ROW IS THE MAIN WINDOW'S OWN TRANSPORT TRIPLE**, `Home` · Play/Pause · `End`, the same three acts on the same three keys as the roster's two skips and its play button. The button, the act body `GuiRenderPlayer::stop`, its bare `v` key, its face arm, its tooltip row and its `PlayerButtonAct` member are all DELETED — its three roads were all user acts and nothing else called it, so nothing had to be kept behind. What makes it superfluous is what the same two days built around it: `Home` already puts the playing file back at its beginning, and the highlight-driven Space (R6) starts any file from its start in one press.

IT WAS R36's (architect 2026-08-28): *"one button that's either play or pause, and the other one is stop. So if the user is playing a track and wants to go back to the beginning of that track, they can hit stop and then play again. This is different from the live transport, because there we have the scrub, and a pause wouldn't make sense — the scrub always returns to the playhead when not playing."* THE ACT WAS: the transport to IDLE with the item resting AT ITS START — the resume point cleared to 0, the item still the transport's — over the one stop body where it was sounding, a silent consumed no-op with no item and on an item already resting there.

**WHAT SURVIVES IT.** The IDLE STATE stays with its three remaining producers (`open()`, the natural end, `close()`) and no user road into it. THE ONE STOP BODY (`GuiPlaybackLifecycle::stop_playback_if_playing`) is untouched — every pause, natural end, close and rebind still passes through it. THE PAUSE GLYPH stays on the two-faced button: a player's transport parks a resumable rest whatever the row's shape, and `MediaPlaybackStop` keeps its one reader in the ROSTER's own play/stop button. Bare `v` is unbound in the mode and falls to its silent catch-all. AND THE HEAD UNIT'S STOP keeps its meaning by composition — the car table below.

### The surface (R3, R5, R9, R24, R31–R33, R35)

THE OVERLAY SITS WHERE THE ON-SCREEN KEYBOARD SITS — *"the overlay sits in the on-screen keyboard's place above the bottom strip, replacing the keyboard there"* (R3), *"the way the keyboard helps the bottom strip"*. THE BAND IS THE SLOT'S AND THE ROW IS THE BUTTON'S (R31/R33, the second pass the same day; the panel's first hours took both from the keyboard). The band takes the slot's x, its width and its BOTTOM EDGE — the bottom row's own lane, lifted, through the one owner `keyboard_slot_band` (app_state.h) that the keyboard's own `surface_rect` reads too — and ITS HEIGHT IS FIXED AT THE CEILING: from THE MENU ROW'S FOOT (`keyboard_slot_max_height_px`, row 1's whole lane through the vertical stack's one geometry owner, margin included so the row itself is never covered) down to the bottom row, EVERY TIME IT STANDS, whatever the listing's length — *"media player should take up the whole window up to (but not including) the first row (File, etc)"* (architect 2026-09-02) at *"a fixed height"* (R35, 2026-08-28, that evening's plastic-and-glass pass). SO THE PANEL IS THE WINDOW UNDER ROW 1: the tab row, the icon row, the overview strip, the trim bar, the ruler, the marker lane and the whole waveform stand under it while a content is up. A SHORT LISTING LEAVES THE REST OF THE BAND AS GROUND and a long one scrolls. R35 retired the growing half of R33 (*"from the bottom strip up to the middle of the waveform... it grows with its content up to that cap, then scrolls"* — which had itself replaced a fixed four-row band that read too short against his dummy folders), and THAT half of the ruling stands: only WHERE the ceiling sits has moved, from R33's waveform midpoint (*"we should automatically make the height from the midpoint of the waveform down so that it's not a fluid height"*) to row 1's foot. The content's own height is the SCROLL CLAMP's input and nothing else's. BOTH CONTENTS TAKE THE NEW BAND — the panel has ONE rect and the owner tag forks the open act alone — so the Open project picker grew with the player. THE ROW IS EXACTLY THE ICON ROW'S BUTTON — the same 32 px box, the same 2 px gap between boxes, the same corner radius and the same 22 px glyph seated at the box's own `(32-22)/2` inset, every number read from render.h's icon-button block (*"we've gone for the button analogy"*; *"the buttons are good enough size for my finger"*) — and A NAME TOO LONG FOR THE LINE RUNS OFF THE EDGE, no wrap and no ellipsis (*"project and file names will be short"*), the painter clipping it to the row. The panel's one authored number of its own is a 2 px outer inset, pcmanfm-qt's 1 px item margin grown with the taller rows. THE BAND WEARS A 1 px TOP BORDER (architect 2026-08-29): the same `kRedesignTabLine` line its BOTTOM edge already wears, that edge being row 8's own border-top, so the panel's two edges match and it reads as a framed block instead of a ground running up into the waveform. It is READ from the bottom row's accessor (`folder_overlay::border_h_px`, one chrome line across four lanes now) and painted INSIDE the band's rect as its first row, so the surface rect, the damage rect and the hit rect are all unchanged — the line comes out of the CONTENT's room through `folder_overlay::content_rect`, which the rows, the scroll ceiling and the keep-visible walk read in the surface rect's place; the band's height being the ceiling's whole extent, there was no room outside it to take. (The band opened 2026-08-28 with no line at that edge, the keyboard's own rule — the two lanes reading as one block on a shared ground — which this supersedes.) THE COLOURS ARE THE FILE MANAGER'S (R32, kdenlive's project bin and pcmanfm-qt's compact view, which agree): the band's ground is `kModalFieldGround` #141618 and NOTHING ALTERNATES; a resting row paints no fill at all; HOVER is `kFolderRowHover` (the accent at the 0.3 click mix over that ground — the derivation ships, and it reproduces the architect's #204357 within one 8-bit step on the green) under a 1 px `kFolderRowHoverOutline` #3694c5 frame; the HIGHLIGHT is `kRedesignAccent` #3daee9 — `kRedesignAccentInactive` #1b4155 WHILE THE WINDOW IS UNFOCUSED (architect 2026-09-02: *"breeze blue should change to #1b4155 when window loses focus (inactive color, the scrub already does this — not sure if it's the same color but it's at least similar)"*; it is NOT the scrub's own measured #1d3847, two Breeze roles that read alike staying two constants under the numerically-close-is-not-the-same rule), through the one fork `accent_for_focus` that the row's fill, the row's list-focused OUTLINE and the modal row's ACTIVE-FOCUS outline all read; HOVERED + HIGHLIGHTED is `kFolderRowHoverSelected` #44bfff; the ink is `kRedesignLabel` white in every face, and a live press arm wears the highlight's own fill. THE HOVER FACES DO NOT SWAP — a hover is a promise the pointer can act, and a pointer over an unfocused window still can — so the panel and the modal row are the FOURTH reader class of `AppState::window_activated` after rows 1 and 2 (the ground) and the scrub (its played groove), and the activation hook damages the band and the modal row for the edge. THE TWO NEVER BOTH STAND, STRUCTURALLY: the overlay's owners — the player and the picker — are modes and not editors, each opener refuses under every editor, each router consumes every editor opener and each veil consumes every pointer press that could raise one, so the keyboard's editor term is false whenever the band stands. The overdraw is the mode's cost, paid only while the panel stands. The as-painted bit is the SLOT'S (`AppState::keyboard_slot_painted_standing`, written only by `GuiPaintHandler::paint_keyboard_slot` on a rect that fully covers the STANDING TENANT'S band — the slot's tallest where neither stands, that being the frame the hide's own damage draws), not either tenant's. UNLIKE THE KEYBOARD IT SCROLLS: `folder_overlay.scroll_px` is THE PRODUCT'S FIRST SCROLL OFFSET — a pixel offset clamped through the one owner `folder_overlay::clamp_scroll`, reset to 0 at every rebuild, stepped ONE ROW PER PLAIN WHEEL DETENT (wheel context 4, live over the band alone and swallowed everywhere else; EVERY MODIFIED WHEEL IS A CONSUMED NO-OP over the band since 2026-08-29, strict modifier validation's own rule — the band binds one wheel act, so the other three chords bind nothing and must not reach the surfaces under the veil either — and a scroll RE-DERIVES the hovered row at the pointer's position, the rows having slid under a stationary pointer), carried by the band's own drag on glass, and moved by `scroll_row_into_view` so a walked highlight stays visible.
