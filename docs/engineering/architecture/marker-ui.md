### Marker Gestures
A marker is ONE pointer item WITH ONE SURFACE: its painted flag box (the painter's stash; one BACKWARD walk, last painted topmost). THE STEMS ARE POINTER-INERT IN ALL CONTEXTS (architect 2026-08-12, the seventh glass ruling — "I definitely don't want to be concerned about accidentally touching a marker"): they still PAINT exactly as row 5 shipped them (class-colored, always on, disabled-no-stem) but no press resolves one, so a plain upper-half waveform press over a stem column is the ordinary placement press, a drag can never accidentally grab a marker, and the waveform is purely region / playhead / pan / zoom — marker work happens in the flag lane. THE SUCCESSION, recorded honestly: the stem was the marker's SECOND surface from 2026-08-01 (plain-exact from the start — the shift and ctrl clicks bound to the flag alone because both modifiers mean a waveform GESTURE at the stem's pixels, a rule the 2026-08-06 symmetry ruling made universal by deleting the `h` view's one-day stem-based multi-select); the stems question stayed OPEN through the touch arc and was answered "stems stay" while the scrollbar plan lived; it CLOSED THE OTHER WAY at this ruling — the scrollbar plan is dead, and the waveform-height clamp (kdenlive-redesign.md's vertical rule) keeps the flag lane in easy reach on every display, which is what the removal was waiting for. `hit_test_marker_stem` and `kMarkerStemGrabPx` are DELETED (the record at their retired site, app_state.h); `marker_stems` survives as the stem painter's stash alone. A plain click ON THE FLAG selects and LANDS THE PLAYHEAD (selection-model.md owns the re-coupling, shift/ctrl semantics, and the never-parked rule); the flag is the drag handle — the press single-selects and arms a `PendingMarkerDrag` (first-class armed gesture: `any_pointer_gesture_active`, the key gate, and the force-end finalizer `finalize_active_drags` all include it), and crossing the one generic `kDragMovedThresholdPx` (8px, Chebyshev, latched) begins `marker_drag.begin_drag(marker, press_x)` — anchored at the PRESS x so the first apply folds the whole catch-up. Shift never arms; read-only selects but never arms. Mid-drag the overlay proposes positions through the displayed map with the fractional time rounded BEFORE the map lookup (`apply_drag_motion` maps `nearbyint(new_t)` — the row-5 refinement fix); the release commits through the pixel-anchored column snap (zoom-viewport-strip.md). POINTER GESTURES HAVE NO CANCEL (rulings 3+4): Esc mid-gesture is a consumed no-op, any end commits, undo is the mitigation.

THE EMPTY MARKER-LANE STRETCHES ARE THE NAVIGATION SURFACE'S LANE MEMBER (architect 2026-08-12, the eighth glass ruling — the lane is "essentially an extension of the upper half", so it takes the upper half's whole vocabulary; the 2026-07-23 press-time parity placement is superseded): PLAIN press = the PENDING CLICK / GRAB-PAN (nothing at press; the MOTIONLESS release runs the deferred placement — deselect + playhead + region dissolve, no stop call, a live session reseeking — AND seeds the EmptyLane double-click candidate; a crossed drag is the captured pan); SHIFT press = the REGION FORMER (the lane joined its y-gate); CTRL press = the strip-drag ZOOM (the grown surface); plain DOUBLE-CLICK = CREATE the marker at the clicked position, SELECT it and LAND the playhead on it (the architect's exact words) — the AUGMENTED drop (warp copy-previous / phase reset N/2 lead-in), the only drop bare `s` performs too, through the standing `_at_playhead` chokepoints (`DoubleClickSurface::EmptyLane`, position-keyed; consume-before-arm, so the second press never becomes a pan) — the SELECT and the LAND are the drop's own acts (drop_marker / drop_phase_reset_at_position single-select what they create and re-seat the playhead), so ONE body serves key and click with no divergence, and home-view + read-only refuse silently. The FLAG BOXES carve out of all of it — flag clicks are lane vocabulary, select-at-press, unchanged.

### The Playhead Beside the Markers
The resting cursor's form is the MARKER-LANE HEAD (19×12 aliased — it sat on the ruler's bottom rows and moved into the marker lane at the row-5 live test, architect 2026-08-01; the occlusion rationale and the record at the head painter, paint_handler.cpp) + the full-height kPlayheadStem line — always painted, ONE form (the region simplification, 2026-07-30; selection-model.md). THE STEM SUPPRESSION (row-5 refinement, reinstating 035e669's model on the new anatomy): the playhead's stem segment does not paint while exactly coincident with any painted marker stem — the compare is the land formula's own EXACT int64 against the marker_stems stash (or the drag-ride index), never pixels — while the head keeps painting under the flags. Z-order, the marker-and-cursor slice of it — THE AUTHORITATIVE FULL SEQUENCE IS THE PAINT-ORDER BLOCK IN `on_redraw` (paint_handler.cpp) and this states only what these forms do inside it: the cursor's WAVEFORM stem → marker stems → THE SCANNER (its own pass, paint_scanner, since 2026-08-01 — the moving playback line paints OVER the stems and over the cursor where they meet, so it crosses a marker instead of blinking out behind it; the resting CURSOR stays under the stems and flags, the hidden-by-marker model being about a cursor COINCIDENT with a marker, not a sweep) → the ruler pass, which carries its own ticks AND the cursor's head, its tip standing on the waveform boundary where the waveform stem begins so the two meet with no lane pixel between them (the head lands after the stems, in a band they never touch, and the ticks stay under it) → the flag blit over all of them. Damage: the flag-cache rebuild damages STRIP + WAVEFORM (it produces marker_stems, a waveform-pixel input — the delay-and-sync doctrine: stash, staged basis, and pixels advance in the same frame).

### The Flag Editor and the Editor Lifecycle
ENTER (bare Return/KpEnter, no editor open) opens the flag editor on the FOCUSED marker (W view only — phase resets have no per-flag editor; read-only refuses at the allowlist); a plain DOUBLE-CLICK on a warp flag opens the SAME editor on that marker (the first click single-selected it and armed no drag; P view and read-only refuse silently, matching Enter). The editor is the FLAG UNROLLED (kdenlive-redesign.md row 5 checkpoint C): the box grows from the flag's own painted column to the FULL text width, wearing the marker's resolved face — opening changes the flag's SIZE and nothing else; invalid commits red-flash in the lane's red pair; `text_editor::State::view_offset_px` gives this one surface minimal-travel horizontal scroll; click-to-byte is nearest-boundary ties-low through the ONE owner `editor_byte_index_at` on the shaped run's `byte_offsets_px`. THE LIFECYCLE IS GUARD-FREE (2026-07-22): while the editor is open, a click in its rendered text places the caret; ANY other click CLOSES it without committing (exactly Esc's teardown, `exit_top_flag_edit_no_commit`; Enter stays the sole commit route, the close placed ahead of every band claim so it is unconditional) — a double-click on the open editor's own marker is close-then-reopen, which IS the double-click-opens rule, no special case. EVERY editor opens with its SEEDED content fully selected (open-selected, one universal rule at the `enter_text_edit` chokepoint; the blank-seeded settings/load editors degenerate to caret 0). Modality — which keys reach an open editor, the playback stop, the wheel transparency, the dialog veil — is conventions.md's; the four dialog editors' box, field and button geometry (they left row 9's status lane for the modal dialog, 2026-08-12 — its own labwc window since that evening, so the geometry is dialog-local) are kdenlive-redesign.md's dialog section.

### Iteration Mode (the flags' bracket adornment)
ITERATION MODE IS TARGET-LEGAL (architect 2026-08-07), which is a MARKER-UI fact before it is a render one: the mode's whole visible form is the inline `+[lo, hi]` bracket `flag_text_iter` splices into every iter-eligible owning marker's composed label, so the flags carry it in BOTH audio views now. Bare `i` toggles the mode in the WARP COLUMN in either view (a phase-reset-view press is the silent no-op it always was; read-only refuses it identically from both views, `i` not being on `read_only_key_blocked`'s allowlist), the bit and the brackets PERSIST ACROSS S↔T IN BOTH DIRECTIONS, and `Ctrl+Alt+R` dispatches the sweep from either view. The DISPLAY needed no relaxing to follow: the bracket rides the one composer, `render_flags` takes `iteration_on` straight from the live bit, and the warp arm is selected by `active_markers_view == 'W'` alone — the painter never consulted the audio view.

WHAT STAYS SOURCE-ONLY IS BRACKET AUTHORING. The flag editor is a warp authoring surface, so both of its open routes still gate on `active_column_authoring_allowed` and its iter grammar with them (`enter_top_flag_edit`'s `iter_on` also requires the warp column and an eligible marker); a press that would open it off home keeps its existing silent refusal, and the S→T toggle still closes a live editor without committing. So in target view a bracket is READ and SWEPT but not typed.

THE SUPERSEDED RULING: entering target view used to exit the mode and wipe every bracket (architect 2026-07-23, commit 434ac13 — "brackets are the step-away batch tool, target view the live-by-hand tool"), and the sweep was gated to source view for its success tail's warp-store write (codex P1, bdf4336). Both premises predate the W+target bare cent step (contortion ruling 8, 2026-07-29), and brackets are DELTA-RELATIVE, so nothing technical bound them to source. The wipe is deleted; the sweep tail's clear survives as a granted home-view-binding exception (tempo-and-home-view.md carries the exception inventory, render-pipeline.md the dispatch record).

### Double-Click Machinery
All double-clicks are hand-rolled (Wayland delivers none) and arbitrated by ONE surface-tagged `DoubleClickCandidate` (surface ∈ {TrimBar, Marker, EditorText, EmptyLane} — app_state.h; `kDoubleClickMs` 500 / `kDoubleClickSlackPx` 8 = `kDragMovedThresholdPx` by construction; Marker additionally keyed on the marker index) so the surfaces cannot cross-fire. The TrimBar candidate is the span-framing double-click and the EmptyLane candidate the marker create (both seed-at-motionless-release, consume-before-arm — zoom-viewport-strip.md and the lane paragraph above; EmptyLane's seed rides the pending click's release since pan-primary, a crossed pan seeding nothing); the text-editor double-click selects the maximal run of the clicked character CLASS via `select_word_at` (word `[A-Za-z0-9_]`+bytes≥0x80 / whitespace / punctuation, the Qt/Kate convention; the ≥0x80 word-classing is what keeps a selection edge on a codepoint boundary now that the editors carry real UTF-8, and the probe itself is exact — `byte_index_from_shaped_x` reads the shaped run's own per-byte pen offsets, so non-ASCII earlier in the line shifts nothing). Any intervening keyboard or wheel command, a moved drag, button-lost, the force-end finalizer, or load clears the candidate.

### Space
SPACE IS THE SOLE PLAYBACK TOGGLE (`is_play_pause_key` = Space, bare-exact at its one shared owner; "i never use enter for playback" — which also drops Return/KpEnter off the read-only allowlist, the desired silent refusal). Space carries a SECOND binding since 2026-07-28: the phase-reset LEAD-IN AUDITION, gated on the reset being FOCUSED (`Selection::phase_overlay_subject`) rather than on the overlay being painted — the `N/2` geometry and the full ruling live in render-pipeline.md.

### Key Repeat
Repeat is ELIGIBILITY-GATED at press time (`repeat_eligible`, an application probe the platform consults BEFORE arming): globally only the continuous step gestures repeat — ALL FOUR BARE ARROWS (Left/Right the two-lane playhead step / marker nudge, Up/Down the tempo cent step; the lane split is decided per fire at dispatch, so the arrows repeat as one family and NO modified arrow repeats), PageUp/PageDown, `=`/`-` zoom, the marker-focus cycle (bare Tab / Shift+Tab / IsoLeftTab; the cycle always recenters on the focused marker, AT THE CURRENT ZOOM — the working-zoom landing of 2026-08-05 was reverted the same day), and the THREE repeating Ctrl chords (re-derived 2026-08-07, when the stay-put alt pair's rollback took the set back down from five) — Ctrl+Shift+Tab plus Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z, one `ctrl && !alt && key == Z` condition covering both (history stepping is a continuous step gesture; a shift pressed mid-hold flips undo to redo under the live-modifier rule; Ctrl+Tab stays one-shot). The march is the one of the three that is MODE-SCOPED (2026-08-07): inside the `h` history view that chord is the row-3 WALK cycle's REVERSE step, one-shot like its forward twin, so its arm carries a `!history_mode.active` term and the march outside the view repeats exactly as before — and inside an open text editor the typing/cursor/edit keys (never Enter/Esc/Tab); every other command is one-shot, and a press that OPENS an editor is judged under the pre-open context so it never repeats into what it opened. Repeat fires recompute modifiers AND codepoint live; any pointer-button press or completed wheel emission disarms the hold. A held key's synthesized repeats coalesce into ONE undo entry by repeat identity (normalization-and-boundaries.md owns the contortion-7 detail).
