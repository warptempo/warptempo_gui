### GUI Surfacing: Nothing Validates at Dispatch or at View Entry

Render dispatch validates NOTHING (`warp_render_preflight` is removed — every failure it could surface was unreachable from the live store; the worker's own resolve→build chain and its stderr are the tripwire surface). Target-view entry (`validate_target_view_entry`, shared by the `t` toggle and load's `active_audio_view=T` restore) is likewise resolve-then-build only — marker arrangements always enter, trim plays no part, and there is no kick-back tick gate (an edit made in target view just re-previews through the same normalizing resolver).

### Mouse Reach: No Global Halo, One Grab Constant Per Surface

THE ONE GLOBAL HIT HALF-WIDTH IS GONE — `kMarkerHitHalfPx`, the 4 px tolerance stems, flags and trim bounds all shared, DELETED 2026-08-02 with its last reader long behind it (no code had read it since 2026-07-20; the deletion record, including the rule that outlived it, is at the site in `app_state.h`). The redesign gave each surface its own authored, `gui_scale`-aware constant instead:

- `kMarkerStemGrabPx` = 4 (`app_state.h`, via `marker_stem_grab_px()`) — the marker stem's grab half-width PER SIDE. A 1px line is under any pointing tolerance, so the drawn stem and the grabbable stem are deliberately not the same column.
- `kTrimEndcapGrabPx` = 4 (`render.h`, via `trim_endcap_grab_px()`) — added to EACH SIDE of the drawn 2px endcap, making the target 10px, close to the retired square chip's width. The one place in the trim lane where paint and hit differ, and it is stated at both owners.
- FLAGS HAVE NO TOLERANCE AT ALL: the box IS the reach, its 1px left border included (2026-08-02 — the stash carries the painted extent, so a marker's reach grew one column to the LEFT of its frame column and a press on the border resolves the marker).

REACH IS VISIBLE-PAINTED-ONLY, and since row 5 that is structural rather than a rule to obey: the flag/stem stashes contain only what the painter emitted (the cull in `iterate_visible_flags_impl` is ASYMMETRIC because the box is — a left margin of `marker_flag_max_width_px` admits a marker whose box reaches in from offscreen, and the right bound is EXCLUSIVE at `viewport_end_sample` so a marker sitting exactly on the displayed end paints and publishes nothing into the inert non-multiple-of-16 gutter), and `hit_test_trim_endcap` culls a bound whose column is outside the viewport exactly as the painter does. The offscreen-halo reach and its blind-grab pull-in machinery were removed 2026-07-18 as a non-use-case; the drags' viewport clamp survives as a live tracking clamp. A GRAB TOLERANCE IS NOT A SPACING GAP — the rule the deleted constant used to carry, now belonging to nothing in particular: markers may sit arbitrarily close, overlap exactly, and cross during gestures; ordering degeneracy collapses at the render boundary, not at authoring time.

### The Painter's Stash IS the Hit Geometry (row 5, 2026-08-01)

`AppState::flag_hit_rects` and `AppState::marker_stems` are published BY THE PAINTER as it paints — one producer each, the flag-cache rebuild's `render_flags` / `render_phase_reset_flags` calls — against the displayed basis those pixels were laid out on. A row-5 flag's WIDTH is its SHAPED label's width, so there is no formula to re-derive it from: recomputing at the hit site would mean a second HarfBuzz pass that could disagree with the pixels. This is the STRONGER form of event-synchronized hit geometry (below): the rects ARE the painted rects, for free and at every moment, rather than by two derivations agreeing. The old live rebuild — `item_viewport_basis` + the displayed map + the drag overlay threaded into `compute_flag_hit_rects` — is deleted with the functions it called, as are `marker_hit_at`, the `MarkerHit` pair and the marker-text lane's run resolver (they died with that lane).

- `hit_test_flag` walks `flag_hit_rects` BACKWARDS and returns the first containing rect: the painter walks the store forward and later boxes cover earlier ones, so last-painted = topmost = what a click grabs. Boxes OVERLAP freely; selection does not lift one (it is a colour swap now), so this is the whole arbitration — one pass, no class split. The shape is a PLAIN RECT (the fused tip-down triangle and its taper test died with the triangle lane). The stash holds the ACTIVE column's boxes only, because that is the column the painter drew. Cold (nothing painted yet) nothing is clickable — the honest answer for a flag with no pixels.
- `hit_test_marker_stem` is the marker's SECOND surface (architect 2026-08-01): a press within `kMarkerStemGrabPx` of an ENABLED marker's stem column, IN THE WAVEFORM'S UPPER HALF, resolves to that marker and routes through the very same click bodies its flag does. It reads `marker_stems`, so the grabbable stem is the DRAWN stem by construction, and a DISABLED marker — which publishes no stem entry at all — is not grabbable for the same reason it is not visible (one fact, not two). NEAREST WINS, TIES TO LATER-IN-STORE, the same topmost rule the boxes use. UPPER HALF ONLY is a structural fit: the plain waveform press already splits by half (upper = playhead placement + region arm, lower = the scrub), so the claim slots into an existing seam and left-scrubbing at a marker's column stays possible. The BARE RIGHT press (2026-08-01) scrubs at full height and never resolves a marker — the stem claim is left-press vocabulary.
- THE STEM SURFACE IS PLAIN-EXACT and SHIFT/CTRL ARE FLAG-ONLY (architect 2026-08-01, second pass): both modifiers already own a waveform gesture at the very pixels a stem stands on (ctrl = the strip drag, shift = the region former), so a modified press near a stem is not a marker hit at all and falls through to the waveform underneath. The gate lives at the ONE resolution site in `on_button_press` (`stem_click`), leaving `mh_index` at -1 for a modified press, so every branch below reads one index from one hit owner.
- THE SUPPRESSED EDITING FLAG PUBLISHES NO HIT RECT (codex 2026-08-02, a6ecf72, correcting the same-day suppression): the open flag editor owns every pixel of its marker's box, so `render_flags` skips that box — and the rect must match the pixels, which is this stash's whole doctrine. The earlier unreachable-by-ordering argument held only while the editor was at least as WIDE as the committed flag, an assumption that pass removed: a narrowed editor left a visually BLANK tail, and a press there closed the editor as an outside press and then fell through to `hit_test_flag` IN THE SAME PRESS. The close edge is safe by construction — closing changes `fp_editing_flag_target`, which misses the flag cache's fingerprint, and ONE `render_flags` pass repaints the box and republishes its rect together; no frame has one without the other in either direction. THE STEM IS THE DELIBERATE EXCEPTION: it is still PAINTED for the whole editing session, so it stays hit-testable by the same paint-equals-claim rule. RECORDED DELTA: a double-click in the narrowed editor's blank tail now drops a marker as an empty-lane press — blank pixels behave as blank.

### Pointer Modifiers

ALT IS POINTER-ONLY VOCABULARY (architect 2026-07-28, retiring the 2026-07-17 gesture pivot's "alt is the pointer/arrow authoring modifier"): on the pointer alt is PAN and nothing else — the alt+drag captured grab-pan and the alt+wheel stepped pan, a matched pair against ctrl's zoom — and on the KEYBOARD alt survives only in the FOUR Ctrl+Alt render/propagate chords (R / Shift+R / P / Shift+P — a fifth, Ctrl+Alt+I, retired 2026-08-02 when Ctrl+Alt+R absorbed the iteration sweep; a SIXTH and SEVENTH, the stay-put undo pair Ctrl+Alt+Z / Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Z, lived one day and were rolled back 2026-08-07 — labwc owns those two chords on the target desktop, so the GUI never received them; the record is in `selection-model.md`). Every alt-modified keyboard gesture it used to name became BARE: the augmented drop is now the only `s` drop, the lead-in audition rides Space's own overlay-state gate, and the tempo step and position steps became the bare arrows under the LANE MODEL (the tempo-image step was a third such route until contortion ruling 8 deleted it) — see `selection-model.md` and `tempo-and-home-view.md`. ALT ARMS NO ITEM GESTURE ANYWHERE: an Alt-exact press performs no action on a marker at all (HISTORY, worth keeping because it is re-proposable: the Alt marker arm retired 2026-07-20 with phase 4, `hit_test_marker_line` deleted; the 2026-07-22 alt-exact flag-click LAND retired 2026-07-23 when the plain marker click took the land) and none on trim (the WAVEFORM trim-stem grab and `hit_test_trim_boundary` were deleted the same day under the architect's "chips-only" answer — bounds are grabbed on the top strip and nowhere else; `render_trim_stems`, the waveform half of the bounds' display, was itself deleted 2026-08-01). A Ctrl+Alt-exact press is a strict-guard no-op.

CTRL keeps its letter chords — S/Q/Z/C/N/D/P — the tab chords, the four Ctrl+Alt chords, and the editors' internal keys, AMENDED for FOUR pointer surfaces: the ctrl-exact TOP-STRIP FLAG press is the membership toggle (2026-07-23; FLAG-ONLY since 2026-08-01, the branch spelled `inside_top` rather than the stem's widened gate); the ctrl-exact WAVEFORM press is the dual-axis strip drag; and the ctrl-exact / ctrl+shift-exact TRIM-BAR presses are the reinstated BEGIN / END bound-set clicks (853c2c4's one-day retirement reversed 2026-08-01 — set-then-arm-the-single-bound-drag on the bar's whole band, with the strictly-inside refusal: a value equal to or past its partner is a consumed no-op that writes nothing, deselects nothing, stops nothing and arms nothing; the rule is `normalization-and-boundaries.md`'s). Ctrl+Shift claims the trim bar and NOTHING else on the pointer.

THE RIGHT BUTTON GAINED ITS FIRST BINDING 2026-08-01: a bare-exact right press anywhere in the waveform, full height, is the audition scrub (`region-scrub-esc.md` owns the statement) — gated on `any_pointer_gesture_active` so a captured drag's delivered button events cannot scrub mid-gesture, and the settings popup owns EVERY button while open (a non-left press on it is consumed inert). Modified right presses remain strict no-ops everywhere.

STRICT MODIFIER VALIDATION IS PROJECT-WIDE (architect 2026-07-28): a modifier combination with no explicit binding is a no-op everywhere, keyboard and pointer alike — `conventions.md` has the full statement and the families where a modifier is a real binding rather than laxity. A PRESS THAT CLAIMS NOTHING STOPS NO PLAYBACK: the top-strip stop is INTENTIONAL, not positional, owned at each claim site, and a claim that can still REFUSE puts its stop inside the refusal gate (the trim bound sets place theirs inside `set_trim_bound_at_click`, past every refusal and immediately ahead of the write).

### The Trim Surface: the Endcap Trim Bar

The trim surface since row 5 (2026-08-01) is the ENDCAP TRIM BAR — one continuous bar with a 2px endcap at each bound over the lane ground — served by `hit_test_trim_endcap`, `route_trim_bar_press`, `trim_endcap_rect`, `TrimBarPressSeed` and `top_trim_row_area` (renamed to this vocabulary 2026-08-02, architect-ordered; they carried the square b/e chips' names for a day). `kdenlive-redesign.md` is authoritative for the lane's paint, `render.h`'s `render_trim_flags` block for the pixel derivation.

GEOMETRY, one owner per fact, shared by paint and hit: A TRIM BOUND IS AN EDGE, not a point — `trim_endcap_rect` puts the begin cap's LEFT edge on its bound column and the end cap's RIGHT edge on its own (the deliberate asymmetry vs centered marker flags, so a bound at frame 0 / EOF shows its cap fully onscreen), and the hit test consumes that same rect inflated by `kTrimEndcapGrabPx` per side. `trim_bound_column` is the ONE bound-to-column formula (it replaced five hand-copied `nearbyint` + clamp spellings) and carries the EOF-wall clamp. The BAR spans the window between the caps, follows an OFFSCREEN bound flush to the edge rather than stopping short, and a culled bound paints no cap at all. `trim_bridge_gap` owns the between-the-endcaps column interval, shared by the router's pair-drag test and the painter's midpoint fit test. THE MIDPOINT MARK (2026-08-01, kdenlive's zone-middle crop blitted verbatim, at the WINDOW's midpoint column) is INFORMATIONAL: binary fits-with-clearance visibility, no hit rect, no gesture, no routing change.

`route_trim_bar_press` (returns whether it CLAIMED the press; a claim never falls through) resolves in this order: (1) an ENDCAP rect hit — `hit_test_trim_endcap`, y-gated on `top_trim_row_area` and x-tested against the inflated cap rects, forward-walked in ascending column — LEFTMOST WINS an overlap, this hit test's OWN deterministic policy, not a mirror of painter z-order (render_trim_flags lays begin then end with no sort and no reverse, and the caps share colors, so the pixels give no cue either way) — arms the SINGLE-bound pending drag; (2) a press inside the trim band whose waveform-relative column falls inside `trim_bridge_gap` AND inside the `[0, area_w)` click gate (the same effective-width clip the painter applies, so the inert gutter neither paints nor arms) arms the PAIR (bridge) drag, always as `is_begin=true` structurally since the pair has no grabbed-bound notion; (3) anything else claims nothing and falls through to the caller's marker handling. THE ROUTER ITSELF ASKS NOTHING ABOUT READ-ONLY (its two internal returns, unreachable behind the one caller's band gate, deleted 2026-08-02) AND NEITHER DOES ANYTHING ABOVE IT SINCE 2026-08-07: that caller's band gate — the whole band's sole read-only defense — was deleted with the ruling that trim is BAND rather than authored content, so a locked tab arms the endcap and bridge drags exactly as a writable one does, and GESTURE ROUTING lost its only member (`validation_topology.md`). Arming is a PENDING drag (`PendingTrimDrag` + the shared `kDragMovedThresholdPx` = 8): nothing mutates the store until motion crosses the threshold.

THE BAND IS ONE ACCESSOR, `top_trim_row_area` — the y-gate of the endcap hit, the bridge test, both bound-set clicks, the span-framing double-click and the painter's lane, so paint, hit and every trim gesture read the same rect and cannot drift. The plain left press inside the top strip resolves band by band (they are disjoint, so nothing contends): the RULER band's plain drag arms the strip drag and returns; the TRIM band consumes a pending span-framing double-click FIRST (surface-tagged `TrimBar`, ahead of every arm, allowed in read-only — pure navigation; the action is `zoom-viewport-strip.md`'s `run_span_framing_command`), else records `TrimBarPressSeed` (which sat ABOVE the band's read-only return while that return existed, so a locked tab framed without arming; the return is deleted 2026-08-07 and the ordering carries no rule any more), else routes. A PLAIN TRIM-BAND CLICK THAT NEVER BECOMES A DRAG IS A CONSUMED NOTHING (architect 2026-07-30): its entire act was publishing the trim window as a region highlight, and that coupling retired with the span form — so the two highlight-only arms and the pre-route stop that served them are gone, and only the motionless-release double-click seed survives. A SHIFT-exact trim-band press claims nothing at all: trim is transparent to shift, the flag boxes' band excludes the trim row, and it ends at the inert top-strip return having stopped no playback. On the WAVEFORM no trim hit test runs at all — the bounds have no waveform pixels to grab since `render_trim_stems` died.

TRIM GESTURES NEVER TOUCH THE PLAYHEAD (architect 2026-07-18): the drags move bounds only — no tracking, no pin, no pre-drag capture/restore (a recorded difference from the marker drag, which TRACKS its grabbed marker; the capture/restore half of that difference dissolved 2026-07-29 when contortion rulings 3+4 gave pointer gestures no cancel at all — no gesture restores a playhead, a selection or a region, and a force-ended trim drag simply keeps its live bounds). The drags DESELECT and STOP a live audition at their FIRST ACCEPTED bound change, and that deselect RESTS. EVERY trim pointer gesture reads the resting pair, WHICH ALWAYS EXISTS (always-set trim, 2026-07-30: the unset state, lone bounds and the `-1` spelling are deleted; the full window `[0, total-1]` is the rest default and renders untrimmed). PARTNER WALLS ARE PER-ROUTE since 2026-08-02 and the authoritative per-route statement is at `input_trim.cpp`'s header: the SINGLE-bound drag clamps INCLUSIVELY at its partner through one owner (`clamp_trim_bound_at_partner`) applied twice — at motion, and again at commit AFTER the release column snap, whose round-trip otherwise pulls the coincident value off by up to a grid span and silently broke the ruled drag-onto-partner quick-clear; the BRIDGE drag needs no partner wall and has none (it moves the pair rigidly by one shared delta, so the gap is invariant), and it also has no viewport clamp — both bounds ride the delta, walls only, blind partner motion deliberate (the offscreen ruling forbids blind grabs, not blind motion of a pair held by its middle); the bound-set CLICKS refuse strictly-inside; the typed and loaded routes have no clamp and are caught by the crossed/equal reset at commit.

### Hit Geometry Is Event-Synchronized — and There Are TWO Epochs

HIT GEOMETRY IS EVENT-SYNCHRONIZED (architect 2026-07-18; the VIEWPORT half joined 2026-07-24 with the occlusion arc). The item-basis pair is `displayed_or_live_target_map` (the map the aimed-at item pixels were painted with) and `item_viewport_basis` (the vp_start / vp_end / effective-width triple the committed flag item cache was built against). Both halves are STAGED by the flag-cache rebuild — the SOLE stage site since the trim-stem cache died 2026-07-25 — and PROMOTED at the TOP of the committing frame's paint, before any painting, so that frame's blitted caches and its live overlays land on the same basis. Hit DECISIONS therefore flip at the exact instant the on-screen items flip: the frame COMMIT that blits the flag cache, not the offscreen rebuild and not the earlier plate publish. Synchronizing by TIME was rejected (the true lag varies through zero with worker load and refresh rate, so any constant would invert the skew in the common fast-commit case). The remaining seams are ALL accepted: commit-to-scanout plus human reaction (irreducible — input always responds to the previously presented frame); the COLD-STATE fallback (first paint / view flip / just after load — both accessors fall back to the LIVE map and viewport, bit-for-bit the pre-mirror behaviour); and the column-based playhead-placement clicks, out of scope by ruling.

THE ITEM-BASIS ROSTER, re-derived by grep 2026-08-02: `hit_test_trim_endcap` and `route_trim_bar_press`'s bridge test on the hit side, and `GuiPaintHandler::paint_trim` on the PAINT side — trim hit and trim paint reading the identical owner chain (`displayed_trim_ms` → `trim_bound_column` → `trim_bridge_gap` / `trim_endcap_rect`), so paint == hit by shared owners rather than by two agreeing derivations. `render_flag_editor_box` is the roster's other item-basis consumer but a SEPARATE one: it places the editor through `painted_column_of_source_frame_on_basis` (the marker column-placement owner), touching none of the trim chain. THE TWO MARKER HIT TESTS ARE NOT ON THIS LIST and that is the point: `hit_test_flag` and `hit_test_marker_stem` read the painter's stash, which is the stronger form of the same guarantee. (`marker_hit_at` and `lane_text_left_x` left the roster by deletion with the marker-text lane; the selected-stem DAMAGE was listed here until 2026-07-30 and was never a consumer at all.) THE PROMOTION COUNTER IS GONE — `displayed_map_gen`, bumped once per top-of-frame promotion, DELETED 2026-08-02: its only subscribers were the hover cache's staleness key and the tick-side stationary-cursor repair, both of which row 5 deleted, leaving it write-only. The promotion itself is unchanged and lives in one block in `on_redraw`, which is where a future subscriber would hang its key.

`item_viewport_basis` was renamed from `displayed_viewport_basis` on 2026-07-30 and the genealogy is kept because the failure it fixed is re-creatable: it and `GuiPaintHandler::plate_viewport_basis` were both spelled `displayed_viewport_basis` and both returned a `DisplayedViewportBasis`, so C++ name lookup silently resolved the unqualified spelling to the member inside class scope and the free owner needed a `::`-qualification to be reachable — two coordinate epochs indistinguishable by grep, which is how three authoritative comments came to disagree about which basis a stem's damage rode. THE EPOCHS STAY DISTINCT BY RULING. `plate_viewport_basis` reads the LIVE `wf_cache.fp_*` and registers the PLATE-REGISTERED overlays with the just-blitted plate; the authoritative enumeration of those overlays lives at its own declaration in `paint_handler.h` (re-derived by grep 2026-08-02) and no other site restates it. The MARKER STEMS are deliberately not among them — they paint from the flag painter's stash, on the basis those boxes were laid out against, so a stem cannot leave its flag. The two accessors are NUMERICALLY EQUAL at every frame committed by the two plate writers (worker publish, synchronous rebuild), each of which rebuilds the flag cache inline before the damaged frame paints; the equality is NOT unconditional, and the live exception is the accepted RESIZE ITEM-ONLY-PROMOTION window (the flag rebuild fires from `on_tick` and stages the OLD fp_vp span over the NEW effective width while the displayed plate still pairs that span with its old width), which is exactly why item-registered consumers must ride the item owner.

DAMAGE FOLLOWS THE BASIS OF THE PIXELS IT ERASES. Since 2026-07-30 the playheads' narrow damage is CADENCE-scoped: it resolves on `plate_viewport_basis` where the site can reach a paint handler (the two 60 Hz scanner sites) and WIDENS to a full waveform-area invalidate for every discrete command — the rule and the per-site table live at `playhead_pixel_x`, `app_state.h`.

### Authoring-Gesture Mechanics Run on the Displayed Paint Basis Too

(2026-07-22, the slippery-drag fix and its codex round, superseding the earlier mechanics-stay-live rule.) THE MARKER DRAG's delta model anchors in the DISPLAYED target domain: per motion, `proposed_src = inv_disp(fwd_disp(orig) + active-domain delta)` against `displayed_or_live_target_map`, with an exact zero-EFFECTIVE-motion short-circuit to `orig` tested on the CLAMPED delta rather than the raw pointer delta (codex round 6), preserving the bit-exact untouched contract — so the flag tracks the pointer 1:1 BY CONSTRUCTION under any map, where the former live-map inverse slipped at the slope ratio wherever displayed ≠ live, or across the press-offset straddle at a fresh breakpoint. Its begin-time wall/viewport clamps and its commit column-snap read the same displayed map (stored equals SHOWN, literally). The TRIM drags (single-bound anchor / motion / clamp, pair motion, release snap) and BOTH NUDGES' pixel anchoring moved to the same basis, as did the phase-reset overlay's map. THE FREEZE PREMISE IS MADE TRUE BY TWO HALVES: `maybe_enqueue_waveform_render` FULLY freezes dispatch while a marker or trim drag is active (a whole-enqueue early return, replacing the original hash-only exclusion whose vp/area fields could sustain a drop-rewind-redispatch loop), and `on_waveform_render_done` DROPS the at-most-one job in flight at the grab (`pending_fp_*` rewound to `fp_*`, supersede cleared) — marker and trim drags ALONE, since the strip drag and alt-pan render synchronously per frame (draining the worker) and must keep rendering, and the region drag never touches the plate. So the displayed basis cannot swap mid-gesture.

`kick_waveform_sync` reclamps through `clamp_viewport_start` before rendering, landing the final clamped geometry in ONE sync render per edit (the tick's live-total backstop owns only asynchronous total changes like a preview completion). THE FLAG-EDITOR COMMIT RUNS NO SYNCHRONOUS RE-WARP AT ALL: the editor is a warp authoring surface that exists only in warp's SOURCE home view (both open routes gate on `active_column_authoring_allowed`, and the S→T toggle closes any open editor without committing before entering target view), so a commit can never run with `active_audio_view == 'T'` and there is no displayed target plate to re-warp — the former `canonical_changed`-gated sync branch was unreachable and is deleted; `canonical_changed` survives ONLY as the undo push's `affects_persistence` gate, and `target_render.trigger()` keeps its `store_changed` gate (the record is at `flag_editor.cpp`, in the commit tail).

STILL LIVE-BASIS BY RULING: the click-playhead placement family (column-based, including drops at the playhead and the `x` region→trim conversion) and the land/follow-placement family (`source_frame_to_active_domain` — the Tab basis, post-commit truth), while walls stay integer source frames everywhere. AND EVERY DISCRETE WARP-MAP EDIT REACHABLE IN TARGET VIEW RE-WARPS SYNCHRONOUSLY, so displayed == live at every command boundary: the divergence window closes at its source, the live-basis `x` conversion coincides with the displayed one by construction, and the displayed-basis mechanics plus the completion-drop gate remain as belt-and-braces for the one residual case (a viewport-dispatched worker job in flight across a gesture boundary, always carrying the then-current map). THE ONE AUTHORITATIVE, GREP-DERIVED CALLER INVENTORY FOR THOSE RE-WARP SITES LIVES AT `viewport.h`'s `kick_waveform_sync` DECLARATION — this file states only the class and points there, per the re-grep-at-retells rule.
