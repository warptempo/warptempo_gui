# The Kdenlive Redesign (row-by-row)

Chartered 2026-07-30, implementation began 2026-07-31. The architect works TOP-DOWN, row by row, by dropping screenshot crops into `tmp/screenshots/kdenlive/redesign/` and pointing the planner at them — every state a surface has (rest, hover, clicked, ...) gets its own crop. Do not run ahead of the screenshots. Design parity is "loosely held, with room for tailoring to our needs"; workflow parity stays Ableton. Design and testing happen at 100% `gui_scale`; 1920x1080 remains the only supported resolution.

## The standing rulings (apply to every row)

- THE CSS FLOAT MODEL (architect 2026-07-31, ruled at the row-1 live test): the architect describes rows in CSS box vocabulary — content dimensions, margins, borders, padding — and a stated dimension NEVER includes a border. A flat button with nothing stated fills its whole row; the first row starts at the very top; NO margin/inset/gap exists unless he states one ("I will tell you when there's spacing — don't assume that"). Adjacent buttons get a 2px gap that is an INVISIBLE SEPARATOR, not margin.
- CORRECT CAPITALIZATION, row by row (architect 2026-07-31): redesigned rows' labels capitalize properly ("Quit", "Save"); the all-lowercase program-text rule carves out exactly those labels and stays for stderr, prompts, and every un-redesigned surface until its own row.
- THE REDESIGNED ROWS ARE WHEEL-INERT: one band test at wheel_context (returns the inert class above the area tests); future rows join by extending the band.
- BUTTONS DISPATCH THEIR CHORDS THROUGH on_key (a synthesized GuiInputState — the button IS its chord): every gate the keyboard route passes (loading/blank, keyboard-modal, read-only, the arm's own refusals) applies identically and nothing can drift. The exception is a button whose action is not a chord dispatch (Quit: finalize + request_close, spelled at its claim).
- ICONS are in-tree Breeze paths rendered by src/gui/icons.{h,cpp} (an SVG d-string subset interpreter over cairo paths, GUI-only; provenance SVGs committed verbatim under assets/icons/breeze/, read by no code). Icon colors are the SVGs' own, hard-coded per the color ruling.

- THE SCREENSHOTS OVERRIDE ALL PRIOR COLOR WORK (architect 2026-07-30/31). The colors.conf tunable domain is SHRINKING to the WAVEFORM AREA AND THE MARKERS (canvas ground, waveform ink, marker classes); every other color a redesign row touches is HARD-CODED from the sampled crop — constexpr, not a key, not in the config grammar. An existing surface keeps its tunables until its own redesign row retires them; no key deletion happens ahead of that. The recorded carve-out lives at render.h's palette-block header.
- THE SANS FACE IS THE REDESIGN'S DEFAULT FAMILY: cairo toy selector "sans" (fontconfig → Liberation Sans on the target) at 12pt through the existing points×4/3 convention (16px at 100%). The monospace face survives on every un-redesigned surface; each row moves its own text over.
- ALL REDESIGNED TEXT GOES THROUGH THE SHAPING CHOKEPOINT (`src/gui/text_shape.h`, HarfBuzz over the cairo font's own FT face): the shaped run is the single width truth — measurement, paint, truncation and hit geometry all read the same run. No Pango, no cross-font fallback, no bidi — one wide-coverage face is the model.
- REDESIGNED DIMENSIONS RIDE THE gui_scale AXIS (`gui_scale_factor()`, render.h), NOT the monospace font's `gui_font_scale()`. The two axes are independent by design — the ruling and the push plumbing (three application points, mirroring font_size) are at the accessor's declaration. Crop-measured 100% values are the authored constants; every conversion rounds with std::nearbyint. Fills land on integer pixel bounds; axis-aligned 1px strokes, when a row ever has them, take the cairo +0.5 half-pixel alignment so nothing axis-aligned antialiases.
- HIT GEOMETRY IS PUBLISHED BY THE PAINTER where a rect depends on shaped text (the displayed-basis doctrine applied to proportional surfaces): the painter stashes the rect it painted; pointer code reads the stash and never re-shapes.

## Row 1 — the menu row (landed 2026-07-31)

Source crops: `row_1_button_{rest,hover}.png` (46x30). The new TOP LANE 0 (kTopLaneCount 5→6, every prior lane renumbered +1; `top_menu_row_area`, `menu_row_h_px` = 30 at 100%): full-width flat ground #292c30 (the rest crop's first column — a DELIBERATE mismatch with kBackground #202326, the crop wins), no ring, painted in `GuiPaintHandler::paint_menu_row` OUTSIDE the loading/total>0 branches (the redesigned rows are audio-independent: visible exactly whenever they are clickable; the toolbar shares the pass), gated on its own exposure so per-frame scanner damage never pays the HarfBuzz shape.

THE QUIT BUTTON, the row's sole content: label "Quit" (the capitalization ruling above), sans 12pt, kRedesignLabel #fcfcfc in BOTH states, flush left, 10px padding per side — button width = nearbyint(shaped width) + 2·pad, baseline solved from the face's own extents (row.y + (h + ascent − descent)/2), never a literal. TWO faces, not three: rest = bare label on the row ground; hover = a filled kRedesignAccent #3daee9 pill, FLUSH at the full 30px row height (corner radius 5, arc-path fill, corners AA) under the unchanged label; A PRESS PAINTS NOTHING NEW — a click keeps the hover face, pointer-out rests it. Hover state and the painted rect live in the shared button roster `app.redesign_buttons` (Quit + row 2's four; one recompute/clear writer pair in input_pointer.cpp, recomputed in on_motion's no-gesture tail against the painter-stashed rects, cleared by the pointer-leave hook; at most one `invalidate_top_strip` per transition — the strip idiom, no narrow rects). An active gesture freezes hover (the tail doesn't run); both cold states (zero stash, pre-motion (−1,−1) cursor) resolve to not-hovered with no special case.

THE PRESS CLAIM sits ABOVE the `app.loading || total_frames() <= 0` guard in on_button_press (quit stays live during load and on a blank state) and BELOW the modal gates (a prompt or bottom-strip editor swallows the press exactly as it does for every other pointer target; Ctrl+Q reaches the same route from the keyboard anyway). Band semantics keep the shape the old zoom row pioneered: modified press = strict consumed no-op; plain left press inside the stashed button rect fires ON PRESS — `finalize_active_drags()` then `prompt.request_close()`, the WM-close ordering, which is Ctrl+Q's exact route (the unsaved-work prompt gates on dirty inside request_close); any other press in the band is a consumed nothing. Nothing in the row drags; there is no arm, no threshold, no release body, no press-state machinery.

ROW-1 REFINEMENTS (architect 2026-07-31, live test PASSED whole — "looks and works great", scale/hover/click all verified): the hover pill is FLUSH — full 30px row height, the inset constant deleted (the crop's apparent 1px margins were the title-bar seam; the CSS float model above is the ruled reading); the label is "Quit" (the capitalization ruling above); the row is wheel-inert. The baseline note stands (nearbyint lands the tie at 20 of 30).

## The zoom row is deleted; the framing double-click rehomes (architect 2026-07-31)

Ordered at the row-1 live test — THE ARCHITECT'S OWN REVERSAL of his 2026-07-27 rollback ruling (80a265e's "do not re-propose the lane deletion or the framer rehoming" is SUPERSEDED by this direct order). The dedicated zoom LANE is gone: its height constants, its ring paint (render_strip_row_ring died with its last caller), its press claim, its strip-drag arm and its double-click surface. THE GESTURE SURVIVES WHOLE: the ctrl-exact waveform press is now the dual-axis strip drag's ONE entry — same StripDragState, same capture, same anchor stem, full reach — and wheel / `=`/`-` / alt-pan / bare `0` / `c` are untouched; tab_X_zoom persists unchanged; StripDragState::double_click_seed died as a field (only the zoom-row arm ever set it).

THE SPAN-FRAMING DOUBLE-CLICK LIVES ON THE TRIM BAR now (the whole chip-row band — chips, bridge, empty space alike; it frames rather than grabs): run_span_framing_command (renamed from run_zoom_double_click_command), region-else-proper-trim-else-whole-song with the same margins and idempotence, read-only allowed (pure navigation). Seeding is a RELEASE act (ChipRowPressSeed: the press records its point above the read-only return, the release seeds only if motionless on both axes AND no trim drag went live — the two "stayed a click" spellings equal by construction, kDoubleClickSlackPx == kDragMovedThresholdPx); the consume runs FIRST at the next plain chip-row press, ahead of every arm, so no pending drag exists under a consumed double-click. DoubleClickSurface::ZoomRow → TrimBar.

## Row 2 — the toolbar row (landed 2026-07-31)

Source crops: row_2_button_{rest,hover}.png (81x32), row_2_separator.png (1x34), row_2_border_bottom.png; assets in assets/icons/breeze/. TOP LANE 1 (roster after this arc: 0 menu 30px, 1 toolbar 45px, 2 trim chip, 3 marker text, 4 flag, 5 triangle): content 44px + 1px border-bottom OUTSIDE the content (the CSS model — stated dimensions exclude borders), ground kRedesignRowGround #292c30 (the row-1 constants renamed row-independent: kRedesignRowGround / kRedesignAccent #3daee9 / kRedesignLabel #fcfcfc, plus kRedesignLine #535659 for the separators and the border, which runs the ENTIRE window width, drawn crisp on integer bounds).

LAYOUT at 100% (left → right, all on gui_scale_factor()): 5px row padding; separator (1x34, 5px margin all around → the line at x=10, y row+5); 5; SAVE button; separator (5px margins); UNDO; 2px gap; REDO; 2px gap; RENDER. Button anatomy (the Save crop, 81x32 reproduced exactly): 6px top/bottom margin (32 tall), no horizontal margin; inside: 12px pad, 16x16 icon vertically centered, 7px gap, 12pt sans label (the extents-centered baseline), 10px pad. Hover face: a 1px kRedesignAccent rounded-rect OUTLINE (radius 5) on the button box at the +0.5 half-pixel alignment — interior untouched, straight edges crisp, corners AA. Same two-face model as row 1; hover state and painter-published rects generalized into ONE roster (app.redesign_buttons — Quit + the four, one recompute/clear pair, one invalidate_top_strip per transition).

THE FOUR BUTTONS ARE THEIR CHORDS, through on_key: Save = Ctrl+S, Undo = Ctrl+Z, Redo = Ctrl+Shift+Z, Render = Ctrl+Alt+R. All four refuse during load/blank and at the read-only gate exactly as the keys do; Ctrl+S reaches through an open flag editor, the others drop at the keyboard-modal gate — inherited, not restated. The toolbar's band claim sits with the menu row's (above the loading guard — visible and hoverable always; the actions self-guard through their routes).

Icons: DocumentSave/EditUndo/EditRedo fill #fcfcfc; MediaRecord fills its own #da4453 (coincides with the marker-red ring by shared Breeze ancestry, not by reference). The d-interpreter's subset: M/m L/l H/h V/v C/c A/a Z/z, implicit repetition, comma/whitespace/minus/leading-dot separation, from_chars numbers; elliptical 'a' via the standard endpoint→center conversion + arc-to-bezier (≤90° segments).
