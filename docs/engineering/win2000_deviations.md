# Where the chrome deviates from Windows 2000

The GUI's chrome is a period Windows measured pixel by pixel (the unit, the DrawEdge families, the roster case, the fonts' cells; `CLAUDE.md`'s rounding and colour rules, `src/gui/render.h`'s palette block), in THREE CHROME VOCABULARIES (`src/gui/chrome_spec.h`): Windows 2000's "Windows Standard" chrome as ReactOS 0.4.16 draws it (architect 2026-10-06, "pivot to Win2K as the official supported version"), the default (Clearlooks held the default for the afternoon of 2026-10-07) and the metric base whose layout every other vocabulary fits, GNOME 2's Clearlooks as Debian squeeze shipped it, the second vocabulary, and Solaris 9's CDE, the third (2026-10-08; its own departures are the last section); this list is Windows 2000's. Windows 95's chrome, the look of 2026-10-06's morning, was dropped the same evening and stands in git history; its colors remain in the theme catalog's record, `windows-95-standard` (`docs/themes/`), shipped nowhere. This list records the FEW MAJOR DESIGN DECISIONS where the chrome deliberately departs from Windows 2000, one line each, with the reason and the owner (architect 2026-10-03: "start a list of things where we deviate from Windows"): first where it follows ReactOS, then the product's own departures in drawing, then its own elements and behaviour. The obvious (antialiased proportional faces, a chrome's compiled colors, Display-P3 bytes) is not a deviation worth a line; the rule stands at its owner and is not restated here.

## Where it follows ReactOS rather than Windows 2000

The vocabulary is named for Windows 2000, whose chrome ReactOS 0.4.16 reproduces in all 29 colours and every metric (Windows XP's Classic and Server 2003's are the same), but its law is the architect's ReactOS captures (2026-10-06: "get it as accurate to ReactOS as we can"); where ReactOS itself departs from Windows 2000, the product follows ReactOS on purpose.

- **The caption's gradient spans the whole caption** (2026-10-06): Windows 2000 holds the icon cell at the start colour, ramps across the title alone and holds the buttons' stretch at the end colour; ReactOS ramps the whole caption rect, under the icon and the buttons (`GRADIENT_FILL_RECT_H` over the full caption), smooth 24-bit as Windows 2000 itself drew at 24 and 32 bits. Owner: `paint_caption_gradient` (render.cpp).
- **Minimise and Restore are ReactOS's Marlett's** (2026-10-06): Windows 2000 draws Minimise 6 x 2 and Restore's two windows 6 wide; ReactOS's own Marlett, drawn at 12 ppem, gives Minimise 7 x 2 and windows 7 wide. Maximise is Windows' own. Owner: paint_handler.cpp's caption glyph block (`kCaptionMinimizeGlyph`, `kCaptionRestoreGlyph`).
- **The face is Wine Tahoma** (2026-10-06): Windows 2000 sets its chrome in Microsoft's Tahoma; ReactOS ships Wine's metric-matched Tahoma (Vera-based, LGPL-2.1+), which the product bundles and draws as its antialiased outline at every size. Owner: `src/gui/gui_font.h` (`kGuiFaceSetWin2000`), `fonts/README.md`.
- **The icons are Tango's, greyed by saturation when disabled** (2026-10-06): Windows 2000 wears Microsoft's own icons; ReactOS wears Tango's, and so does the product — Tango 0.8.90's drawings — with ReactOS's disabled face, comctl32's ILS_SATURATE at 192 / 255 (each pixel its .30 / .59 / .11 luminance, alpha kept). Owner: `src/gui/icons.h`'s head, `assets/icons/tango/README.md`, `icons::draw_disabled`.

## The product's own departures in drawing

The chrome is scalable (the tablet runs at up to 300 %), so where Windows drew a pixel picture at 96 dpi the product draws what that picture would have been at the panel's own resolution.

- **The relief's corners are mitred** (2026-10-06, the architect overruling Windows' square join): DrawEdge joins a light edge to a dark one squarely, the dark side owning the corner pixel; here, the chrome being scalable, the top-right and bottom-left corner blocks of every raised and sunken edge are split along the 45-degree diagonal, antialiased, as a real bevel's corner is ("Anything the original designers would have drawn with a diagonal if they could, we should"); the one-colour frames and the cards' flat line stay square. Owner: `paint_relief_frame` (render.cpp).
- **A dither's cell is one device px** (2026-10-06, "dithering gets translated into whatever it perceptually becomes"): the checked face and the trim track's checker are Windows' pattern brush, Hilight over the ground, at the panel's own pixel, never at a scaled Windows px and never a blended colour; at 300 % the pattern is three times finer than a 96-dpi screen's. Owner: `paint_checker_rect` (render.cpp), render.h's palette head.
- **The glyphs are scalable outlines** (2026-10-06): the icons are Tango's scalable drawings, rendered by resvg at every scale, never a 16- or 24-px bitmap scaled up; the caption's Close is the smooth outline of Marlett's 8 x 7 staircase (the architect's pick of the mocks), not the staircase; the playhead's head is WordPad's ruler marker as a filled outline. Owner: `src/gui/svg_icon.h`, paint_handler.cpp's caption glyph block (`kCaptionCloseOutline`), render.h's playhead block.
- **A disabled checked button keeps its sunken edge** (2026-10-08, the architect on the view group greyed under the color picker: the checker without the edge read as "a different color but not sunken"): ReactOS's flat toolbar draws a disabled checked button with the checker and no edge (toolbar.c's `TOOLBAR_DrawFrame` sinks the edge only for an interactive button); here the checked face is the checker inside the sunken line whatever the enabled bit, the disabled state only saturating the glyph — a checked button that loses its edge when disabled no longer reads as checked. Clearlooks agrees on its own, GTK sinking an insensitive active toggle. Owner: `paint_toolbar_box` (paint_handler.cpp).
- **Clearlooks' focused title halo is a stroked outline** (2026-10-07): metacity's `title_text_focused` draws four copies of the title in shade (sel, 0.7) one px down, right, left and up under the white title; scaled, four axis-shifted antialiased copies grow plus-shaped corners on every diagonal edge ("not continuous … jagged" at 300 %). The product's reading of the same op is the title's own glyph path stroked 2 Windows px wide (one each side of the edge, the copies' reach) with round joins and caps, the title filled over it — within a pixel of the four copies at 100 %, smooth at every scale. Owner: paint_handler.cpp's caption title block (`paint_caption_row`), `text_shape::append_shaped_run_path`.
- **Clearlooks' trim-lane scroll-bar body is the product's own light slider** (2026-10-07; the trim lane's alone — the popup lists wear squeeze's gummy bar, below: "Clearlooks is anonymous enough that we can get away with our own scroll bar"): squeeze's gummy slider is spot[1]'s blue ramp with a ring, a border and three grips; the trim lane's body under clearlooks is the window ground between one Windows px of the gummy separator's light tone along its top and one of its dark tone along its bottom — no grips, no ring, no end columns. GTK's trough stays; THE CAPS FOLLOW THE BAR (2026-10-07 ~16:40, on the glass: the gummy stepper's dark border "sits against the light of the scroll body" and the caps "look tacked on"): each cap's ring is the bar's own two tones — the one ramp from the light line at its top to the dark tone, "to imitate the reflection of light", ENDING EARLY (2026-10-07 evening, the TC2 sheet's step j: a whole-height ramp made the straight outer column read slanted) at 15 % of the box, inside the rounded outer corners' arc, the dark tone flat below it to the bottom row — its face the ground (bg[ACTIVE], flat, pressed) and one Windows px of the dark tone where it meets the body, so it still reads as a button; the engine's chevron kept. Owner: `paint_cl_slider` and `paint_cl_stepper` (clearlooks_paint.h).
- **Clearlooks' overflowing popup list scrolls with a scroll bar, not GtkComboBox's arrows** (2026-10-08, on squeeze's Country combo: "I'd much rather have the scroll bar inside, and we can use the canonical scroll bar"): GTK 2 scrolls an overflowing combo popup with a scroll arrow at its top and its bottom; here the three list popups wear a vertical scroll bar inside the list's frame at its right, under both chromes — Windows 2000's own list scroll bar under win2000, squeeze's own gummy GtkScrollbar under clearlooks (the classic trough, the gummy steppers, the blue spot[1] slider with its three grips; 2026-10-08 ~12:50, "the official Clearlooks scroll bar … the one from the trim bar doesn't work here"). Owner: `paint_cl_scroll_trough_v`, `paint_cl_scrollbar_stepper`, `paint_cl_scrollbar_slider` (clearlooks_paint.h), render.h's popup scroll block.

## The program's own elements and behaviour

- **The trim lane is a scroll bar whose thumb is the kept region** (2026-10-02/03): Windows' thumb shows the viewport and its arrow buttons stand at the bar's ends; here the thumb spans the kept region between the trim bounds, and the two 16-px arrow buttons ride the thumb's own ends as the bounds' handles (the left arrow is the begin, the right the end). Owner: the trim lane block at `trim_lane_h_px` and `render_trim_flags` (render.h / render.cpp).
- **The flags are not Windows at all** (2026-10-03; the kinds 2026-10-04): the marker flags are the Acid flag — the program's own flat box in its kind's colour (warp, phase reset, and the history view's added and removed; an invalid flag wears the removed pair, one red for both, the context telling them apart — 2026-10-04, again 2026-10-07) under the one flag label, a one-px outline in the palette's own `flag_outline` role (the fifteenth since 2026-10-08, its compiled default the canvas's black; from 2026-10-07 to 2026-10-08 the outline was the canvas by rule), never the scheme's DkShadow (2026-10-07: "otherwise it will poke out when going into the canvas"), selection a brighter face of its pair's own colour under the same one label (Windows' selected-icon reversal, a face that changes rather than a frame that appears; the outline stays) — and the in-place label edit departs from Windows' F2 rename too (2026-10-07, under every chrome): opening the editor recolours nothing, the flag keeping its selected face, its outline and its stem, its text and caret the one flag label, the selected substring the chrome's selected pair (render.h's EDITING paragraph) — standing on the well's border with one Windows px of air above for the ruler's minor ticks, its stem crossing the well's top lines between the outline carried down both its sides, so the stem never touches the well (2026-10-05). Owner: render.h's marker-lane paragraph, the marker-lane block at `kMarkerLaneAirPx` (render.h).
- **Cards, not message boxes** (the Windows look 2026-10-02): what Windows would say in a modal message box the app says on a notification card — the cards stacking down from the toolbar's band at the top right, each in Windows' tooltip frame, timed and closed by a press — so a message never stops the work. Owner: `src/gui/notifications.h`'s head, `paint_notifications` (paint_handler.cpp).
- **A refused entry is a selected text and a card** (2026-10-03): Windows answers a refused entry with a message box or a balloon; here a refused Enter selects the field's whole text, the first keystroke replacing it, and the reason is a notification card — no frame, colour or glyph changes on the field (the red frame retired the same day: "a red outline and the card is redundant"). Owner: `text_editor::refuse` (text_editor.h), `render_flag_editor_box` (render.cpp), the dialog field in `paint_modal_dialog` (paint_handler.cpp).
- **Row 8 is one time field and a line of words, not a status bar of parts** (2026-10-03; the field 2026-10-05): the clock alone sits in a one-line sunken time field in the body face, the period's (ACID Pro 3.0, Vegas Audio) — the dialog field's 23 Windows px tall with its 5-W pad, where the period's 17 sat round MS Sans Serif's 9-row digits (2026-10-08, "it doesn't work as the other fields do"; its colors stay the time field's own pair on the chrome), standing 5 W in from the window's left edge with the state line's first ink 5 W past it; the state / history line is painted on the bare chrome at the normal face, the Vegas / ACID pattern (words on the chrome, a time in a sunken cell). Owner: `paint_bottom_row_buttons_and_clock` (paint_handler.cpp).
- **Chrome buttons act at the lift, hotkeys at the press** (2026-08-13): Windows' buttons act at the release too, but its menus and toolbars differ in places; here the one timing doctrine covers every roster button, with the anchors' toggle, dismissals and the keyboard at the press. Owner: the doctrine block at `GuiInputHandler::on_key` (input_handler.h), `AppState::ChromePress`.
- **The tooltip waits for a still pointer, within a slop** (2026-09-29; recorded 2026-10-06): Windows 95's tooltip counts every mouse move, a mouse at rest not jittering; here the wait restarts, and a standing tooltip's life restarts, only on a motion of more than 3 Windows px from where the pointer was anchored on either axis — Android's own hover slop (4dp), because a hovering S Pen jitters and would otherwise starve the wait. Not a departure: a hint gone down by its 5 s life or by a click on its button does not come back while the pointer stays on that button, however it moves, until the pointer leaves the button and arrives again — Windows' own rule (2026-10-06). Owner: `kTooltipHoverSlopPx` (render.h), `note_tooltip_hover` (input_pointer.cpp).
- **A tooltip can carry a second line** (2026-07-31; the two-line form needs comctl32 4.70, after Windows 95): on a button whose press admits a modifier the tooltip adds a line naming the modified act, so the twin a Shift- or Ctrl-click reaches is discoverable without a menu. Owner: `redesign_button_tooltip` (app_state.h), `paint_shift_tooltip` (paint_handler.cpp).
- **No hover faces but the toolbars' hot case** (2026-10-02, the frozen design; 2026-10-06): the dialogs, the lists, the menus and the caption paint no hover state; the one hover face is the toolbars' hot case, Explorer's own flat toolbar (not a departure). Owner: render.h's palette head, `paint_toolbar_box` (paint_handler.cpp).
- **A popup list's scroll arrow steps once per press, and its wheel scrolls three rows under both chromes** (2026-10-08): Windows repeats a held scroll arrow; here one press scrolls one row and the arrow stays pressed until the lift, the product's simplification. The wheel takes Windows' three lines a notch under Clearlooks too (GTK scrolls a page's tenth), for symmetry. Owner: render.h's popup scroll block.
- **The color picker is the program's own** (2026-10-07): Windows' ChooseColor dialog, with its swatch grid, its custom-color wells, its hue-and-saturation field and its luminance bar, was not the model; the program's colors are picked on one slim card without a caption, GNOME 2's own GtkHSV wheel (the hue ring with the saturation-and-value triangle inside it) under both chromes, beside the live chrome's own combo boxes, sliders, sunken hex field and push buttons, with OLD and NEW swatches, Copy and Paste between elements and a menu of named palettes (`src/gui/color_picker.h`'s head).

## The CDE vocabulary's own departures (2026-10-08)

The third vocabulary, `cde` (`src/gui/chrome_spec.h`'s `kChromeSpecCde`, the painters `src/gui/cde_paint.h`), is Solaris 9's CDE 1.5 — dtwm and Motif as the guidebookgallery captures draw them (`tmp/research/cde_solaris/report.md`), under the settled rule for every later vocabulary: period authentic except for the proportional fit to Windows' layout. These lines record where it departs from CDE itself, the architect's rulings of 2026-10-08 first.

- **The dtwm frame stands outside the app, a sizing frame only while restored** (architect 2026-10-08 ~17:45, reversing that afternoon's ruling that had hidden it on the tablet: "the dtwm frame as in the original, on the tablet too"): dtwm's 5-px frame — 2 light + 2 face + 1 dark, mirrored, in the active title's colors, its corner pieces' grooves 24 px (the frame plus the title bar) from each outer corner — stands round every window, the tablet's and the maximized laptop's included, at 5 W with the corners at 5 + 17 = 22 W; as the restored laptop's sizing frame always did, it lies outside the app's geometry (the platforms' `frame_px_`), so the lanes are inset by it, the well narrows by its two sides and keeps its 954 rows. It resizes the window only while restored — on a maximized window it is inert, its press a consumed nothing and its hover the arrow — and the corners' hit reach stays the product's caption height rather than the grooves. Owner: `paint_cde_window_frame` (cde_paint.h), `window_frame_px` (render.h), `claim_window_frame_press` (input_pointer.cpp).
- **The focus ring is not drawn** (architect 2026-10-08 ~15:00, the same words): CDE paints a 1-W ring in the active-title color round the focused text widget; here no field, list or button wears it — the flag editor's no-box rule, the caret, the dialog's DkShadow focus frame (the body's bottom shadow under the Solaris scheme) and the window menu's greyed verbs are the cues. Owner: `paint_cde_field` (cde_paint.h).
- **The bevels are one W everywhere** (architect 2026-10-08 ~14:10, sheet 01): CDE's authentic weight — only dtcalc used Motif's canonical 2 — mitred at the two-tone corners like every ring of the product (Motif's XmeDrawShadows gives the whole top row and left column to the top shadow, a one-px staircase). Owner: cde_paint.h's head, `paint_relief_frame`.
- **No Close button; Close is the window menu's** (architect 2026-10-08 ~14:10, ruling 3): dtwm's title bar has the window-menu button at the left and Minimize and Maximize at the right; here the caption's Close slot is that window-menu button, which drops the product's window verbs — Restore, Minimize, Maximize, a line, Close — in a Motif pulldown (dtwm's Move, Size, Lower and Occupy Workspace are the window manager's and absent); the menu opens at the press like the menu row's. Owner: `DropdownMenu::Window` and `kWindowPopupItems` (app_state.h), `claim_caption_press` (input_pointer.cpp).
- **Go Sans stands in for Lucida Sans** (architect 2026-10-08 ~14:10, ruling 4, "excellent choice"): Solaris's interface font is B&H Lucida Sans, a proprietary 14-px bitmap strike (cap 10 in a 15-row cell); the product sets the chrome in Go Regular, Bigelow & Holmes' own open face, at the base's 8-row cap in the 13-row cell, outlines only. The title is the medium face, as dtwm's is; the bold file is bundled and unread. Owner: `kGuiFaceSetCde` (gui_font.h), `fonts/README.md`.
- **The heights are Motif's arithmetic at the base cell, the ruler lane the leftover** (architect 2026-10-08, ruling 2; the leftover ~17:45): the title bar 17 (dtwm's 19 at cap 10), the menu bar 27 (Motif's 29: the RowColumn's margin 3, the cascade's one-px shadow and its margin 2 round the 13-row cell, not the 23 a pure 0.8 scaling gives), the band the base's 30-W case in 2 + 30 + 2 with dtfile's etched separator under it, 36, the scroll bar 13; with the frame's 5 + 5 these leave the ruler lane 4 of the base's 162 W, so the well keeps the base's 954 rows at 300 %. Owner: `kChromeSpecCde`, `chrome_stack_authored_h` (render.h), main.cpp's lane table.
- **The ruler stands behind the flags** (architect 2026-10-08 ~17:30, his glass pass: "the ticks reduced and the timestamp pushed down; the flags remain as they are, but the ticks and the timestamp hide behind the flags (they are not really that useful)"): the base's ruler is a 17-W lane of labels over a comb; under cde the lane is the 4-W leftover carrying 3-W ticks hanging from its top and the playhead's head, and the labels stand at the marker lane's rows, centred on the flags' labels, drawn before the flags so a flag covers whatever label it stands on. Owner: render.h's ruler block, `paint_ruler_row`.
- **No hover face at all** (2026-10-08): the product's rule allows one hover face, the flat toolbars' hot case; Motif has no hover and the cde toolbars take none — a press sinks the case, a checked case wears the select color inside its sunken line (XmNselectColor), nothing lights under the pointer. Owner: `GuiToolbarStyle::MotifFlat` (chrome_spec.h), `paint_cde_tool_button`.
- **Motif's insensitive stipple at one device px a cell** (architect 2026-10-08 ~17:45, "stippling for the font and the sliders and anything else authentic … a combination of period-authentic (no color blend) and modern (no pixelation)"): CDE drops every other pixel of an insensitive label's ink through its 50 % stipple bitmap (dtcalc's greyed keys); under cde every disabled word, every disabled icon and every disabled glyph the product draws itself (the caption's Maximize on the tablet, the menu's check mark, the combo's wedge) goes through that checker at the panel's own pixel, phase at the element's top-left — Windows' checker rule, never a blended grey; the emboss and the saturate stay the other two chromes'. No insensitive scale or arrow exists in the product to stipple. Owner: `paint_stipple_begin` / `paint_stipple_end` (render.h), `svg_icon::stippled_copy`, `show_embossed_run`.
- **The scroll bars are CDE's own 13, smaller than the base's 16** (architect 2026-10-08 ~17:45: "the canonical scroll bar seems smaller than the one we have; we reused Windows' metrics; make them historically accurate", with the rule that a vocabulary's element may be smaller than the base's where its source is, never larger without his ruling — chrome_spec.h's head): the trim lane and the popup lists' bars are Motif's XmScrollBar — the sunken select-color trough, the raised slider inside its ring, the beveled triangles, 11 inside the 13 as the notepad capture's — the 3 W saved the ruler lane's; the trim lane's caps ride the kept region's ends (the program's own trim bar, above) with one W of trough between an arrow and the slider as the capture has it. Owner: the spec's `scroll_bar_px`, `paint_cde_trough`, `paint_cde_slider`, `paint_cde_arrow` (cde_paint.h), `render_trim_flags`.
- **The cards, the tooltip and the menus are Motif panels** (2026-10-08): CDE has no tooltip element; a card, the tip and a pulldown are the body under one raised line, black text, no flat frame line (the card trio under the Solaris scheme is the body, black and the body's bottom shadow). The dropdown's lit item is Motif's armed row — a raised box in the body, its label unchanged — where Windows' is the selected pair; the lists (the folder overlay, the combos) invert as XmList does. Owner: `paint_popup_chrome`, `paint_dropdown` (paint_handler.cpp).
- **The well's frame is a text widget's one-W shadow** (2026-10-08): dtpad's text area sits in a 1-px shadow (dtfile's icon pane in a 2-px one); the well takes the one line — the body's bottom shadow on its top row, its top shadow on the bottom row — and the canvas inside, Clearlooks' shape in Motif's tones. Owner: `render_canvas` (render.cpp).
- **The scrub is XmScale's slider and trough** (2026-10-08): the sunken select-color trough over the thumb's rows and a raised one-W slider at Windows' 11-W thumb width, where the Audio tool's own position slider is a black custom gauge; the picker's six sliders the same. Owner: `paint_cde_scale_trough`, `paint_cde_scale_slider` (cde_paint.h).
- **No caption icon** (2026-10-08): dtwm draws no icon in its title bar; the cde caption shows none (the window-menu button stands where Windows' icon does), the Tango AppIcon staying the launcher's. Owner: `paint_caption_row`'s cde arm (paint_handler.cpp).
