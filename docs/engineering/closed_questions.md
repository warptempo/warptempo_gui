# Closed questions

One line per question the architect has closed: what was asked or tried, the ruling, its date, and the owner in code whose comment holds the rule (a symbol, a file). A future session does not re-propose anything here. History and the reasoning live at the owner's comment and in git.

## Authored data model

- **A render-display position pair and the `.renderwarpmarkers` / `.renderphaseresetmarkers` sidecars** — retired, 2026-07-11: positions on a render's target axis are computed live through the snapshot map and never serialized. Owner: the header of `src/parser/frame_format.h`.
- **A seconds or millisecond-grid position anywhere in the data path** — ruled out, 2026-07 (the whole-frame domain): authored positions are whole int64 source frames and MM:SS.mmm is display-only. Owner: `snap_authored_frame` (app_state.h), `src/parser/frame_format.h`.
- **The trim end's exclusive `total` wall** — unified away, 2026-07-18: every authored position shares the one inclusive `[0, total-1]` domain and an end stored at `total` is load-fatal, no migration. Owner: `first_past_eof_wall_defect` (marker_store_validate.h).
- **Making the trim crop's end inclusive (rendering to `map(e + 1)`)** — accepted as is, 2026-09-02: the exclusive end is one more sample of the crop's own rounding class and is what makes the full-window translation load-bearing. Owner: `plan_trim`'s crop (src/prepost/trimmer.cpp).
- **A tempo wheel or any second tempo-step surface** — none exists, fossil removed 2026-08-12: bare Up/Down is the whole tempo step. Owner: the bracket block of `src/parser/value_format.h`, `GuiWarpMarkersOps::adjust_tempo_cents`.
- **A double crossing of tempo outside `tempo_from_cents`** — ruled out, re-grepped 2026-08-29: tempo is integer cents at rest and the fingerprint and the BPM cell stay in cents. Owner: `tempo_from_cents` (value_format.h).
- **A deviation chain on a pass or a label reference** — ruled out, 2026-09-19 ("a pass is simply a pass"): `pass+0.01` and `a.aa+0.01` are refused as malformed bases. Owner: `WarpMarker::tempo_deviation_cents` (warpmarkers_parse.h).
- **`-0.00` as a deviation term** — refused, 2026-09-18: zero has one spelling, `+0.00`, which stays on the chain as history. Owner: `parse_deviation_cents` (value_format.h).
- **A deviation chain reaching the render or the fingerprint** — ruled out, 2026-09-18: the chain is spelling, `tempo_cents` is the resolved total and `MarkerForRender` has no such field. Owner: `warp_tempo_run` (warpmarkers.h).
- **Flatten acting on the selection** — ruled out, 2026-09-19 ("universal always"): both kinds walk the whole warp store and read no selection. Owner: `tempo_flatten_actionable` (warpmarkers_ops.cpp), `TempoFlattenKind` (app_state.h).
- **Flatten as an Edit-menu row or a bottom-row verb** — ruled out, 2026-09-19: its one pointer home is the icon row's iteration group after Grid Iterations, Flatten Deviations its shift twin. Owner: `RedesignButton::IconFlatten` (app_state.h).

- **The BPM opener forcing grid iterations off** — ruled out, 2026-09-10 ("we should card the exit"): the opener is refused under a lit lamp and bare `i` is the mode's one exit. Owner: `GuiFlagEditor::wipe_iter_state` (flag_editor.h), `enter_bpm_mode`.
- **The load in place as an iteration-mode exit or a bracket reset** — ruled out, 2026-09-02 ("iteration mode is where you stand"), and vacuous under the iteration lock since 2026-09-10. Owner: `apply_recipe_in_place` (input_handler.h, flag_editor.cpp).
