## THE BUG AND THE RULING
On the tablet the S Pen sends HOVER_EXIT a few ms before every tip-down, and a lift precedes the next hover sample by a few ms. Each is handled as a pointer leave that clears the roster's hover bits, and the Android loop paints after every input drain, so the frame in that gap reaches the glass. On the icon row this is invisible: a roster tool button's hover outline is a Breeze SnapIn fade (render.h's fade block at `kHoverFadeMs` / `HoverFadeKind`, `redesign_button_hover_fade_kind` in app_state.h, the painter's level reader `roster_button_hover_steps`-or-equivalent at paint_handler.cpp ~209–216, the tick `tick_hover_fades` in input_pointer.cpp), full while hovered and fading out over 100 ms in 10 steps when the bit drops, a return mid-fade full again at once — so a few-ms gap paints at step 9 or 10. The menu row's three anchors (File / Edit / Settings) have NO fade: `redesign_button_hover_fade_kind` returns nullopt for a menu anchor, and the pill in `paint_menu_row` (paint_handler.cpp) is a boolean, `enabled && (hovered || the open-menu anchor)`, so the gap frame is the anchor fully at rest — a visible blink at the first touch and at the lift (his words: "as the pen touches, for an instant it changes colours and it blinks").

THE ARCHITECT'S RULING (2026-10-01, "do what the icons do"): THE ANCHORS TAKE THE ROSTER'S SNAPIN FADE. The pill is full while the anchor is hovered or its menu is open; when the hover bit drops (and no menu holds it) the pill fades out over the existing `kHoverFadeMs` in `kHoverFadeSteps`, exactly the icons' tail; a return mid-fade is full at once. No new constant, no delay logic in the input. The one visible change, on both machines: on a true pointer-out the pill fades out over 100 ms instead of snapping off — a deliberate departure from Breeze's QMenuBar (which registers no engine), ruled because every other button in the app fades and the pen's gaps fall inside the tail. The fade is PAINT, NEVER A CLAIM (the rule at the fade block stands): hit tests, the press, the cursor and the tooltip keep reading the hover bits.
