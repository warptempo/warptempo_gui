## What and why
The architect ruled (2026-10-02 13:55) that the roster buttons within a group TOUCH, as in Windows 95's WordPad toolbar: the 2-logical-px gap between the 32-logical-px button boxes goes to 0; the etched separators keep their own gap between groups. Before the app changes, he judges MOCK-UPS on the tablet: the frozen theme (thin relief) and the thick-relief theme, each with the button gap removed. The renderer today paints every button at the scene's MEASURED x (`SCENE['buttons'][i]['x']`, 64 device px wide at a pitch of 68, i.e. a 4-device-px = 2-logical-px gap) and every separator at its measured x. Add one theme option that re-packs the buttons.

## The option
`buttons.gap`: `null` (the default, OPT_DEFAULTS) = the scene's measured positions, as today, byte-identical output for every existing theme (verify: render themes/frozen.json and tmp/palette/set_w_thick/mock_W1_thick_buttons.json before and after your change into your scratch dir and `cmp` the PNGs — identical). An integer >= 0 = the gap between adjacent buttons of one group, in LOGICAL px (device px = gap × S, S = the scene's scale, 2 on the tablet); validate like `trim.cap_w` (a whole number, not bool, >= 0, else SystemExit with one line).

## The re-pack (mirror the app's walk, paint_handler.cpp ~1840–1870: left groups walk right from the left pad, the icon row's view group and all of the bottom row's groups sit flush at the right margin)
Per row (icon, bottom), derive the GROUPS from the scene: order the row's buttons by x; a separator (`SCENE['separators']`, same `row`) between two buttons closes a group. Then derive CHAINS: consecutive groups whose inter-group spacing is the standard one (last button right edge → separator x == separator x + w → next button x; measure it off the scene rather than hard-coding 8 — in scene 1002 both are 8 device px). A chain is RIGHT-ANCHORED when its last button's right edge is the row's right margin (C.W − the icon row's first button x, i.e. 2304 − 16 = 2288 in scene 1002; derive, do not hard-code), else LEFT-ANCHORED. In scene 1002 the icon row has two chains (Save … IconLoadInPlace from x 16, left; ViewSW ViewTW ViewTP ending at 2288, right) and the bottom row one (right). Re-pack each chain: left-anchored from its first button's measured x walking right, right-anchored from its last button's right edge walking left; within a group buttons at pitch w + gap·S; between groups the measured separator gaps are KEPT (gap, separator, gap) and the separator's x moves with the pack. Buttons and separators get new x; y, w, h unchanged. The glyph is centred in the new box (draw_app_glyph is called with `b['x']` directly at render.py ~452 — it must take the re-packed x; make the local x the one source). Nothing else moves (the clock, the label box, the menu are untouched). Apply the re-pack where the scene is read for buttons AND separators (draw_buttons, draw_separators) — one function computes the packed geometry once per theme (memoize on the Theme), both painters read it; with gap null it returns the scene's own numbers.

Check scene 1002a as well (`--scene 1002a`): it must re-pack without error.
