### R3. The card's dismissal moves from the press to the LIFT; a held card's clock pauses.
His words: "if a card is being held it should turn off the timer so it shouldn't disappear; holding turns off the timer, releasing re-enables", and "I agree on the dismissal" (the lift). A long press can only be known at the lift, so THE CARD BECOMES A CHROME-STYLE BUTTON: act at the lift, slide-away cancel (a lift outside the pressed card dismisses nothing), the hold ORed with shift at the lift exactly as a roster button. Press and release both consumed, whatever the button (the card stays opaque; a non-left press is still the consumed nothing). The 2026-08-13 ruling "every dismissal stays at the press" (closed_questions.md line 662, and `AppState::ChromePress`'s head in app_state.h, ~6888) gains the card as its ONE STATED EXCEPTION, with the reason: a card is an EVENT the user closes, not a popup the next press dismisses — a popup's dismissal is a side effect of a press aimed elsewhere, a card's is the press's own act, and an act lives at the lift.

The clock: from the press to the lift, the pressed card's expiry is suspended; its remaining span resumes at the lift (a plain lift inside dismisses it anyway; a slide-away lift re-arms it). A card already due at the press is NOT banked (the hover rule, same reason: a pause does not resurrect).
