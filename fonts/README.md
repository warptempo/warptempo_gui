# The product's face

The two Liberation Sans files are the product's face on the tablet (the rules at `src/gui/gui_font.h`, the one face owner): LIBERATION SANS, Regular and Bold — the Regular every text but the title bar, the lists' headings and the ruler's digits, which read the Bold (architect 2026-11-19, send Z: "regular is good"; the bold-everywhere of 2026-11-04 night superseded) — the only face since 2026-10-12 (architect: "making Liberation Sans and GNOME 2.28 the only options, hard-coded, and drop their config keys"; Tahoma and FreeSans, the `font` device key and the Settings Font row were removed that day). The APK carries them as its assets; the build that packs them and the program that reads them take the names from one list, `kGuiFontFiles` in `gui_font.h`. The program never asks the system for a font. The face is drawn as its antialiased outline at every scale, at each use's em from the one pixel table (`kGuiFaceSizes` in `gui_font.h`). The files' own metrics are read at the install, and any embedded bitmap strike would never be drawn.

## The size table

The sizes are one table, `kGuiFaceSizes` in `gui_font.h`, each em authored in W (one W = 2 device px on the tablet at gui_scale 200; the unit of 2026-11-05) and scaled with gui_scale, rounded at the element (the Arc 9 pt text, architect 2026-11-18 / 19, send Z): eight uses, each naming its file — Body 12 Regular (the lists, the cards, the pages' sentences and the value sub-page's label and entry, the word buttons, the NOISE number, the status bar), Bold 12 Bold (the title bar and the lists' group headings), Small 10 Bold (the ruler's digits), Program 12 Regular (the clocks, the tab's name, the tooltip's name line), Keys 12 Regular (the on-screen keyboard's caps; the default of the live size, the `osk_font_px` tuning key's), Hint 12 Regular (the tooltip's hint line), Label 12 Regular (the cues' labels and the lane's editors) and Caption 10 Regular (the progress page's count). The Legend use (the keys' printed legends) retired with the legends 2026-11-18, and the Large use (a question's 14.4 W Bold sentence in the xfwm dialog window) with the dialog window, send EE part 2, 2026-11-20. No tracking; the four math signs are set down onto the hyphen's axis and the pipe lifted onto the digits' band (`kGuiLiftedSigns`).

The vertical metrics are the files' own, read once per file at the install (`src/gui/gui_font_bundled.cpp`) in em and scaled at each use: FreeType's ascender, descender and line height (the file's hhea), the "H" ink the cap band is centered on (1409 of 2048 units), the "l" ink the modal box's text seat reads, the "x" ink the marker lane centers, the "l" stem and the OS/2 strikeout position the struck cue label wears, and the lifted marks' offsets; the ink a seat centers (the ruler's digits, the logo lane's items) is measured on the painted road itself (`gui_font_ink_band`). The face keeps its own widths. A retune swaps a file or a table row; only the comments that quote these numbers need working out again.

## Provenance and licence

**Liberation Sans 2.1.5** (`LiberationSans-Regular.ttf`, `LiberationSans-Bold.ttf`): the liberation-fonts 2.1.5 release (https://github.com/liberationfonts/liberation-fonts), as Arch Linux packages it in `ttf-liberation` 2.1.5-2 (`/usr/share/fonts/liberation/`), byte-identical. Their `name` table says "Liberation Sans" / "Regular" and "Bold", Version 2.1.5. Liberation Sans is metric-compatible with Arial, a stand-in for it, and the product names it by its own name; its kerning is why the architect keeps it ("my go-to font because of the kerning. It looks the best at 1080p … the most well-behaved of all the fonts", 2026-10-09). TrueType, it meets FreeType's light autohinter (`src/gui/gui_font_bundled.cpp`), keeping every glyph on the baseline row at the tablet's scales. It was the product's fallback face once before, 2026-10-05 to 2026-10-06, and again beside FreeSans from 2026-10-09 (git history).

The licence that travels with them is the package's `LICENSE`, the SIL Open Font License 1.1, verbatim as `LICENSE-Liberation-OFL.txt`: "Digitized data copyright (c) 2010 Google Corporation with Reserved Font Arimo, Tinos and Cousine. Copyright (c) 2012 Red Hat, Inc." The product ships the files unmodified under their own names, as the OFL's reserved-name clause wants, with no tracking (architect 2026-10-09: no custom font targeted at a few scales is ever shipped). The vertical metrics are the files' own (`src/gui/gui_font.h`, since 2026-10-23).
